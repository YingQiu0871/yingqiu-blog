> 2017 年 10 月 21 日 · 昆一国际 · 英文作文（原文为英文，仅修正拼写与语法）

Kunming NO.1 High School
Longxiang Wuhua, Kunming
Yunnan Province, P.R.C.
October 21, 2017

Beijing Luhuayuan
Jinchen Panlong, Kunming
Yunnan Province, P.R.C.

Dear Professor,

After the school made up the new phones-and-computers rule, I have thought a lot. In my view, the school made a completely wrong decision, so I am writing this letter to you to show you my opinion: I suggest students should always be allowed to receive their phones or computers.

As we all know, our school is teaching in English. For international students, understanding the teachers can't be done without a dictionary, but most of the electronic dictionaries such as Casio can't translate most of the professional terms — only some common words. Seriously, the dictionaries recommended by the school are totally useless. They can't bring any help to our students.

On the other hand, all of the students can use computers to write notes quickly and conveniently. For example, if the teacher is talking about a rose, we can create a new OneNote page, search the source, allusions, pictures and other information on the internet, and copy them down. Even after failing to keep up with the teachers' rhythm, we can also use the voice input option to input what the teacher said and think carefully after the class.

By using computers, we can have more options to write our notes: words + pictures + OCR + voice + all kinds of highlights + infinite related hyperlinks.

In summary, I think allowing students to receive their phones and computers is a necessary and helpful decision that our school should make. And I believe it will have a great effect.

Yours faithfully,
Yingqiu
