## BTC after the rebound

BTC fell through a full bear year in 2021 and 2022, then rebounded from early 2023. That was a rebound after a main downtrend, and a finished trend never reverses straight into a new one. What follows is a range, and only a mature range picks a direction.

The rebound has turned down, and I put the odds at 90% that it is over. BTC now ranges, probably until year end, with volatility shrinking. I expect one more leg down toward support; from there it either breaks the prior low or stabilizes and starts a new move. Many altcoins look ready to break with it, so keep stops on.

## Why the inside of a box has no edge

Trading inside a range, long or short, is asking to lose. Certainty appears only when the box is fully formed and squeezed to the point where it must choose. Entries belong there, not in the middle.

Three things make a good trade: an entry logic built on reading market cycles, a tight stop at a clean turning point, and a calm hold where the exit is decided by the logic, not by price wiggles.

## Cycles and the PERP short

I trade naked candles, no indicators. The market moves in phases driven by human nature: a range ends and launches a main uptrend, the uptrend turns into a high range, that breaks into a bigger range or a main downtrend, and a new range begins. The structure repeats on every timeframe.

The PERP short was a bet on the shift from uptrend to range. The coin had run two legs without a proper correction and was pressing the top of its daily box. I never short the top directly; I wait for a breakdown structure. Because the trade was counter-trend, I closed half early and the rest near the box's prior low.

## Key points

- After a main trend ends, consolidation is unavoidable; BTC will likely range into year end.
- Only trade a box when it is mature enough to choose a direction.
- A good trade has cycle-based logic, a small stop and a steady hold.
- Counter-trend trades: take half off early. With-trend trades: hold.
- Until consistently profitable, trade a few hundred dollars and always use a stop.
