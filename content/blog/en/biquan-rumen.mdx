> A digest of the Chinese primer, written in November 2026 from public sources (listed at the end of the Chinese version). Markets and rules change quickly. This is a study note, not investment advice.

The other notes in this column assume the reader knows what a perpetual contract or a funding rate is. This one covers the step before: what a complete beginner should understand before sending money to an exchange.

Three conclusions first. Nobody stands behind a crypto transfer, so a wrong address, a lost key or a failed exchange usually means the money is gone. The fastest ways for a beginner to lose are leverage, obscure small coins and following someone else's calls. And the rules where you live need checking before you start.

## What it is and what moves it

Bitcoin is a public ledger kept by thousands of computers. Transfers are batched into blocks roughly every ten minutes; miners compete to add them and are paid in new coins. That reward halves about every four years, most recently on 20 April 2024 to 3.125 BTC, and supply is capped at 21 million. Ethereum adds programs called smart contracts and moved from mining to staking on 15 September 2022.

Bitcoin has no cash flow, so price is whatever buyers and sellers agree on. Spot ETFs approved in the United States in 2024 widened access. Reported prices show the swings: a record near 126,000 dollars in October 2025, about half of that by February 2026, and roughly 86,000 a year after the peak.

## Keys, exchanges, stablecoins

- A wallet stores a private key; the coins stay on chain. The 12 or 24 recovery words are the key. Write them on paper, never type them into a website, never give them to anyone.
- A centralised exchange holds your coins for you. FTX collapsed in November 2022 after misusing customer funds, and Bybit lost about 1.5 billion dollars to hackers in February 2025.
- Stablecoins such as USDT and USDC hold reserves against each token. The algorithmic UST failed in May 2022. The United States, the European Union and Hong Kong have since legislated on stablecoins.

## Spot versus contracts

Spot means you own the coin and can wait. A leveraged contract multiplies gains and losses, and a move of about ten percent against a 10x position wipes out the margin. Perpetual contracts also carry a funding payment between longs and shorts, commonly every eight hours. On 10 October 2025 roughly 19 billion dollars of leveraged positions were liquidated in a day. Beginners should leave contracts alone.

## Scams and rules

Chainalysis put 2025 scam losses near 17 billion dollars. The common shapes are romance-investment schemes, fake support staff, phishing links that ask for a wallet approval, signal groups, and guaranteed high yields.

Mainland China treats crypto-related business as illegal financial activity under notices of September 2021 and February 2026. Hong Kong licenses platforms and stablecoin issuers. The EU's MiCA regime has applied in full since 30 December 2024. Tax treatment varies by country.

## Key points

- Decide an amount you can lose entirely, and never borrow it.
- Practise creating, backing up and restoring a wallet with a tiny sum.
- Buy spot in major coins on large, permitted platforms.
- No leverage, no tipped small coins, no signal groups.
- Record every trade with its reason, and move part of any profit back to a bank account.
