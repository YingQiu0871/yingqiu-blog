## Why altcoins could move at all

Before trading or reviewing any altcoin, ask what BTC was doing at the time. On the large seasonal cycle BTC is still in winter, but every large cycle has smaller cycles inside it. After a month without a real altcoin rally, the small pivot where BTC's decline ended was enough for money to try altcoins. CEL and TRB both started after 5 May, once BTC had confirmed its bounce. Rallies born from a small pivot are smaller and less durable.

## CEL, step by step

Mark the structure first. CEL's box top was 0.4; a break there would open a bounce against the long downtrend. Resistance above: the supply peak near 0.6, then 0.87, then the round number at 1.0.

Four trade locations followed. Chase the breakout after the false break at 0.4 was reclaimed (I entered at 0.43 but my size was too big to hold). Buy the small-divergence bottom near 0.46 with the stop under the low; never trade breakouts inside a small divergence. Reduce when an accelerating leg hits resistance. After the large divergence, wait for the second wave and clear out if it fails.

The large divergence led into a range with its floor at 0.66; the break from that floor was a fresh main uptrend, exited at 0.87 and near 1.0. At 1.0 the bounce had reached its ceiling; I closed my own long there without looking higher.

## TRB and the missing trades

TRB is a large range with messy supply, so there is no main uptrend to lean on. Trade it as resistance-to-resistance breakouts only, never buy dips. Five waves ended near 115, then an acceleration leg marked the top, hard to short but a top. The one certain long was the bounce inside the range that followed.

I missed these because my trading has been sloppy, my size does not fit these books (about 30% lost to slippage in CEL), and I was away from the screen. Both coins came from a small BTC pivot, so the likely sequence now is range, then slow decline.

## Key points

- Read the BTC cycle before trading an altcoin; small pivots give small, short-lived rallies.
- Mark the box top and the resistance levels before entering.
- In a main uptrend, buy small-divergence bottoms and never break out from them.
- After a large divergence, wait for the second wave; exit when it fails.
- Range-structured coins are breakout-only; do not buy dips in them.
- If a coin cannot absorb your size, leave it alone.
