A digest of the Chinese chapter.

## Four filings

Everything a US-listed company files is free on the SEC's EDGAR database. The 10-K is the audited annual report, due 60 days after year-end for the largest companies. The 10-Q covers each of the first three quarters. An 8-K reports important events, generally within four business days, and carries the earnings press release. The proxy statement covers directors, executive pay and ownership. The SEC sets what must be disclosed but does not vouch for its accuracy.

## Where to start in a 10-K

The order of items is fixed. Read Item 1 (Business), Item 1A (Risk Factors, compared with last year's) and Item 7 (management's discussion) first, then the statements and the auditor's opinion in Item 8. A change of auditor in Item 9 is a warning sign. Item 5 shows what was actually bought back.

## Expectations and guidance

On August 28, 2024 Nvidia reported $30.04 billion of revenue against a $28.7 billion estimate and guided above consensus; CNBC reported the shares down 8% after hours. The price already held a bigger beat. Guidance often matters more than results: Meta's first-quarter 2022 outlook erased about $230 billion of market value in a day, and Walmart cut its profit outlook between reports on July 25, 2022. Meta's shares were then up 178% for 2023 by mid-December.

## Key points

- Filings are free on EDGAR; read the 10-K, not the glossy report.
- Start with Items 1, 1A and 7.
- Earnings day measures results against expectations, not against zero.
- Guidance can outweigh the quarter itself.
- A one-day reaction does not settle who is right years later.
