## Sizing the correction

Last week's trades were awkward: news noise, moves in the small hours, and entries that all required chasing. Still, there was a market, and losing money in it is on you.

I had called 46000 to 48000 a swing resistance and warned against longs. BTC spiked to 48000, held up on news for one more high, then turned. The correction now under way corresponds to the full two-month rally from 28000 to 49000. It is a large one because two things coincide: a major daily-timeframe resistance and a completed five-wave structure at the top of several legs. That means the bullish momentum of this leg is over. A strong market ranges here, a weak one drops lower, but a direct turn to new highs is very unlikely. Altcoins already show the momentum broken and money leaving.

That shift is why I keep holding the short. Once momentum has flipped, time works against the longs, which is the one condition that lets a counter-trend position be held.

## Two scripts after the range

Do not trade the correction itself; whether it stops at 40000 or 33000 is irrelevant. Watch what happens when the range resolves. Up means one more leg of the big rebound from the bottom of the range to the top, with logic, space and trend behind it. Down means price returns inside a box, where there is no entry logic because stops and targets are unclear. A downturn also ends the whole rebound and brings a long ranging period, like the months after the 2020 crash, when dip buyers kept the market from falling straight through.

ETH has already had its move, like a loot box that paid out below expectations. It stays stronger than BTC and will lead next time, but with capital gone I will not long it now.

Treat these two months as a complete cycle, from launch through small and high divergence to the top turn, and study where each phase sat, which coins led, and why it started and ended where it did. Every market repeats the pattern.

## Key points

- Correction scale is set by a high-timeframe resistance plus a position at the top of a multi-leg rise.
- A large correction ends the previous uptrend; a short held through it has time on its side.
- Stay out of the correction and judge only the direction out of the range.
- Up continues the big rebound; down returns to a box with no entry logic.
- Money-making difficulty comes in phases: review the phase you missed and wait, rather than fight the hard one.
