## Where the logic lives in a range

BTC has been sitting in a high-level range. Inside a range there are only two places with a real setup: the first bounce after price falls from the top of the box, and the breakout once the consolidation has matured.

The one decent trade was a short after the second test of the range bottom. Three things had to be clear first: the downside targets (supports near 59000 and 57000, each able to hold), the rule not to get greedy, and the fact that this was a counter-trend short inside a larger uptrend.

## Do not chase the break

When the bottom near 60000 gave way and price hit 59600, many people shorted right there. Right direction, wrong technique. A straight-line fall with no pullback is dangerous to chase.

Wait instead. After the break, price bounces. When the bounce stalls and turns down, enter at that pivot with the stop just above it, around 60300. The first small hook does not count; the real bounce comes after another leg down. Take half off at the first support and close the rest if price climbs back toward the pivot.

## The false-breakout long

The second trade was a long on the false breakdown. The logic was weaker and I skipped it; after a poor run I now take only the cleanest setups. It was still valid because two things lined up: a range false-breakout structure and a larger uptrend behind it. Confirmation came when price retested the high, then held a dip. Target was the supply zone at 62500 to 62900, half there and the rest at the top. In a choppy market, take profit quickly.

## Altcoins have no liquidity

With 190k USD at 5x, one order in CEL moved price three percent each way, 30% of capital lost to slippage round trip. Only BTC and ETH can take size now. W failing to rally despite a good structure means money has left. Rest.

## Key points

- Inside a range, only the first bounce from the top and the matured breakout carry logic.
- A downside break inside an uptrend is a counter-trend short: small target, no greed.
- Never chase a straight-line break; enter at the pivot of the bounce, stop above it.
- A false breakout is only a long when a larger uptrend backs it.
- When your size costs 30% in slippage, stick to BTC and ETH or stay out.
