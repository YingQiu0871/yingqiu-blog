## From summer to winter

BTC's final push to a new high was the hottest moment of the cycle; from there the market slid into autumn, and the sell-off that followed opened winter. After a full flush the market is at its coldest, which is why I rested for two days right after it. A cycle runs from one winter through spring, summer and autumn to the next winter.

This winter differs from the last. BTC topped at a new high and drew in fresh money, so some heat remains: PEPE and the animal coins made new highs, FLOKI moved, then AI and WLD woke up. That residual heat can carry one or two coins, and that observation is the only reason I took WLD today. A breakout alone is never a reason to buy; environment and risk appetite come first, which is why OP's clean breakout failed.

## Seven logical short-term trades on PEPE

Assuming a safe environment, the PEPE five-minute chart offered these trades, each for one leg only: the breakout of the daily box at the start; longs near the bottom of the first small disagreement, accepting a few stops before the fourth attempt works; the second wave as the uptrend resumes; a cautious third-wave attempt; standing aside once a large disagreement is due; the right-side entry when that consolidation breaks to a new high; and the top after the second large disagreement, where I began probing shorts. Never buy the left side of a large disagreement, and after two of them stop trying for a third wave.

## My own review

I exited early because I expected a large disagreement, animal coins pull back hard, and BTC near 60000 looked due for a deep correction. Then I lost the core position in short-term trades and never bought back, once because the move was too fast, once because I had relaxed. I caught one short but missed the bottom after it. One out of ten. I had called five times from the bottom and it did 5.6. Without a correction of ten days to a month, it will not run cleanly again.

## Key points

- The hottest leg marks summer; the full flush that follows is winter, when you rest.
- A winter after a BTC new high keeps enough heat for one or two coins.
- Breakouts need a supportive environment; in winter, skip them.
- Trade a leader in seven trend-following pieces and take one leg at a time.
- Enter large disagreements on the right side, after the breakout.
- Spotting the leader and the environment matters more than any detail.
