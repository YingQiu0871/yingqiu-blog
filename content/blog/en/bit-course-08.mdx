No trade review this week; my own trades were messy. Instead, the market and two leaders that show where a run can be traded.

## BTC, ETH and the alts

BTC is in an upward rebound inside a large box. This launch should reach about 42,000; beyond that the market becomes hard to trade. I sell wherever it stops above 42,000, and if it breaks my cost the move is over. The template is 2019. ETH, once it starts, has no reason not to run; 3,000 is certain, the entry is the problem, and I defend 2,050.

Last week alts had no independence: they rose and fell with BTC. A breakout that drags with no volume, like ROSE, deserves caution. USTC was the exception because its volume and crowd let it ignore BTC. For next week: SOL and LINK are coiling with room above, BIGTIME leads but is already high, ORDI needs more consolidation, TIA can only be scalped.

## USTC: how one leader run should be traded

The first real entry was the breakout attempt at 20, where volume changed and space opened; below that there was no logic. After the acceleration a large divergence had to follow: no longs, no shorts, no range trading until a box formed. Then long the second test of the lows with a stop under it, long again when it held higher, and hold the breakout until the move accelerates. The false breakout and the small platform rolling over marked the end. Four trades; I made twelve, most of them worthless. The box it sits in now is a pretty trap.

## BIGTIME: honor the only valid stop

I entered on the stabilization, left early because BTC looked weak, missed the re-entry, chased, and was stopped repeatedly because I kept moving stops. After a launch the only valid stop is the low of the small divergence; a breakout-level stop is meaningless. Five waves are done and a large divergence is due, not a super-large one, since room above remains. Enter when it stabilizes.

## Key points

- BTC targets 42,000 and the run ends if it breaks cost; ETH is a 3,000 story once it starts.
- When BTC churns at highs, alts follow it; trust a coin's own structure only when its volume and crowd are real.
- The first entry in a leader is the breakout attempt at the key level, nothing below it.
- After acceleration wait for a box, buy the second test, hold through the breakout.
- The only valid stop is under the small divergence.
- Trade leaders or untouched coins only; picking up everything means falling with everything.
