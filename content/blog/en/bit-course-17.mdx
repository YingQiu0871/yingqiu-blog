## Three conditions for a breakout long

The ICP long needed three things at once: space (a dense supply zone at 15.2 left room from 10 to 15), trend (a prior main uptrend made the long a with-trend trade), and a box (a large correction formed a range, and the entry was at the turning point where the range broke out). The stop belongs at the start of the rally, at 10 or at most 10.1, wherever you entered. Exit at the target or scale out when the move feels wrong.

What decides how long you hold is the wider market: where BTC stands, whether it may fall, whether money is still active. Altcoin rotation was already late, so I took profits early instead of holding for 15.2. Had BTC been launching from 28000 at the same time, I would have held for far more.

BLUR had the same structure and failed. Identical setups diverge when the market mood is hostile to alts; which one runs is probability, so you trade with a stop and modest expectations.

## Why counter-trend shorts hurt

BTC was a rebound hitting resistance near 45000 with a completed five-wave structure at a swing top. At such a point the first rule is never to chase longs. I shorted twice: the first I abandoned because a counter-trend position kept threatening new highs; the second I chased when the 44600 low of a small box broke. It fell as expected but too fast, and in a counter-trend short, acceleration means you cut size no matter what.

ORDI combined a five-wave top, a false breakout from a small platform and BTC's own correction. It bounced before it fell, and I scaled out. Even when right, a counter-trend trade is miserable to hold, which is why I neither post nor recommend shorts.

A breakout is a structure, not a price: the range finishes, the pullback holds and price turns up. Leaders resonate with BTC, have room on the daily chart, and are usually the current hot or newly listed coin. Indicators are useless; the core of trading is finding the same market and the same human behaviour in history.

## Key points

- A trend long needs space, trend and a box together; the stop sits at the rally's origin.
- Market mood decides holding time; late in altcoin rotation, take profit early.
- At a five-wave top inside a rebound, never chase longs.
- Counter-trend shorts get bought repeatedly; cut size the moment they accelerate.
- A breakout is the end of consolidation turning up, not a move past the old high.
- Most of the time the market ranges; sit it out and wait for the main uptrend.
