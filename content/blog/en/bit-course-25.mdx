## The rebound trade I hesitated on

After a strong uptrend, the end of a correction offers one logical long: the rebound. You do not buy every dip. Wait for a right-side signal, take the bounce, and leave, because what follows is a range you cannot read.

The best entry of the whole move came near 62000, where BTC held above the prior low and pushed up with force. I wanted it badly, but Powell was speaking at two in the morning and I had been losing, so I hesitated two minutes, it ran one percent, and I let it go. Once the bounce is done, every trade before the range breaks out is a trade without logic.

## Why I still hold a small base position

The real range is 68700 to 60800. One day it breaks out, and the upward break is where you size up to whatever you can bear, even half your capital.

I went in early anyway, with very light size and a wide stop. Do not copy that. My reason is the bull market mood: in past bull markets such corrections often skip the neat consolidation, tap a double bottom and run, because too many people want in. Still, the market is weak right now, so sideways-then-breakout is becoming more likely. My spot is small, with a stop at 60000.

## Mood over news, review over theory

My edge is trading trend according to market mood. I ignore news and fundamentals; a rising market is the good news, a falling one the bad news. Alts only start when BTC starts. Right now is plain winter: keep your hands still.

Improving comes down to reviewing your own trades and other people's. Since 26 February there have been four rounds of market, each with its own leaders, from GAS, ORDI, Link and SOL to Big Time, then catch-up names like ATOM and TIA. Only the first two legs are good, and leader rotations track BTC's own rhythm.

## Key points

- Trade the post-correction rebound only from the right side, then exit.
- The 62000 pivot was the best entry of the move; two minutes of hesitation cost it.
- Range 68700 to 60800; the upward breakout is where you commit heavily.
- Left-side entries are for light size and wide stops only.
- Mood drives trend; news is priced in. No counter-trend trades, no overtrading, no trades without logic.
