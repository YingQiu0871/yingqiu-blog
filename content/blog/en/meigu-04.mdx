## Accounts

Compare brokers on regulation, the full cost of moving and converting money, available order types and sessions, and paperwork. Commissions are no longer the difference. A cash account buys only with what you have; a margin account lends, charges interest and can sell you out.

## What each order promises

A market order guarantees a fill, not a price. A limit order guarantees a price, not a fill. A stop order becomes a market order once triggered. On May 6, 2010, between 2:40 and 3:00 p.m., more than 20,000 trades in over 300 securities printed at least 60% away from their prior prices, some at a penny, as market makers pulled back and left placeholder quotes. Those trades were cancelled; anything less than 60% away stood.

## After hours

Outside 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Eastern, liquidity is thin and the national best bid and offer does not apply. Earnings usually land then. Meta closed at $323 on February 2, 2022, reported after the bell and finished the next day at $237.76, down 26%. A stop at $300 would have filled far below it.

## Key points

- Use limit orders when the market is thin: at the open, on news, in small names.
- A stop price is a trigger, not a floor.
- Fractional shares cannot be transferred between brokers and may carry no vote.
- Currency conversion is often the largest hidden cost for non-dollar investors.
- Dividends to non-US residents are withheld at 30%, or 10% under the China treaty; settlement is T+1; the $25,000 pattern day trader minimum was replaced in 2026.
