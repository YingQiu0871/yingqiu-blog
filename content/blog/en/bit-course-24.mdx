## Why this correction stays shallow

In quiet weeks like the last one the job is to rest and protect capital; as long as BTC has not truly broken down the market will not run out of chances.

Historically every BTC top has corrected around 20 percent. This time I told everyone to stop shorting once the drop stalled. BTC kept wicking on the way up, and each wick is a mini correction that shakes out shorts piece by piece. The selling pressure never builds into one big move, so the downtrend has already stopped making lower lows.

## Think like it is a bull market

Treat the mood as a bull market. In a normal market a box like this would test the high, form a range and break out only after a full consolidation. With this much heat it can simply tap the lows and go, so lean more aggressive as long as BTC stays above the mid fifty to sixty thousand area.

I opened a small left-side base position where the decline looks exhausted, aiming for a retest that holds and a push toward the prior high; if it gets there the stop moves to breakeven. The two key spots for bulls are the double-bottom retest and the breakout, and the number to watch is the 72500 zone: every break, take a trade with split sizing, normal leverage and a stop.

## The two most certain opportunities

Right now I see only BTC and PEPE. PEPE led the whole way while BTC climbed, in resonance with BTC, and capital remembers such leaders, as it did with ORDI. Wait for the right side; the ideal is both consolidating for a couple of weeks and then launching together.

Technicals are a bonus, not the reason a coin moves. Money buys a coin because of the moment it appeared and its relationship to BTC. Rotations like YGG and Gala have no pattern and are mostly luck.

## Key points

- Repeated wicks on the way up release selling pressure early, so the correction need not reach 20 percent.
- Treat the mood as a bull market while BTC holds above the mid fifty to sixty thousand area.
- Key BTC spots: the double-bottom retest and the 72500 breakout zone, with split sizing and a stop.
- BTC and PEPE are the two high-conviction setups; wait for PEPE's right side.
- Timing and capital flow beat chart structure.
