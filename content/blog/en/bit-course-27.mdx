## Where BTC sits in its box

BTC tried to break its range at the second and third top and failed, so the box got complicated. The real upward break comes only after BTC revisits the 71000 area, corrects and tries again; the bearish version is a bounce that dies in that zone. Either direction is possible, so wait.

CORE's rally on 15 April shows how alts start: BTC stabilised, and capital moved into alts with a lag, picking the previous crowd favourite because no strong new coin existed. Its chart justified 1.7 to 2.1, yet it reached 2.8. Only market mood explains that.

## Three dimensions of the system

First, the market cycle. Money has seasons: spring is average, summer easiest, autumn and winter harder. In spring everything rallies with BTC and you cannot tell the leader. In summer the coin that rises with BTC again after a correction is the fiercest leader (ORDI, Big Time). Autumn brings choppy catch-up rallies; winter is dead. The season also sets your aggression: expand in spring and summer, cut size and leverage in autumn and winter.

Second, the coin's own cycle, with six logical entries: the launch from a big platform, a small pullback, the top at the major pullback, the second-wave launch, the short after the uptrend ends, and the bounce inside the range. Points 1 and 4 deserve full size, 2 and 6 come next, 3 and 5 are counter-trend and not recommended. Anything else is a sickness. Exits differ: hold a launch through the major pullback, but run a range bounce only until it turns.

Third, psychology, which is really position management: split positions and low leverage.

## My own forty thousand dollar lesson

My recent BTC short was a valid point 5; BTC now bounces inside a range supported at 60000 to 62000. Every trade after that was left-side scalping outside the system, a habit picked up after a few early entries made fifty to sixty thousand U. Those wins cost me over three hundred thousand U. With low leverage and patience, even those trades would have paid.

## Key points

- BTC must revisit 71000 before a real break; until then, wait.
- Alts start with a lag after BTC's stage change, and capital defaults to past favourites.
- When unsure of the season, trade summer only and hunt the leader.
- Six logical entries exist in a coin's life; 1 plus 4 is the best, 3 and 5 are counter-trend.
- Psychology depends on split positions and low leverage.
