## Coins without rules

ACE is not pushed by the market as a whole; someone is controlling it. Such a coin can rise 50 or 100 times and still be worse to trade than ORDI moving 20 or 30 percent. Market-driven coins have patterns you can grasp; a controlled coin has none. I have blacklisted it. My two shorts on it were also wrong: after a large-structure breakout, a small divergence should be bought at the bottom, not shorted.

## The trades that had logic

TRB was the best setup of the week and I missed it. The case for the 193 platform: altcoins were hot, price had cleared the historical high at 190 with nothing above, and the long consolidation counted as a big correction before wave two. Enter around 193 to 195 with a stop at 192, target the round number 250 sized from its usual wave, and leave a limit order at 248 or 249. It hit 250 exactly.

ICP was a tightly converging box with a clear turning point. It failed, but I would take that trade any number of times: a complete large structure, small-timeframe resonance, a hot altcoin, open space above and a good entry.

Two ETH longs were stopped out. Chasing 2350 without a pullback is allowed only with light size and a stop at 2300, where a full retrace would break the bullish rhythm. Several BTC trades and an EGLD chase were unnecessary.

## ORDI at the top

ORDI ran a textbook five-wave leader move. In the wave-four range you already know a final push is coming, so the longer it lasts the closer you watch; the start comes from a small platform below, where heavy size belongs. I chased at 58 to 59, which forced a smaller position. At such heights you take profit while it rises and never sit through the correction. When it dumped back fully, I exited.

## Key points

- Never trade controlled coins, however far they rise.
- After a large breakout, buy the small divergence at the bottom instead of chasing.
- Entry near the platform allows a small stop; chasing forces light size and a wide stop.
- With no resistance above, target a round number and place the order in advance.
- Corrections are survivable low in a move, not at the top; sell as it rises.
- BTC and ETH need rest and rotated altcoins are tired, so next week is for defence.
