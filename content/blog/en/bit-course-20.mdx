## The BTC second wave

BTC started from around 43000, repeating an earlier pattern: a strong first leg, a correction that converges cleanly, then a second wave that runs straight up. When the first leg has been that fluent, the market already has heat and consensus, so the second wave can be fluent even if the correction looks incomplete. Near the prior high I put the odds at 60% new high, 40% correction, so taking half off there is reasonable.

## Two entry points for altcoins

Altcoins were traded at two moments. On 8 February, as BTC started, I looked for coins resonating with it and found only TIA on the daily: a trend structure, a long 4-hour convergence, room to new highs. On 12 February, as BTC pushed through 49000, I prepared a list in advance and bought ORDI, a former leader with a good platform and a first target at 72, and then ZETA, the strongest coin on the gainers list with no overhead resistance. For ZETA the entry was the classic one: after a main uptrend breaks to new highs, buy near the low of the first sideways disagreement.

## Independent sector moves

LPT and WLD did not resonate with BTC; the AI sector ran on its own after the SORA news. For a strong independent move you only need BTC not to collapse, then trade the coin's own cycle. LPT at 14 had room to 18.9 or 20, with the entry after the first large disagreement ended. It was the highest-conviction trade of the AI run; I sized it too small. WLD never gave a proper entry, so missing it is normal.

Going forward, if ETH holds above 3050, watch AI coins, mainstream coins that have not rotated yet, and recently listed coins. The market is in its tail phase, so lower size, leverage and expectations.

## Key points

- A fluent first leg makes a fluent second wave likely once the correction converges.
- Take half off near the prior high; new high and correction are both live.
- Enter altcoins at BTC's start and at its breakout of a key level, picking strong daily structures.
- Missed the start of a main uptrend? Buy the first sideways disagreement after the new high.
- Independent sectors need only a stable BTC; trade their own cycle.
- Environment outranks every indicator; timing the entry matters more than its form.
