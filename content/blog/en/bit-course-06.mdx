This session is my own self-criticism. BTC whipsawed between 35,000 and 38,000 all week, and my review turned up too many tilted, logic-free entries.

## Two questions every trade must answer

Cycle and logic decide whether to go long and how far the move can run. For ORDI, a first large divergence inside a main uptrend with room above says long, target around 20. Only then comes the second question: where to enter on the small timeframe. Look for a turning point, such as a pullback that fails to make a new low, or wait for the range to break. Big picture bullish plus small timeframe just stabilized is resonance. Small timeframe still falling is counter-trend, and the two are not comparable in win rate or risk-reward.

## The common fault: entering too high

Across ORDI, SOL, MEME, ETH and TIA the logic was mostly right, but I bought breakouts at the top of structures instead of the stabilization point at the bottom. That means late entries, wide stops, bad risk-reward, and losses even on false breakouts that used to cost nothing. A breakout trade is about the structure breaking, not the price level. The only valid stop sits below the turning point; anything higher gets harvested repeatedly. If you cannot hold it, size down.

## Catching every leader and still losing

A BNT bottom-fish began with two counter-trend scalps that made 5,000 U. That profit was bait; I kept going and gave back over 20,000 U plus my composure.

SOL, LINK, ORDI, GAS, ARK, INJ, TIA and BNT were all identified. LINK bought at 8 hit the 15 target, but I dumped the base high. BNT was the cleanest call of the cycle and a top-up at the highs cost me the base, roughly 1.5 million. A 12-coin BTC base below 29,000 was churned away. The root cause was staring at the screen while holding; with a position on you are not objective.

## Key points

- Decide direction and target with cycle and logic first, then find the small-timeframe entry.
- Buy the stabilization point, not the breakout price; the structure is what breaks.
- The only valid stop is below the turning point. Size down rather than move it.
- Profits from counter-trend scalps are bait for bigger losses.
- Holding a base position means stepping away from the screen.
- BTC is in a mid-cycle rest; ORDI and TIA still have second-wave setups, SOL and AVAX have little room left.
