A quiet review week. Every trade shared the same logic, a large consolidation breaking out toward a main uptrend; some worked, some did not. After the last drawdown I fixed two habits: no adding at highs, and no watching positions.

## Chasing is allowed, heavy chasing is not

On BTC I bought a pullback with a stop at 37,500, then added when a deep wick was reclaimed. The logic was fine; the faults were size on an already-high entry and not cutting when the small platform turned weak. WLD was the same.

The question on any breakout is whether the large-scale structure is complete. WLD had done its groundwork, so a straight run to 5 was reasonable and chasing was justified. But a chased entry has only one valid stop, near the launch point, which is far away. That forces half of normal size, and I used more than normal.

## When direction is right, keep trying

SNX was a large box heading for at least 4, with 4.5 as resistance. I missed the ideal entry, tried two pullbacks and was stopped twice. That is acceptable. The market limits your attempts, each carries a small stop, and if the big picture is right one of them runs. WLD took four attempts on the same logic.

Catch the big things and let the small ones be rough. Big means cycle, structure, logic. Small means which turning point to enter and where the stop sits. On BLUR I re-entered when a false breakout was reclaimed, stop under the recent low, and it ran.

## Next week

BTC sits high, neither strong nor weak, and could correct. As long as it does not crash, money flows into alts, and this second alt wave looks stronger than the first. Trade alts from unstarted positions with small stops and light size. Watch ORDI, ARK, TIA, SOL (77, then 95), APE (2.5 to 2.8, stop 1.52), WLD (5, stop 2.56), EGLD, LINK, QTUM, BLUR and SMR. ETH bought below 2,000 is a hold for 3,000.

## Key points

- A complete large-scale structure justifies chasing, but the distant stop means half size.
- Cut or reduce when the small timeframe turns weak instead of riding to the stop.
- Repeated small-stop attempts are the cost of being right on direction.
- Get the big picture right and allow rough entries and stops.
- New listings that only rise do not run far; deep dips that relaunch do.
- Second-wave alts are stronger, but only enter where the move has not started.
