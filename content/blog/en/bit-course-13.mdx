## BTC: bullish, not buying

BTC has risen since October without a proper pullback. The direction is still up, but price sits at a relative high where profit-taking has not been digested, so breakout attempts from here risk false breaks and chop. The safe structure is a consolidation lower, a reset, then a new launch. Until then any long is a short-term trade, and expecting this breakout to carry to 60,000 or 70,000 is a fantasy.

ETH has more room and does not need a deep flush first. The signal to watch: both sit in boxes, and one day ETH turns stronger than BTC and leads the breakout. That is when ETH becomes a buy with a larger expectation. I closed my ETH after its failed breakout and will wait for the range to settle.

## Short-term structures

For BTC, wait for a push up that holds and try a short-term long there. If the lower edge fails, a probing short is fine, with 38,000 possible if bears are strong. For ETH, never short; watch the 2400 platform and buy its breakout.

## Rules that do not change

Position management applies to every timeframe. High leverage brings big drawdowns and forces stops too tight to be reasonable, so nobody earns steadily with it. BTC and ETH at most 10x, alts at most 5x. Skip hedging, locking and other tricks; more actions mean more confusion. Once you buy, the outcome is set: take profit or stop out, then switch off and sleep. Most of trading is waiting.

The alt rotation that began when BTC stalled at 45,000 has run its course. News, including the ETF, only stimulates in the short term and never changes a trend; reasons get invented after the fact. Markets move like seasons: after a long rise comes rest, and after rest the next move.

## Key points

- A bullish direction at a high is not a buy; wait for a correction and a reset.
- ETH leading BTC out of the range is the signal to get active in ETH.
- BTC short term: long a held push up, probe a short if the lower edge fails; ETH long only above 2400.
- Leverage caps of 10x on BTC and ETH and 5x on alts, with no exceptions.
- Plan before the entry, then let the market decide between profit and stop.
- News never changes a trend; the loss you can bear matters more than conviction.
