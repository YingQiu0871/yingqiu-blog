## Two phases in one week

BTC broke out, ran, then went flat. During the run every altcoin moved together. Once BTC ranged at the highs, altcoins chopped, false breakouts multiplied and only scattered catch-up names like QTUM, Gala and FLOKI moved.

## With-trend trades: STORJ, BSV, WLD

Through STORJ's entire main uptrend I placed no shorts. Inside an uptrend I separate small corrections from big ones. After a big correction, a breakout entry can be held for the whole next leg. Small corrections are scalps, best bought on the dip rather than chased, because by then everyone knows the coin is going up. Every breakout I take must meet three conditions: a main uptrend, room above, and a with-trend structure. After two small corrections the fifth wave is in and I stop buying.

BSV ran straight to resistance, so I bought the turning point of the big correction where the lows stopped breaking. I exited early on nerves before the stop was hit and missed the move. WLD looked ideal, a hot new coin with clean supply and a clean base. Both breakout attempts failed and no good structure followed, so I stopped.

## Counter-trend trades and tilt

TRB at 120 was my best-built short of the week: dense supply, a round number, a vertical run from the 70s. I shorted five times at sensible structures and none worked until after I gave up and went to sleep. That is the problem with counter-trend trades: at a target resistance, nobody knows which structure finally breaks it. A with-trend breakout has one launch point; a reversal has many. MINA and Gala were worse, revenge trades after a loss with no logic at all.

## Key points

- Inside a main uptrend, only go long; let breakouts after big corrections run, scalp small ones from the dip.
- Three conditions for any entry: main uptrend, room above, with-trend structure.
- Counter-trend shorts need many attempts because the reversal point cannot be known.
- Every losing trade came from unclear logic or tilt; never reverse into a random trade after a loss.
- The curve survived a bad week because of position splitting, not stops or reward-to-risk.
- The next altcoin leader: clean daily chart, early strength with BTC, resilience during BTC's correction, room above; SOL and LINK top the list.
