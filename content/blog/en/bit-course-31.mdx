## BTC and ETH

Both read as a range making a second test of the top. If price pushes through, trade the breakout; above it the room is open. But a break this soon is a high-level break after too short a consolidation, so a false breakout is likely. Take it anyway, with modest expectations.

## Why PEOPLE, and why early

The ETH surge was the trigger. My ETH take-profit order filled overnight, but the size of the move said altcoins would follow, so the job became finding the leader. PEPE moved first and I missed its breakout while away. I chose PEOPLE because it had already shown life before ETH moved, its daily structure was clean, and it has a history of running. Pick coins that have already performed.

I took the breakout before the prior high for two reasons. The entry sat at the start of a move after a large correction, so it was with the trend. And BTC and ETH had already rallied, so the market was openly hot and waiting for the prior high would have been too late. Without that backdrop, wait for the prior high. I added at the new high, leaning on a good base cost with a wide stop, which was dangerous; the add should have been lower.

## Who leads, and what season it is

PEPE is already high and its moves are not smooth; the strongest, smoothest coin these days is PEOPLE. Only one wave has run across altcoins and it made real money, which puts the market between spring and summer. A wave that strong usually carries the leader to another leg after it corrects. The drawback is that BTC and ETH have reached the top of their swing, so money may start to leave.

The first wave is over and PEOPLE's structure has broken into a larger correction. Watch for the second wave in PEPE and PEOPLE, and ignore lesser altcoins. A leader never comes from a coin that sat still in the first wave.

## Key points

- Treat BTC and ETH as a range retest; trade a break but expect a false one.
- When ETH surges, immediately look for the altcoin leader among coins that already moved.
- With the market openly hot, break out early; otherwise wait for the prior high.
- The leader is the smoothest, most influential coin, never a laggard from wave one.
- After a strong first wave, focus on the leader's second wave, not on catch-up names.
