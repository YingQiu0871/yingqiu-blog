## A rebound in its tail phase

BTC fell from 69000 to 18000 and has since made two rebound legs; this is the second. The first test of the old high has no breakout momentum, so with over 90 percent probability this is a rebound, not a main uptrend, and its ceiling is somewhere just above 60000. From 18000 to here, more than 85 percent of that space is already used. Whatever comes next is a fish-tail market. A genuinely good move needs time to correct and rebuild momentum first.

There is still room up to 59000 and the trend is up, so the bias stays long, but longs are expensive at this height. The conclusions: do not short, keep expectations small, trade short-term only, and forget about holding to 70000 or 80000. If the next long stalls at 50000 or 48000, you leave.

## Two ways the range can resolve

BTC is ranging between 40000 and 46000. To push to 52000 or 58000 it must first digest this correction, either by space or by time. Losing 40000 and correcting lower is the better path and would give me one more good entry. If buyers keep it pinned up here instead, it needs a long enough sideways period, and nobody can say how long that is. Then you can only try each breakout with small expectations and sell early. At the top of the last cycle, shorts failed repeatedly because dip buyers kept coming, so shorting is off the table.

News such as the ETF decision is only a short stimulus: a dump or a pump, then the market resumes its own path. Only high-leverage holders need to act before the announcement.

ETH has not moved much and has at least 50 percent of room toward 3600. If ETH starts leading BTC and breaks out, that is the start of a new altcoin round, and you spread across alts as a whole new cycle.

## Key points

- This is a rebound at 85 percent of its space; the rest is fish-tail trading.
- Long bias, short holding periods, small expectations, no shorts.
- A correction resolves by space or by time; the drop is the better entry, the sideways path is hard to board.
- News never changes the underlying trend; it only shakes leveraged positions.
- ETH leading BTC is the signal for the next big altcoin move.
- Open full size at once, take profit in three parts when the position is risky, and never average down.
