## The rhythm mistake behind a losing week

The only move last week was a BTC rebound that lifted ETHFI. I had bought BTC on the left side to guard against a bull-mood rally with no proper correction, and the guard worked. The fatal error came after: because it worked, I treated a lukewarm market as a strong one and traded longs far too eagerly.

Two plans for BTC remain: if the 72000 zone breaks upward, go long without thinking and stop out if it is false; if the level fails, wait for the next consolidation. BTC and PEPE stay the two most certain setups while BTC holds 60000.

## One trade at a time

Reviewing losses means pulling out each trade and deciding whether it belonged to the system. The ETHFI longs at new highs, the breakout attempts at the 7.3 platform, the BCH long into a minor pullback and the first PEPE breakout were all logical; their stops were normal friction.

The unacceptable ones were different. A short on a false range breakout I refused to close when it bounced. A PEPE left-side base position cut at the exact low out of frustration. Two revenge shorts on ETHFI. A late SUI chase with a wide stop. Worst of all, after those stops I skipped the ETHFI rebound that did come, eight or nine percent, which would have paid for the week. Following the system would have saved fifty to sixty thousand dollars.

The root was fear of missing out. I never built a full BTC position, felt left behind for a month, and started buying early everywhere. Even the twelve thousand dollars from the left-side BTC entry was harmful: one winning trade outside the rules can collapse a whole system.

## Stops are your life in this market

I survived because every trade had a stop and sizing stayed split. Without stops, SUI alone could have been ruinous. Going forward: no left-side entries, a sober read of market heat, smaller size, and no trade without logic.

## Key points

- Reducing drawdown in a dead market comes down to position size.
- Breakouts and pullback entries on a leader are system losses; revenge shorts and chasing are not.
- Above 60000, BTC and PEPE remain the two most certain opportunities.
- Fear of missing out caused every unacceptable loss.
- Stops and sizing are your life; one skipped stop eventually blows up the account.
