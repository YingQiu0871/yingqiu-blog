## BTC for the next half year

BTC finished a clean main downtrend last year and has rebounded since January. That rebound is nearly done. Next comes a box between the rebound high and the downtrend low, probably until year end, with each swing smaller than the last. I lean bearish here, but a bias is not a signal. Short-term levels: 28100 to 28200 above, 24800 below.

## How a box forms, and where the edge is

On TRB's daily chart: a box is born when a main trend ends and a big rebound sets the opposite extreme. That rebound is the early phase. A second touch of either edge moves it into the middle phase. Only after two highs and two lows, with price squeezing against an edge, does the box have energy to break.

TRB has not done that, so this leg's top is most likely in and going long is a bad bet. Shorting is not automatically good either: inside a box price can pull back shallow or deep, fail to hold, or print a false breakout first. The only entry with certainty is at the edge of a mature box, where price either goes or it does not. Trading the middle of a range is asking to die, and I never chase breakouts.

## How I review charts

Open AIcoin, 15-minute for BTC and ETH, 5-minute for altcoin leaders. Start with a specific problem, such as why your breakout entries keep failing. Go back to a past period like May 2021, drag forward one candle at a time, stop wherever you would enter, decide the stop, then scroll on to check yourself. At every pause ask which phase of the cycle this is.

## Key points

- BTC's rebound is ending; expect a shrinking range into year end.
- A box needs two highs and two lows plus a squeeze at the edge before it can break.
- No edge exists inside a range; wait for the mature edge and never chase breakouts.
- Most of my profit comes from main uptrends; after that leg I sit out the range.
- Review with a question in mind, candle by candle, naming the cycle phase each time.
- Leaders find you: watch the heat list and enter after the first pullback.
