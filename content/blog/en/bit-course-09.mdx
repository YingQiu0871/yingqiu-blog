## Leaders move with BTC

Last week I traded four coins: short-term ORDI and BIGTIME, plus a basket of BTC, ETH and SOL opened when BTC launched and closed near 44,000. BTC launched on 1 December around 11:00 and topped on 6 December at 07:00. ORDI and BIGTIME launched and topped at the same times, and once BTC reached 45,000 both lost momentum. ORDI's earlier run on 7 to 10 November followed the same pattern.

Every leader needs resonance with BTC. When BTC is in late consolidation, capital tries alts with good daily structures and attention. When BTC launches, alts rise with it to BTC's swing top. When BTC reaches that top and starts to oscillate, capital retreats from the leaders. That is why I opened the basket where I did, and why I closed it and went short near 44,000.

## After the leader tops

Money then rotates into catch-up coins: MEME, BAKE, later ETH, OP, ARB, SOL and LINK. None of these rallies are as clean as the leader, because BTC needs to correct. Catch-up rallies are leftover heat; the main capital is resting. A leader is certain, a catch-up coin is random, so after the leader tops, rest.

## Divergences in a main uptrend

If you missed the launch, wait for a divergence and buy the stabilization, never a breakout at highs. Take the first divergence boldly, try a second, then stop. Acceleration at highs is waiting to die, and a false breakout there means the top is in. Once the bounce after the main uptrend is done, delete the coin from your watchlist until BTC has corrected or the coin has consolidated for a month. Do not short a top you have identified: you fight momentum, dip buyers cap the reward, and you miss the next long.

## Key points

- Leaders launch at the end of BTC's consolidation and retreat at BTC's swing top.
- While BTC has not topped, the alt leader is unlikely to top.
- Catch-up rallies are weaker and random; rest instead of chasing them.
- Buy the first and second divergence in a main uptrend, then stop; no breakouts at highs.
- Identifying a top is not a reason to short it.
- Split capital into parts; at most 5x leverage on alts and 10x on BTC.
