## A defensive week, reviewed

The market has no heat and the big profits of this round are gone; keeping what you made in BTC's two waves is the win. To improve faster: be on the right path, review trades, then practise deliberately. A calm mind speeds it up, so never trade with borrowed money.

My WLD longs were box-bottom bounces, which may stall, grind or shoot; that uncertainty is why I do not recommend them. The later longs played a fifth wave out of the box, sold into strength because a large correction follows the last wave.

## Eight INJ shorts

I shorted the end of a five-wave rise on INJ eight times and lost over 30,000 U. The direction and logic were right, and that is the dangerous part: the market is always right and you are always its student. The stops were too tight, because a counter-trend short cannot afford a wide one; a single candle could run to 40. So I kept chasing turns that look like every other top, and after too many losses I did not hold the attempt that worked. Counter-trend trades have no solution: wide stops blow up, tight stops bleed, and the reward is small either way. If you must, go light and give the stop room, or rest.

## ACE short and long

Shorting ACE was sound: a converged box, a new coin with heavy early selling pressure, and a lukewarm BTC. Converged new coins are good shorts; do not long them early. The problem was size, inflated by the INJ losses, plus a fake downside breakout. The ACE long I posted was a classic breakout, retest and second wave, the one entry worth heavy size, and I would take it again ten thousand times. It failed, so the stop mattered: below the retest, or 15.5 if you chased. Without stops ACE would have killed you several times.

## Key points

- Lower size, give stops room, and stop trading against the trend; that would have saved 40,000 U this week.
- The market is always right; correct logic does not justify a counter-trend trade.
- Box-bottom bounces have no certainty; breakouts at the end of a box can offer 1 to 10.
- Converged new coins are strong shorts in a cool market.
- Good setups still fail; every trade carries a stop, and holding losers means you doubt you can earn it back.
