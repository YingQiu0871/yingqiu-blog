## Six trades, two types

Last week's trades looked like six separate ideas: shorts on TIA and BTC, longs on ING, OP, ETH and ETC. They fall into two types: counter-trend shorts at a high after a completed five-wave move, and trend longs after a full correction. Nine out of ten trades I post look like one of these.

## The short side

A high-position short needs three things in order: I judge the price to be high, the market is retreating, and only then do I look for a pattern. The pattern can be a false breakout, a double top or a blow-off; it does not matter. The TIA short had all three, yet it never reached my 11.5 or 10 to 10.2 targets. That is what counter-trend means: if good conditions guaranteed the move, it would be a trend trade. So take profit early, never hold a counter-trend trade to a target, and never go long at such a level even if you refuse to short it.

On the BTC short near 44500, if you missed the start you have two choices: chase with a small position and a wide stop of about 1.5%, or wait for support to break completely after the dip-buyers are flushed out. Either way, size down; a heavy late entry cannot survive the pullback that is likely there.

## The long side

ING and OP had clean structures: a large advance, a full correction, then a fresh push. ETH and ETC had the same shape. The difference was the environment. Mainstream money was retreating, so ING and OP stalled; ETH had news resolve, the market reached consensus, and I treated it as a new main uptrend. A move of that scale always has a resonating altcoin leader, and ETC, with a big daily structure and an ETH-related chain, was my seed pick. I started scaling out at 29 against a first resistance at 30, with 35 as the second target.

## Key points

- Environment, not the chart pattern, decides whether the same setup wins or loses.
- Counter-trend shorts: high, retreating market, then any pattern; take profit early.
- Never go long at a high after skipping many low entries.
- Late entries mean small size with a wide stop, or waiting for a full breakdown.
- News does not create a trend; it removes the disagreement that blocks one already in place.
- Learn the direction and logic; stops and entries should fit your own temperament.
