## The week in review

BTC's second wave barely paused until the low 50000s, consolidated briefly, then ran to 57000 and on to 65000, far stronger than the tail-end move I expected. My base case is another push, probably through 70000, then a consolidation with one more spike before a top.

Asked to name the week's three best trades, everyone said PEPE. It stacked three things: resonance with BTC's surge, a history as a wild coin, and an animal-coin sector that had not moved for a long time. My comparison was SHIB, and the two charts moved identically.

## Why catch-up trades felt bad

Once PEPE reached a high, many coins started catching up, but BTC kept pulling and none ran cleanly. I opened a lot of LTC, SOL and DOGE and made nothing; I entered while BTC was still rising, before money had rotated out. DOGE was the strongest mainstream catch-up from 27 February, LTC after the 29 February wick was an excellent entry, and SOL near 118 was strong but I could not hold it.

Resonance beats catch-up. Resonance trades are made with foresight while BTC moves; catch-up trades begin only after everyone can see the market is strong, so they chop by nature. Trade them with low leverage and wider stops, or skip the phase if you can catch the leader.

## The seasons of a cycle

Spring is the start, when you can chase a little. Summer belongs to leaders such as PEPE moving with BTC. Autumn, once BTC is high and consolidating, is catch-up season. After the final spike comes winter and the whole market goes cold. Knowing the season tells you where the opportunity is and how clean it will be.

Next week the priority is defence. Do not FOMO after missing trades. Two setups are worth waiting for: PEPE after its current correction completes, and WLD once its high consolidation ends, using APT as the template; from 10 the target is at least 15.

## Key points

- Trades start with BTC; if you miss it, find the resonating altcoin leader at once.
- Leader candidates: new coins, hot coins, strong daily charts, animal coins, news-driven sectors.
- Catch-up trades begin after BTC pulls back, not while it is still rising.
- Trade catch-ups with light size and wide stops, or skip them for the leader.
- Spring chase, summer leaders, autumn catch-up, winter shutdown.
- Defend first, and wait for PEPE's correction and WLD's consolidation to resolve.
