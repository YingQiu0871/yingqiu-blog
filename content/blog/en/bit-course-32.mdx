## PEOPLE and the week's two setups

Treat PEOPLE's earlier move as a large platform. It broke out straight from the base, pulled back on top of it, and is now restarting: read that as a daily-level box breakout and the start of a main uptrend. The first resistance above is reachable with good odds and probably means a new high; the second lies further up. Beyond that, take it one level at a time.

BB has also broken its platform near 0.71 with plenty of room on the daily chart; watch for the retest of the prior high and the break.

Two setups look relatively certain for the week: the leader's second wave in PEOPLE, and the catch-up rallies the leader drags along once PEOPLE and BB have risen far.

PEPE deserves less attention. Its market cap is high and, with a good structure in a strong altcoin market, it managed only 50%. Money no longer likes it. Altcoins are currently trading independently of BTC and ETH, so as long as the leader keeps climbing, keep trading altcoins. BTC offers only a small trendline setup with modest odds, since its consolidation is short.

## Catch-up is the fallback, not the plan

I named the leader; do not go looking for catch-up names instead. Catch-up trades exist for when you missed the leader and have nothing else. TURBO was a range bounce after a finished uptrend; I took profit early, though 80 to 82 was visible. NOT was a continuation from a moderate divergence, worth one or two legs and then out; buying it now, after several divergences and at a high level, is fish-tail trading. W was a catch-up attempt that went nowhere; its daily structure and history are poor.

## Sizing when you chase

I entered late today because the breakout looked unclean, then went in light once it confirmed. Perfect structures rarely arrive, so adjust: if you chase, cut size so the loss is acceptable, and give the stop reasonable room. A tight stop on a chased entry gets hit, then you chase again and lose again.

## Key points

- PEOPLE's breakout and retest marks the start of a main uptrend; aim for the first resistance and reassess.
- Watch the leader's second wave and the catch-up rallies it triggers; shift attention from PEPE to BB and PEOPLE.
- While altcoins trade independently of BTC, follow the leader rather than BTC.
- Catch-up trades are what you do after missing the leader, never the first choice.
- Chased entries need smaller size and a wider, reasonable stop.
