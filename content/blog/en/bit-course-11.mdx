## BTC and ETH

Two things about BTC are certain: it is in a large box with no breakout structure in sight, and the daily uptrend has not ended. So BTC probably still has upward momentum. But price is entering the upper, chip-dense half of the box at a relative high, so the move from here will be choppy and nothing like the clean run before. Short term, if the current zone holds, BTC may push toward 45,000, meet resistance and fall back into the range.

ETH keeps its chance of a clean independent rally as long as it holds 2100; every cycle has ended with an ETH move after BTC ranges. Neither has corrected enough yet. Hold existing ETH longs; without one, wait for a bigger correction. Bullish but not buying.

## A market with no leader

During BTC's second leg to 44,800 only BTC, ORDI and BIGTIME rose. ORDI stopped on 7 December at 03:00. Then ADA and DOT ran until the 11th, the market corrected into BTC's wick, and since then a new batch moves every couple of days: INJ, WLD, SOL, OP. Ask who the alt leader was during the ORDI run and nine out of ten people agree. Ask who it is this week and every answer differs.

You only know the leader after it has pumped, and coins with the same structure are everywhere. Capital is dispersed, nothing has persistence, and once a coin like WLD or SOL has run, trading the pullback only buys you a range. The daily profit effect is a trap. I had been trading it anyway, so I cut my positions. Rotation like this consumes bulls' confidence and rarely lasts; I will defend until BTC and ETH have corrected fully and capital refocuses.

## Key points

- BTC is still in a box and still in an uptrend, but the upper half of the box at a high is hard to profit from.
- Short term, watch the current zone: hold and break toward 45,000, then likely back into the range.
- ETH holding 2100 keeps the independent-rally scenario alive; bullish but not buying.
- A leader you can only name after the pump is not a leader; dispersed capital means no persistence.
- Go all in on focused, resonant leader runs; cut size and defend during rotation.
- Nothing in this market is certain, only probabilities.
