## Reasonable expectations on BTC and ETH

BTC not breaking out yet is the opportunity, not a missed one. The longer it consolidates, the better the breakout. Wait for a pullback or the break, or hold with a stop below. But count the waves: this leg would be the fifth of a bounce, at a high level. 73k or 75k is possible, yet decisions rest on what happens most often out of 100 similar setups, not on the best case.

ETH has a full daily-level correction behind it, a good launch point for a long. The only risk is BTC dragging it down.

## The PEPE long and the BTC trendline trade

PEPE showed a textbook structure: main uptrend, a huge divergence at the top, a complete re-consolidation, then a breakout. That is a new main uptrend starting, and you take it even though one time in ten it fails, as this one did.

The BTC trade used a second-rate trick I do not recommend learning: a large trendline formed inside a long range, broken near 63000. CPI did not cause the move.

Because the trade sat inside a range, where any resistance can reverse price completely, I scaled out at 66000, 67000 and around 70000. The first tranche filled overnight near 65900. In the morning I moved the 67000 order to 70000, a mistake born of nerves. Inside a range, cash in as you go.

## Why shorting PEPE was flawed

On the large scale PEPE is a range, not a downtrend, and nobody said the top of a range is a short. A real short needs a high level, convergence and a five-wave structure, with the large divergence as the target. Without those, only a quick short is possible: after a false breakout collapses back through the trendline, short the bounce. I did that twice and was lifted both times, so I left; weak logic and little room below. The breakdown came later while I was streaming. A short grid across the range would suit this chop better. I do not recommend shorting it.

## Key points

- Decide by the most probable outcome across 100 similar setups, never by the best case.
- A breakout from a full re-consolidation after a huge divergence is a new main uptrend; take it despite the 10% failure rate.
- Trades inside a range are not trend trades: scale out at each resistance.
- A range top is not a short signal; shorts need high level, convergence and five waves.
- Counter-trend shorts may need many attempts; expect to be stopped before the one that works.
