**氢核NMR谱学特征与J耦合类型 Spectroscopic Features of 1H NMR and Types of J Coupling →**

---

- **NMR谱图主要轴与化学位移 Main Spectrum Axis and Chemical Shift →**

- NMR谱图的横轴代表化学位移（chemical shift），用ppm（δ）表示，常见范围为0~12。

The horizontal axis of the NMR spectrum represents chemical shift (δ), measured in ppm, typically ranging from 0 to 12.

- 这些化学位移区域反映了氢在不同分子结构环境中的电子云密度。

These chemical shift regions indicate electron density variations for hydrogens in different molecular environments.

- **谱峰的多重性及邻氢信息 Peak Multiplicity and Neighboring Protons →**

- 每一组信号对应于分子中一组等效氢核。

Each set of signals corresponds to a group of equivalent protons in the molecule.

- 多重性的本质是相邻氢核之间的自旋-自旋耦合作用。

The essence of multiplicity is spin-spin coupling between neighboring protons.

- 常见多重性包括：

Common multiplicities include:

- 单峰（singlet, s）：无邻氢。→ Singlet (s): no neighboring protons.

- 双峰（doublet, d）：1个邻氢。→ Doublet (d): one neighboring proton.

- 三重峰（triplet, t）：2个邻氢。→ Triplet (t): two neighboring protons.

- 四重峰（quartet, q）：3个邻氢。→ Quartet (q): three neighboring protons.

- 双双峰（doublet of doublets, dd）：分别与2个不同环境的邻氢耦合。→ Doublet of doublets (dd): coupled to two nonequivalent neighboring protons.

- 多重性说明了被观测氢核周围的邻氢数量和分布，帮助揭示分子结构片段。

The multiplicity reveals the number and distribution of neighboring protons, aiding structural fragment identification.

- **自旋-自旋耦合常数J（J Coupling Constants）→**

- J耦合由化学键连接的相邻氢核产生，导致谱图出现多重峰。

J coupling is generated by spin interactions of protons connected through chemical bonds, resulting in multiplet peaks.

- J耦合常数以Hz为单位，通过多重峰中相邻峰的距离测量。

The coupling constant (J) is measured in Hz, determined by the distance between neighboring peaks in a multiplet.

- J值大小与化学键的类型、电子环境及氢核空间关系有关。

J values depend on the type of chemical bond, electronic environment, and spatial relationship between protons.

- **J耦合类型及典型意义 Types of J Coupling and Typical Meanings →**

- J3耦合（三键耦合）：通过3个键相连的氢核常见于sp3碳链，是NMR中最典型的耦合类型。

J3 coupling (three-bond coupling): Protons coupled via three bonds (usually in sp3 chains), is the most typical NMR coupling.

- J2耦合（双键耦合）：通过2个键链接，常见于相邻sp2碳、或手性碳附近的sp3碳。

J2 coupling (two-bond coupling): Protons coupled via two bonds, commonly seen in adjacent sp2 carbons or sp3 carbons near chiral centers.

- J4耦合（四键耦合）：通过4个键间接耦合，芳香环中meta位氢核间常见。

J4 coupling (four-bond coupling): Coupling via four bonds, typically observed between meta protons on an aromatic ring.

- 在非芳香性环烃结构（sp3环状结构）内也可观测到J4耦合。

J4 coupling also occurs in non-aromatic cyclic (sp3) structures between distant hydrogens.

- **杂化类型和分子结构对耦合的影响 Influence of Hybridization and Structure →**

- sp3杂化碳链上的氢核可显示出J2、J3或J4耦合，具体取决于分子空间结构与对称性。

Hydrogens on sp3 carbon chains may display J2, J3 or J4 coupling, depending on spatial structure and symmetry.

- 如果sp3碳邻近手性中心，两氢核往往不等效，会显示J2或J3耦合；

If an sp3 carbon is adjacent to a chiral center, the two protons may be nonequivalent, resulting in J2 or J3 couplings.

- 芳香体系meta位氢核之间表现为J4耦合，para耦合通常极弱。

Meta hydrogens in aromatic systems exhibit J4 coupling, while para couplings are generally very weak.

- 烯烃等sp2结构也有典型J2和J3耦合类型。

Alkenes and other sp2 systems also exhibit characteristic J2 and J3 coupling.

- **J耦合常数大小与判别 J Coupling Constant Magnitude and Interpretation →**

- 结构不同对应的J常数值（Hz）有规律可循，可借助参考表判断结构片段类型。

The magnitude of J constants in Hz vary with structure and can be referenced to judge structural motifs.

- 按照化学位移区间及J耦合常数，进行综合分析，更精确推断分子结构。

Combining chemical shift regions with J coupling constants enables more accurate molecular structure identification.

- **谱峰积分（Integration）指示氢的数目 Peak Integration Indicates Proton Number →**

- 每个信号下的积分面积与贡献该信号的氢核数成正比。

The integration (area under a signal) is proportional to the number of protons producing that signal.

- 例如出现面积比为3
2:1的峰，则分别代表3、2、1个氢。

For example, if the integration ratios are 3:2:1, they represent 3, 2, and 1 protons, respectively.

- 典型甲基（CH₃）无邻氢，表现为单峰且积分为3。

A typical methyl group (CH₃) with no neighboring protons shows a singlet peak with integration value of 3.

- **氢核NMR谱分析的综合思路 Comprehensive Approaches for 1H NMR Spectrum Analysis →**

- 明确信号的化学位移、积分、形状（多重性）及J耦合常数，结合结构和杂化类型，逐步推断结构信息。

Identify the chemical shift, integration, multiplicity, and J coupling constant for each signal and combine with knowledge of structure and hybridization type to infer the molecular architecture.

- 参考耦合常数与化学位移的典型值，验证推理是否合理。

Consult reference values for coupling constants and chemical shifts to cross-check the reasoning.
