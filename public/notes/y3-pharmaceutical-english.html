句子

1
Biopharmaceutics is the subject focusing on studying the regularities of absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion of drug and their preparations in vivo, and further elucidating the relationships among preparational factors, biological factors and drug effects.

生物药剂学是研究药物及其制剂在体内的吸收、分布、代谢、排泄过程，阐明药物的剂型因素、机体的生物因素与药物效应之间相互关系的一门科学。

1
Before the reader can appreciate the meaning and clinical significance of biopharmaceutics, it is necessary to introduce the concept of drug bioavailability.

在读者理解生物药剂学的意思和临床意义之前，有必要介绍一下药物的生物利用度这一概念。

1
The therapeutic response of a drug is normally dependent on an adequate concentration of the drug being achieved and then maintained at the site or sites of action of the drug.

药物的治疗反应通常取决于药物的作用部位是否达到并保持足够的药物浓度。

1
In the case of systemically acting drugs (i.e. drugs that reach these sites via the systemic circulation), it is generally accepted for clinical purposes that a dynamic equilibrium exists between the concentration of drug at its site(s) of action and the concentration of drug in blood plasma.

临床上认为，全身作用的药物(即通过体循环到达作用部位的药物)在其作用部位的药物浓度和药物的血浆浓度之间存在一种动态平衡。

1
An important consequence of this dynamic equilibrium is that it permits a therapeutically effective concentration of drug to be achieved at its site(s) of action by adjustment of the concentration of drug in blood plasma.

这种动态平衡的一个重要作用是它允许通过调整药物的血浆浓度达到在作用部位上有治疗作用的药物浓度。

1
Strictly speaking, the concentration of drug in plasma water (i.e. protein-free plasma) is a more accurate index of drug concentration at the site(s) of action than the concentration of drug in whole plasma since a drug may often bind in a reversible manner to plasma protein.

严格地来讲，比起整个血浆中的药物浓度，在其作用部位的血清(即无蛋白血浆)中的药物浓度是一个更加准确的指标，因为药物与血浆蛋白的结合往往是可逆的。

1
Only drug which is unbound (i.e. dissolved in plasma water) can pass out of the plasma through the capillary endothelium and reach other body fluids and tissues and hence its site(s) of action.

只有游离的药物(即没有溶于血清中)才能透过毛细血管内皮进入血浆，到达其他体液和组织中而最终到达其作用部位。

1
Drug concentration in whole blood is also not considered to be an accurate indirect index of the concentration of drug at its site(s) of action since drug can bind to and enter blood cells.

由于药物可与血细胞结合并进入血细胞，因此全血中的药物浓度也不能作为药物作用部位浓度的准确间接指标。

1
However, to measure the concentration of an unbound drug in plasma water requires more complex and sensitive assay methods than to measure the total concentration of both unbound and bound drug in total plasma.

但是，检验血清中某个游离药物的浓度比检验血浆中游离和结合的药物的总体浓度需要更复杂、更灵敏的检验方法。

1
Thus, for clinical purposes, drug concentration in blood plasma is usually measured and is regarded as an index of drug concentration at the site(s) of action of the drug and of the clinical effects of the drug.

因此，在临床上，一般只测定药物的血浆浓度，并把它作为作用部位和药物临床治疗效果的一项指标。

1
However, it should be realized that this is a simplification and may not always be valid.

但应认识到这只是一种简化的做法，并不总是有效

1
Indeed one should not draw inferences about the clinical effects of a drug from its plasma concentration until it has been established that the two are consistently correlated.

实际上，除非已经证实药物的临床效果和血浆浓度是相互关联、相互一致的，否则，不能从血浆浓度推测其临床效果。

1
It has been assumed that the plasma drug concentration is directly proportional to the clinical effect of that drug.

现在普遍认为，血浆药物浓度与该药的临床效果有直接的比例关系。

1
Strictly speaking, the concentration of drug in plasma water (i.e. protein-free plasma) is a more accurate index of drug concentration at the site(s) of action than the concentration of drug in whole plasma since a drug may often bind in a reversible manner to plasma protein.

严格来说，血浆水（即无蛋白血浆）中的药物浓度比全血浆中的药物浓度更能准确反映药物在作用部位的浓度，因为药物通常会以可逆的方式与血浆蛋白结合。

1
Only drug which is unbound (i.e. dissolved in plasma water) can pass out of the plasma through the capillary endothelium and reach other body fluids and tissues and hence its site(s) of action.

只有未与血浆结合的药物（即溶于血浆中的水）才能通过毛细血管内皮细胞从血浆中排出，到达其他体液和组织，从而到达其作用部位。

1
Drug concentration in whole blood is also not considered to be an accurate indirect index of the concentration of drug at its site(s) of action since drug can bind to and enter blood cells. 由于药物可与血细胞结合并进入血细胞，因此全血中的药物浓度也不能被视为药物作用部位浓度的准确间接指标。
2
However, to measure the concentration of an unbound drug in plasma water requires more complex and sensitive assay methods 【than to measure the total concentration of both unbound and bound drug in total plasma】.

但是，检验血清中某个游离药物的浓度比检验血浆中游离和结合的药物的总体浓度需要更复杂、更灵敏的检验方法。

1
Thus, for clinical purposes, drug concentration in blood plasma is usually measured and is regarded as an index of drug concentration at the site(s) of action of the drug and of the clinical effects of the drug.However, it should be realized (that this is a simplification and may not always be valid.)

因此，在临床上，一般只测定药物的血浆浓度，并把它作为作用部位和药物临床治疗效果的一项指标。但应认识到这只是一种简化的做法，并不总是有效。

1
However, to measure the concentration of an unbound drug in plasma water requires more complex and sensitive assay methods 【than to measure the total concentration of both unbound and bound drug in total plasma】.

但是，检验血清中某个游离药物的浓度比检验血浆中游离和结合的药物的总体浓度需要更复杂、更灵敏的检验方法。

1
Thus, for clinical purposes, drug concentration in blood plasma is usually measured and is regarded as an index of drug concentration at the site(s) of action of the drug and of the clinical effects of the drug.However, it should be realized (that this is a simplification and may not always be valid.)

因此，在临床上，一般只测定药物的血浆浓度，并把它作为作用部位和药物临床治疗效果的一项指标。但应认识到这只是一种简化的做法，并不总是有效。

1
The concentration of drug in blood plasma depends on numerous factors.

血浆中的药物浓度取决于多种因素。

1
These include the relative amount of an administered dose (that enters the systemic circulation), the rate at (which this occurs,) the rate and extent of distribution of the drug between the systemic circulation and other tissues and fluids and the rate of elimination of the drug from the body.

包括所用药物进入体循环的相对的量、吸收的速度、体循环、其他组织及体液之间的分布的速度和程度，以及药物从体内排出的速度。

1
Apart from the intravenous route of drug administration, (where a drug is introduced directly into the blood circulation), all other routes of administering systemically acting drugs involve the absorption of drug from the place of administration into the blood.

除了静脉注射这一给药途径的药物可以直接进入血液循环外，所有其他具有全身作用的药物的给药途径都涉及将药物从给药部位吸收进血液的过程。

1
Drug must be absorbed in a sufficient quantity and at a sufficient rate in order to achieve a certain blood plasma concentration （which, in turn, will produce an appropriate concentration of drug at its site(s) of action to elicit the desired therapeutic response）.

药物必须以足够的剂量和足够的速率被吸收，以达到一定的血浆浓度，进而使药物在作用位点达到适当的浓度以实现预期的治疗效果。

1
It follows （that there are two aspects of drug absorption （which are important in clinical practice, namely, the rate and the extent to which the administered dose is absorbed.））

药物吸收在临床实践中具有两方面的重要意义，即所给予药物被机体吸收的速率和程度。

1
Simply 【because a certain dose of a drug is administered to a patient】, there is no guarantee (except for intravenous administration) （that all of that dose will reach the systemic circulation.）

仅给于患者给予一定剂量的药物，并不能确保(除了静脉给药)所有药物都进入体循环。

1
The fraction of an administered dose of drug （that reaches the systemic circulation in unchanged form） is known as the bioavailable dose.

以原形进入体循环的药物的剂量被称为生物可利用的剂量。

1
The relative amount of an administered dose of a particular drug （which reaches the systemic circulation intact） and the rate at （which this occurs） is known as the bioavailability of that drug.

药物的生物利用度被定义为该种药物以原形进入体循环的相对量及速率。

1
Bioavailability is thus concerned with the quantity and the rate at 【which the intact form of a particular drug appears in the systemic circulation following administration of that drug.】

生物利用度与给药后以药物原形进入体循环的药物的剂量和速率相关。

1
The bioavailability exhibited by a drug is thus very important in determining whether a therapeutically effective concentration is achieved at the site(s) of action of the drug.

因此，药物的生物利用度对确定药物在作用部位是否达到有效的治疗浓度非常重要。

1
In defining bioavailability in these terms it is assumed that intact drug is the therapeutically active form of the drug.

在定义生物利用度时，假定完整的药物是具有治疗活性的药物形式。

1
This definition of bioavailability would not be valid in the case of a pro-drug, whose therapeutic action normally depends on it being converted into its therapeutically active form prior to or on reaching the systemic circulation.

生物利用度的这一定义不适用于原形药物，因为原形药物的治疗作用通常取决于药物在进入全身循环之前或之后转化为其治疗活性形式。

1
It should also be noted （that in the context of bioavailability, the term systemic circulation refers primarily to venous blood (excluding the hepatic portal blood during the absorption phase) and arterial blood （which carries the intact drug to the tissues.））

还应注意到，在讲生物利用度时，体循环主要是指静脉血(不包括吸收过程中的肝门血流)和将完整药物运到组织的动脉血流。

1
Hence according to the definition of bioavailability, an administered dose of a particular drug in an oral dosage form will be 100% bioavailable 【only if the drug is completely released from the dosage form into solution in the gastrointestinal fluids.】

因此，根据生物利用度的定义，只有当药物被完全从其剂型释放到胃肠液中，这种药物以口服形式服用的剂量才可以被百分之百地生物利用。

1
The released drug must also be completely stable in solution in the gastrointestinal fluids and all of the drug must pass through the gastrointestinal barrier into the mesenteric circulation without being metabolized.

释放的药物还必须在胃肠液的溶液中完全稳定，并且所有药物必须通过胃肠屏障进入肠系膜循环而不被代谢。

1
Finally, all of the absorbed drug must pass into the systemic circulation without being metabolized on passing through the liver.

最后，所有被吸收的药物都必须进入全身循环，而不经过肝脏代谢

1
Thus any factor which adversely affects either the release of the drug from the dosage form, its dissolution in the gastrointestinal fluids, its stability in the gastrointestinal fluids, its permeation through and stability in the gastrointestinal barrier or its stability in the hepatic portal circulation will influence the bioavailability exhibited by that drug from the dosage form in which it was administered.

因此，任何对药物从剂型中的释放、药物在胃肠液中的溶解、药物在胃肠液中的稳定性、药物在胃肠屏障中的渗透和稳定性或药物在肝门静脉循环中的稳定性产生不利影响的因素，都会影响药物从给药剂型中的生物利用度。

1
Many factors have been found to influence the time course of a drug in the plasma and hence at its site(s) of action.

我们发现很多因素都可以影响药物在血浆中和作用部位的经时过程。

1
These include the foods eaten by the patient, the effect of the disease state on drug absorption, the age of the patient, the site(s) of absorption of the administered drug, the coadministration of other drugs, the physical and chemical properties of the administered drug, the type of dosage form, the composition and method of manufacture of the dosage form and the size of dose and frequency of administration of the dosage form.

包括病人吃的食物、疾病状况对药物的吸收影响、病人的年龄、所给药物的吸收部位、所服用的其他药物、所给药物的理化性质、剂型、成分、剂型制备方法、剂量大小和服用频率。

第二篇

The New Drug Package Insert-Implications for Patient Safety

The package insert contains detailed drug information compiled and distributed by the drug manufacturer after FDA review and approval. 药品说明书包含药品生产商经过 FDA 审查和批准后编制和分发的详细药品信息。The purpose of the package insert is to provide complete and unbiased prescribing and safety information to health professionals. 说明书的目的是向卫生专业人员提供完整且公正的处方和安全信息。In 1968, a two-line warning placed on the isoproterenol inhaler package is considered the first patient package insert. 1968 年，异丙肾上腺素吸入器包装上放置的两行警告被认为是第一个患者包装说明书。 Although in 1970 the FDA mandated that a separate patient package insert detailing risk and benefits accompany each package of birth control pills, it was not until 1979 that the FDA promulgated the content and format of physician prescribing information inserts (also known as the package insert). 尽管在 1970 年 FDA 规定每个避孕药包装必须附有一份单独的患者说明书，详细说明每个避孕药包装的风险和益处，但直到 1979 年 FDA 才颁布了医生处方信息说明书（也称为包装说明书）的内容和格式。 Within the Warnings section of this document, the term "boxed warning" (black box warning) was used for the first time in FDA labeling requirements. 在本文件的警告部分中，术语“黑框警告”（黑框警告）首次在 FDA 标签要求中使用。Since then, the volume and detail of the package insert has increased significantly. 从那时起，包装说明书的体积和细节显着增加。In the 1980s, the FDA acknowledged that the information included in the package insert had become so lengthy, detailed, and complex that it was difficult for health practitioners to find specific information and to distinguish critical information from less important issues. 在 20 世纪 80 年代，FDA承认包装说明书中包含的信息变得如此冗长、详细和复杂，以至于健康从业者很难找到具体信息并将关键信息与不太重要的问题区分开来。

To address the problem, the FDA conducted research to assess how prescription drug labeling was used by health care practitioners, and to determine which labeling information was considered the most important. 为了解决这个问题，FDA 进行了一项研究，评估医疗保健从业人员如何使用处方药标签，并确定哪些标签信息被认为是最重要的。The studies documented that many practitioners usually find the information they need, but that the process was often time-intensive and clinically inefficient. 研究表明，许多从业者通常都能找到他们需要的信息，但这个过程往往耗时且临床效率低下。In addition, the package insert format disproportionately stressed the occurrence of extremely rare clinical events. 此外，药品说明书格式过分强调了极其罕见的临床事件。As a result, the FDA developed a new package insert format that has three major sections: (1)"The Highlights of Prescribing Information"; (2)"Full Prescribing Information Table of Contents"; and (3)"The Full Prescribing Information"(FPI). 因此，FDA 开发了一种新的包装说明书格式，其中包含三个主要内容：（1）“处方信息要点”； (2)《完整处方信息目录》； (3)“完整处方信息”(FPI)。This new organization of information improves access to critical information and makes the label more user-friendly. 这种新的信息组织方式改善了对关键信息的访问，并使标签更加用户友好。The new label format proposal was issued in December 2000 and, after public meetings and comment by practitioners, a final version became official in June 2006. 新的标签格式提案于 2000 年 12 月发布，经过公开会议和征求意见后经过实践者的努力，最终版本于 2006 年 6 月正式发布。A transition to the new format will not be mandatory for drugs that received FDA approval more than 5 years before the final ruling in June 2006. 对于 2006 年 6 月最终裁决之前 5 年以上获得 FDA 批准的药物，不强制要求过渡到新格式。However, pharmaceutical companies may elect to reformat the package insert for these older drugs. 然而，制药公司可能会选择重新格式化这些旧药物的包装说明书。Drugs approved within the 5-year window must resubmit the package insert in the revised label format during a 3~7-year phase－in period to comply with the new FDA standards. 在5年窗口内批准的药物必须在3~7年的阶段内以修订后的标签格式重新提交说明书，以符合新的FDA标准。Here list some sections selected in the new drug package insert. 这里列出了新药说明书中选取的部分章节。

1
Highlights of Prescribing Information处方信息要点

Most of the information included in the "Highlights of Prescribing Information" section communicates risks or warning information. “处方信息要点”部分中包含的大部分信息传达风险或警告信息。A succinct summary of critical clinical information is presented in a bulleted format that is cross-referenced to the FPI section for more in depth explanation. 关键临床信息的简洁摘要以项目符号格式呈现，与 FPI 部分交叉引用以获得更深入的解释。The organization of the section reflects the priorities and most common patterns of product insert usage as expressed by practitioners during FDA reformatting studies. 该部分的组织反映了从业人员在 FDA 重新格式化研究期间表达的产品说明书使用的优先事项和最常见模式。

2
Black Box Warning黑匣子警告

The black box warning is set apart as the most prominent information included in a product insert. 黑框警告被设置为产品说明书中最突出的信息。Any warning elevated to the status of a black box warning must be bolded (only the heading must be in all capitals, not the text of the warning) and "boxed" by a solid black line on all four sides. 任何提升为黑框警告状态的警告都必须加粗（只有标题必须全部大写，而不是警告文本），并在所有四个边上用黑色实线“装箱”。A black box warning is indicated in the following three situations, but may be used in other situations to highlight warning information that is particularly important to the prescriber: 在以下三种情况下会显示黑框警告，但也可在其他情况下用于突出显示对处方者特别重要的警告信息：

(1) There is an adverse reaction so serious in proportion to the potential benefit from the drug that it is essential that it be considered in assessing the risks and benefits of using the drug. 与该药物的潜在益处相比，不良反应非常严重，因此在评估使用该药物的风险和益处时必须考虑该不良反应。This includes potentially life threatening or permanently disabling adverse reactions. 这包括可能危及生命或永久致残的不良反应。

(2) There is a serious reaction that can be prevented or reduced in frequency or severity by patient selection, careful monitoring, avoiding certain concomitant therapy, addition of another drug or managing patient in a specific manner, or avoiding use in a specific clinical situation. 通过选择患者、仔细监测、避免某些伴随治疗、添加另一种药物或以特定方式管理患者，或避免在特定临床情况下使用，可以预防严重反应或减少其发生频率或严重程度。

(3) FDA approved the drug with restrictions on use and distribution to assure safe use. FDA 批准了该药物，但限制使用和分销以确保安全使用。

A black box warning has implications for the manufacturer, health care provider, and patient. 黑框警告对制造商、医疗保健提供者和患者都有影响。Manufacturer implications include a restriction on the degree of advertising, a potentially negative impact on sales, decreased use of the drug, and an increased risk of litigation. 制造商的影响包括广告力度的限制、对销售的潜在负面影响、药物使用的减少以及诉讼风险的增加。From the provider and patient perspective, the substitution of a drug without a black box warning may actually entail greater expense and exposure to another set of side effects than the use of the drug with a black box warning. 从提供者和患者的角度来看，与使用带有黑框警告的药物相比，替代没有黑框警告的药物实际上可能会带来更大的费用并面临另一组副作用。Further, in the absence of patient awareness of the potential dangers of a drug, when untoward events are precipitated by the drug, there is also an increased risk of litigation. 此外，在患者缺乏对药物潜在危险的认识的情况下，当药物引发意外不良事件时，诉讼的风险也会增加。

3
Recent Major Changes, Indications and Usage, Dosage and Administration, Dosage Forms and Strengths最近的主要变化、适应症和用途、剂量和给药方法、剂型和优点

The "Recent Major Changes" section lists only major changes in the boxed warning, indications and usage, dosage and administration, contraindications and warnings, and precautions sections. “最近的主要变化”部分仅列出了黑框警告、适应症和用法、剂量和给药方法、禁忌症和警告以及注意事项部分的主要变化。The three sections following Recent Major Changes are practical information giving indications and usage, dosage and administration, and dosage forms and strengths. 最近主要变化之后的三个部分是实用信息，提供适应症和用法、剂量和给药方法以及剂型和强度。These informational sections placed after the most serious warning issues have been identified to facilitate practical use of the label, whose major purpose is to provide dosage information for routine use. 在确定了最严重的警告问题之后，再放置这些信息部分，以方便标签的实际使用，标签的主要目的是提供日常使用的剂量信息。

The sections following the routine use information are warning or risk information of high importance. 常规使用信息后面的部分是非常重要的警告或风险信息。 These sections include contraindications, warnings and precautions, adverse reactions, drug interactions, and use in specific populations. 这些部分包括禁忌症、警告和注意事项、不良反应、药物相互作用以及特定人群的使用。

Contraindications禁忌症

A drug is classified as contraindicated in the clinical situation for which the risks outweigh any possible therapeutic benefit of the drug. 在临床情况下，一种药物被归类为禁忌药物，其风险超过了该药物任何可能的治疗益处。 Only known hazards, and not theoretical possibilities, can be listed. 只能列出已知的危险，而不是理论上的可能性。 If there are no known contraindications for a drug, "none" must be designated in this section. 如果药物没有已知的禁忌症，则本节中必须指定“无”。 The order in which the contraindications are presented in the text reflects the relative public health risk. 文中禁忌症的排列顺序反映了相对的公共卫生风险。The significance of the contraindications is based on the likelihood of occurrence and the size of the population potentially affected. 禁忌症的重要性取决于发生的可能性和可能受影响的人群的规模。

Warnings and Precautions警告和注意事项

When an adverse reaction is considered clinically significant, or when the reaction risk is serious, it will be included in the "Warning and Precautions" section. 当不良反应被认为有临床意义，或反应风险严重时，将纳入“警告和注意事项”部分。 There must be reasonable evidence of a causal relationship between the drug and the reaction. 必须有合理的证据证明药物与反应之间存在因果关系。 The order of the list of adverse reactions (ADRs) reflects their relative public health significance. 不良反应 (ADR) 列表的顺序反映了其相对的公共卫生重要性。The relative seriousness of the reaction and the ability to prevent or mitigate its occurrence are prioritized in this section. 本节优先考虑反应的相对严重性以及预防或减轻其发生的能力。A description of the reaction and outcome, including time to resolution, significant sequelae, estimated risk of ADR, and discussion of any known risk factors for the reaction, are required. 需要对反应和结果进行描述，包括解决时间、重大后遗症、预计的 ADR 风险以及任何已知的反应风险因素的讨论。 Treatment or management strategies for the ADR and discussion of any possible steps to reduce the risk, shorten the duration, or minimize the severity of a reaction are included in this section. 本节包括 ADR 的治疗或管理策略，以及降低风险、缩短持续时间或尽量减轻反应严重程度的任何可能步骤的讨论。

Observed ADRs and expected ADRs are included in the Warnings and Precautions section. 观察到的 ADR 和预期的 ADR 包含在“警告和注意事项”部分中。Observed ADRs are those events that have been observed in association with use of the drug and that are serious or are otherwise clinically significant. 观察到的 ADR 是指观察到的与药物使用相关的严重事件或具有其他临床意义的事件。 "Clinically significant" means that the ADR may require: adjustment of drug dosage or regimen, discontinuation of the drug, supplement treatment with an additional drug, appropriate patient selection to avoid the ADR, avoidance of concomitant therapy which triggers the ADR, evaluation of the patient for medication compliance, and use of alternative laboratory tests. “具有临床意义”是指 ADR 可能需要：调整药物剂量或治疗方案（疗程）、停药、用其他药物补充治疗、选择适当的患者以避免 ADR、避免引发 ADR 的伴随治疗、评估患者的用药依从性以及使用替代实验室测试。

Expected ADRs are events that can be anticipated to occur with a drug, based on observations from other members of the drug class or animal studies. 预期 ADR 是根据药物类别其他成员或动物研究的观察结果，预计药物可能发生的事件。Expected ADRs are appropriate for warnings and precautions if the reaction is clinically serious, indicating that it could have an outcome of death, life-threatening illness, or require hospitalization to treat. 如果反应在临床上严重，表明可能导致死亡、危及生命的疾病或需要住院治疗，则预期 ADR 适合警告和预防措施。

The New Drug Package Insert-Implications for Patient Safety

The package insert contains detailed drug information compiled and distributed by the drug manufacturer after FDA review and approval. 药品说明书包含药品生产商经过 FDA 审查和批准后编制和分发的详细药品信息。The purpose of the package insert is to provide complete and unbiased prescribing and safety information to health professionals. 说明书的目的是向卫生专业人员提供完整且公正的处方和安全信息。In 1968, a two-line warning placed on the isoproterenol inhaler package is considered the first patient package insert. 1968 年，异丙肾上腺素吸入器包装上放置的两行警告被认为是第一个患者包装说明书。 Although in 1970 the FDA mandated that a separate patient package insert detailing risk and benefits accompany each package of birth control pills, it was not until 1979 that the FDA promulgated the content and format of physician prescribing information inserts (also known as the package insert). 尽管在 1970 年 FDA 规定每个避孕药包装必须附有一份单独的患者说明书，详细说明每个避孕药包装的风险和益处，但直到 1979 年 FDA 才颁布了医生处方信息说明书（也称为包装说明书）的内容和格式。 Within the Warnings section of this document, the term "boxed warning" (black box warning) was used for the first time in FDA labeling requirements. 在本文件的警告部分中，术语“黑框警告”（黑框警告）首次在 FDA 标签要求中使用。Since then, the volume and detail of the package insert has increased significantly. 从那时起，包装说明书的体积和细节显着增加。In the 1980s, the FDA acknowledged that the information included in the package insert had become so lengthy, detailed, and complex that it was difficult for health practitioners to find specific information and to distinguish critical information from less important issues. 在 20 世纪 80 年代，FDA承认包装说明书中包含的信息变得如此冗长、详细和复杂，以至于健康从业者很难找到具体信息并将关键信息与不太重要的问题区分开来。

To address the problem, the FDA conducted research to assess how prescription drug labeling was used by health care practitioners, and to determine which labeling information was considered the most important. 为了解决这个问题，FDA 进行了一项研究，评估医疗保健从业人员如何使用处方药标签，并确定哪些标签信息被认为是最重要的。The studies documented that many practitioners usually find the information they need, but that the process was often time-intensive and clinically inefficient. 研究表明，许多从业者通常都能找到他们需要的信息，但这个过程往往耗时且临床效率低下。In addition, the package insert format disproportionately stressed the occurrence of extremely rare clinical events. 此外，药品说明书格式过分强调了极其罕见的临床事件。As a result, the FDA developed a new package insert format that has three major sections: (1)"The Highlights of Prescribing Information"; (2)"Full Prescribing Information Table of Contents"; and (3)"The Full Prescribing Information"(FPI). 因此，FDA 开发了一种新的包装说明书格式，其中包含三个主要内容：（1）“处方信息要点”； (2)《完整处方信息目录》； (3)“完整处方信息”(FPI)。This new organization of information improves access to critical information and makes the label more user-friendly. 这种新的信息组织方式改善了对关键信息的访问，并使标签更加用户友好。The new label format proposal was issued in December 2000 and, after public meetings and comment by practitioners, a final version became official in June 2006. 新的标签格式提案于 2000 年 12 月发布，经过公开会议和征求意见后经过实践者的努力，最终版本于 2006 年 6 月正式发布。A transition to the new format will not be mandatory for drugs that received FDA approval more than 5 years before the final ruling in June 2006. 对于 2006 年 6 月最终裁决之前 5 年以上获得 FDA 批准的药物，不强制要求过渡到新格式。However, pharmaceutical companies may elect to reformat the package insert for these older drugs. 然而，制药公司可能会选择重新格式化这些旧药物的包装说明书。Drugs approved within the 5-year window must resubmit the package insert in the revised label format during a 3~7-year phase－in period to comply with the new FDA standards. 在5年窗口内批准的药物必须在3~7年的阶段内以修订后的标签格式重新提交说明书，以符合新的FDA标准。Here list some sections selected in the new drug package insert. 这里列出了新药说明书中选取的部分章节。

Black Box Warning黑匣子警告

The black box warning is set apart as the most prominent information included in a product insert. 黑框警告被设置为产品说明书中最突出的信息。Any warning elevated to the status of a black box warning must be bolded (only the heading must be in all capitals, not the text of the warning) and "boxed" by a solid black line on all four sides. 任何提升为黑框警告状态的警告都必须加粗（只有标题必须全部大写，而不是警告文本），并在所有四个边上用黑色实线“装箱”。A black box warning is indicated in the following three situations, but may be used in other situations to highlight warning information that is particularly important to the prescriber: 在以下三种情况下会显示黑框警告，但也可在其他情况下用于突出显示对处方者特别重要的警告信息：

英译汉

In the case of systemically acting drugs (i.e. drugs that reach these sites via the systemic circulation), it is generally accepted for clinical purposes that a dynamic equilibrium exists between the concentration of drug at its site(s) of action and the concentration of drug in blood plasma.

对于全身作用药物(即通过体循环到达这些部位的药物)，临床上普遍接受的观点是，作用部位的药物浓度和血浆中的药物浓度之间存在动态平衡。

An important consequence of this dynamic equilibrium is that it permits a therapeutically effective concentration of drug to be achieved at its site(s) of action by adjustment of the concentration of drug in blood plasma.

这种动态平衡的一个重要结果是，它允许通过调整血浆中的药物浓度，在作用部位达到治疗有效的药物浓度。

Strictly speaking, the concentration of drug in plasma water (i.e. protein-free plasma) is a more accurate index of drug concentration at the site(s) of action than the concentration of drug in whole plasma since a drug may often bind in a reversible manner to plasma protein.

严格地说，血浆水(即无蛋白质血浆)中的药物浓度是作用部位药物浓度的更准确的指标，而全血浆中的药物浓度则不是这样，因为药物通常可与血浆蛋白以可逆的方式结合。 只有未结合的药物(即溶解于血浆水)才能通过毛细血管内皮从血浆中通过，到达其他体液和组织，从而到达作用部位。

Only drug which is unbound (i.e. dissolved in plasma water) can pass out of the plasma through the capillary endothelium and reach other body fluids and tissues and hence its site(s) of action.

只有未结合的药物(即溶解于血浆水)才能通过毛细血管内皮从血浆中通过，到达其他体液和组织，从而到达作用部位。

Drug concentration in whole blood is also not considered to be an accurate indirect index of the concentration of drug at its site(s) of action since drug can bind to and enter blood cells.

全血中的药物浓度也不能被认为是作用部位药物浓度的准确的间接指标，因为药物可以与血细胞结合并进入血细胞。

However, to measure the concentration of an unbound drug in plasma water requires more complex and sensitive assay methods than to measure the total concentration of both unbound and bound drug in total plasma.

然而，测量血浆中非结合药物的浓度比测量血浆中结合和非结合药物的总浓度需要更复杂和更灵敏的分析方法。

Thus, for clinical purposes, drug concentration in blood plasma is usually measured and is regarded as an index of drug concentration at the site(s) of action of the drug and of the clinical effects of the drug.

因此，出于临床目的，通常测量血浆中的药物浓度，并将其视为药物作用部位的药物浓度和药物临床效果的指标。

However, it should be realized that this is a simplification and may not always be valid.

然而，应该认识到这是一个简化，可能并不总是有效的。

Indeed one should not draw inferences about the clinical effects of a drug from its plasma concentration until it has been established that the two are consistently correlated.

事实上，在确定两者之间始终相关之前，不应从血浆浓度推断药物的临床效果。

It has been assumed that the plasma drug concentration is directly proportional to the clinical effect of that drug.

人们假定血浆药物浓度与该药物的临床效果成正比。

The concentration of drug in blood plasma depends on numerous factors.

血浆中药物的浓度取决于许多因素。

These include the relative amount of an administered dose that enters the systemic circulation, the rate at which this occurs, the rate and extent of distribution of the drug between the systemic circulation and other tissues and fluids and the rate of elimination of the drug from the body.

这些包括给药剂量进入全身循环的相对数量，以及其发生的速率，药物在全身循环和其他组织和液体之间分布的速率和程度，以及药物从体内排出的速率。除了静脉注射途径外，在所有其他用于给予系统作用药物的途径中，都涉及将药物从给予部位吸收到血液中。

Apart from the intravenous route of drug administration, where a drug is introduced directly into the blood circulation, all other routes of administering systemically acting drugs involve the absorption of drug from the place of administration into the blood.

除了静脉途径的药物给予方式，即将药物直接引入血液循环外，所有其他给予全身作用药物的途径都涉及从给药部位吸收药物进入血液。

Drug must be absorbed in a sufficient quantity and at a sufficient rate in order to achieve a certain blood plasma concentration which, in turn, will produce an appropriate concentration of drug at its site(s) of action to elicit the desired therapeutic response.

药物必须以足够的量和足够的速度吸收，以达到一定的血浆浓度，而血浆浓度又会在作用部位产生适当的药物浓度，以引起期望的治疗反应

It follows that there are two aspects of drug absorption which are important in clinical practice, namely, the rate and the extent to which the administered dose is absorbed.

由此可见，药物吸收有两个方面在临床实践中很重要，即给药剂量的吸收速率和吸收程度。

Simply because a certain dose of a drug is administered to a patient, there is no guarantee (except for intravenous administration) that all of that dose will reach the systemic circulation.

仅仅因为给病人注射了一定剂量的药物，就不能保证(静脉注射除外)所有的剂量都能进入体循环。

The fraction of an administered dose of drug that reaches the systemic circulation in unchanged form is known as the bioavailable dose.

给药剂量中以不变形式进入体循环的部分称为生物有效剂量。

The relative amount of an administered dose of a particular drug which reaches the systemic circulation intact and the rate at which this occurs is known as the bioavailability of that drug.

一种特定药物在每次给药剂量中能够完整地进入体循环的相对量以及这种情况发生的速率被称为该药物的生物利用度。

Bioavailability is thus concerned with the quantity and the rate at which the intact form of a particular drug appears in the systemic circulation following administration of that drug.

因此，生物利用度与给药后某一特定药物的完整形态在体循环中出现的数量和速率有关。

The bioavailability exhibited by a drug is thus very important in determining whether a therapeutically effective concentration is achieved at the site(s) of action of the drug.

因此，药物所表现出的生物利用度对于决定药物作用部位是否达到治疗有效浓度是非常重要的

In defining bioavailability in these terms it is assumed that intact drug is the therapeutically active form of the drug.

在这些术语中定义生物利用度时，假定完整的药物是药物的治疗活性形式。

This definition of bioavailability would not be valid in the case of a pro-drug, whose therapeutic action normally depends on it being converted into its therapeutically active form prior to or on reaching the systemic circulation.

这种生物利用度的定义在前药的情况下是无效的，前药的治疗作用通常取决于它在进入体循环之前或之后转化为治疗活性形式。

It should also be noted that in the context of bioavailability, the term systemic circulation refers primarily to venous blood (excluding the hepatic portal blood during the absorption phase) and arterial blood which carries the intact drug to the tissues.

还应注意，在生物利用度方面，“体循环”一词主要指静脉血(不包括吸收阶段的肝门静脉血)和动脉血，它们将完整的药物运送到组织中。

Hence according to the definition of bioavailability, an administered dose of a particular drug in an oral dosage form will be 100% bioavailable only if the drug is completely released from the dosage form into solution in the gastrointestinal fluids.

因此，根据生物利用度的定义，口服剂型中特定药物的给药剂量只有在药物完全从剂型释放到胃肠道液体中的溶液中时才具有100%的生物利用度。

The released drug must also be completely stable in solution in the gastrointestinal fluids and all of the drug must pass through the gastrointestinal barrier into the mesenteric circulation without being metabolized.

释放的药物在胃肠道液体中必须是完全稳定的，并且所有的药物必须通过胃肠道屏障进入肠系膜循环而不被代谢。

Finally, all of the absorbed drug must pass into the systemic circulation without being metabolized on passing through the liver.

最后，所有被吸收的药物必须进入体循环而不经肝脏代谢

Thus any factor which adversely affects either the release of the drug from the dosage form, its dissolution in the gastrointestinal fluids, its stability in the gastrointestinal fluids, its permeation through and stability in the gastrointestinal barrier or its stability in the hepatic portal circulation will influence the bioavailability exhibited by that drug from the dosage form in which it was administered.

因此，任何不利影响药物从剂型释放、在胃肠液中的溶解、在胃肠液中的稳定性、在胃肠屏障中的渗透性和稳定性或在肝门静脉循环中的稳定性的因素，都会影响药物从给药剂型中所表现出的生物利用度

Many factors have been found to influence the time course of a drug in the plasma and hence at its site(s) of action.（我们发现很多因素都可以影响药物在血浆中和作用部位的经时过程。）

② These include the foods eaten by the patient, the effect of the disease state on drug absorption, the age of the patient, the site(s) of absorption of the administered drug, the coadministration of other drugs, the physical and chemical properties of the administered drug, the type of dosage form, the composition and method of manufacture of the dosage form and the size of dose and frequency of administration of the dosage form.

（包括病人吃的食物、疾病状况对药物的吸收影响、病人的年龄、所给药物的吸收部位、所服用的其他药物、所给药物的理化性质、剂型、成分、剂型制备方法、剂量大小和服用频率。）

Thus, a given drug may exhibit differences in its bioavailability if it is administered in the same type of dosage form by different routes of administration, e.g. an aqueous solution of a given drug administered by the oral and intramuscular routes of administration.

（因此，一种药物的同一剂型会因给药途径的不同面呈现不同的生物利用度，例如，药物的水溶液可以通过口服和肌肉途径给药。）

② A given drug may also show differences in its bioavailability from one type of dosage form to another when given by the same route, e.g. a tablet, a hard gelatin capsule and an aqueous suspension administered by the peroral route.

（药物的不同剂型也会通过同一种给药途径呈现不同的生物利用度，例如，经口服用片剂、硬胶囊剂和水溶性混悬剂。）

③ A given drug might show different bioavailabilities from different formulations of the same type of dosage form given by the same route of administration, e.g. different formulations of an aqueous suspension of a given drug administered by the peroral route.

（药物同一剂型的不同处方通过同一给药途径可能呈现不同的生物利用度，例如某一药物不同处方的水溶性混悬剂通过口服给药。）

Variability in the bioavailability exhibited by a given drug from different formulations of the same type of dosage form or from different types of dosage forms etc., can cause patients to be under-or overmedicated.

同一剂型的不同制剂或不同剂型等的给定药物所表现出的生物利用度的差异可导致患者用药不足或过量。

The result may be therapeutic failure or serious adverse effects particularly in the case of drugs which have a narrow therapeutic range.

结果可能是治疗失败或严重的不良反应，特别是在治疗范围较窄的药物中。

The entry of a drug into the systemic circulation following the administration of a drug product usually involves:

服药后药物进入体循环通常包括:

the release of the drug from its dosage form into solution in the biological fluids at the absorption site, and the movement of the dissolved drug across biological membranes into the systemic circulation.

药物从其剂型释放到吸收部位的生物液体溶液中，溶解的药物穿过生物膜进入体循环。

The study of the various factors which can affect these processes and the application of this knowledge to obtain the expected therapeutic effect from a drug product when it is used by a patient are known as biopharmaceutics.

对影响这些过程的各种因素的研究，以及在患者使用药品时应用这些知识以获得预期治疗效果的研究，被称为生物药剂学。

现在普遍认为，血浆药物浓度与该药的临床效果有直接的比例关系。

14
The concentration of drug in blood plasma depends on numerous factors.

血浆中的药物浓度取决于多种因素。

15
These include the relative amount of an administered dose (that enters the systemic circulation), the rate at (which this occurs,) the rate and extent of distribution of the drug between the systemic circulation and other tissues and fluids and the rate of elimination of the drug from the body.

包括所用药物进入体循环的相对的量、吸收的速度、体循环、其他组织及体液之间的分布的速度和程度，以及药物从体内排出的速度。

16
Apart from the intravenous route of drug administration, (where a drug is introduced directly into the blood circulation), all other routes of administering systemically acting drugs involve the absorption of drug from the place of administration into the blood.

除了静脉注射这一给药途径的药物可以直接进入血液循环外，所有其他具有全身作用的药物的给药途径都涉及将药物从给药部位吸收进血液的过程。

Drug must be absorbed in a sufficient quantity and at a sufficient rate in order to achieve a certain blood plasma concentration （which, in turn, will produce an appropriate concentration of drug at its site(s) of action to elicit the desired therapeutic response）.

药物必须以足够的剂量和足够的速率被吸收，以达到一定的血浆浓度，进而使药物在作用位点达到适当的浓度以实现预期的治疗效果。

18
It follows （that there are two aspects of drug absorption （which are important in clinical practice, namely, the rate and the extent to which the administered dose is absorbed.））

药物吸收在临床实践中具有两方面的重要意义，即所给予药物被机体吸收的速率和程度。

19
Simply 【because a certain dose of a drug is administered to a patient】, there is no guarantee (except for intravenous administration) （that all of that dose will reach the systemic circulation.）

仅给于患者给予一定剂量的药物，并不能确保(除了静脉给药)所有药物都进入体循环。

20
The fraction of an administered dose of drug （that reaches the systemic circulation in unchanged form） is known as the bioavailable dose.

以原形进入体循环的药物的剂量被称为生物可利用的剂量。

21
The relative amount of an administered dose of a particular drug （which reaches the systemic circulation intact） and the rate at （which this occurs） is known as the bioavailability of that drug.

药物的生物利用度被定义为该种药物以原形进入体循环的相对量及速率。

22
Bioavailability is thus concerned with the quantity and the rate at 【which the intact form of a particular drug appears in the systemic circulation following administration of that drug.】

生物利用度与给药后以药物原形进入体循环的药物的剂量和速率相关。

23
The bioavailability exhibited by a drug is thus very important in determining whether a therapeutically effective concentration is achieved at the site(s) of action of the drug.

因此，药物的生物利用度对确定药物在作用部位是否达到有效的治疗浓度非常重要。

24
In defining bioavailability in these terms it is assumed that intact drug is the therapeutically active form of the drug.

在定义生物利用度时，假定完整的药物是具有治疗活性的药物形式。

25
This definition of bioavailability would not be valid in the case of a pro-drug, whose therapeutic action normally depends on it being converted into its therapeutically active form prior to or on reaching the systemic circulation.

生物利用度的这一定义不适用于原形药物，因为原形药物的治疗作用通常取决于药物在进入全身循环之前或之后转化为其治疗活性形式。

26
It should also be noted （that in the context of bioavailability, the term systemic circulation refers primarily to venous blood (excluding the hepatic portal blood during the absorption phase) and arterial blood （which carries the intact drug to the tissues.））

还应注意到，在讲生物利用度时，体循环主要是指静脉血(不包括吸收过程中的肝门血流)和将完整药物运到组织的动脉血流。

27
Hence according to the definition of bioavailability, an administered dose of a particular drug in an oral dosage form will be 100% bioavailable 【only if the drug is completely released from the dosage form into solution in the gastrointestinal fluids.】

因此，根据生物利用度的定义，只有当药物被完全从其剂型释放到胃肠液中，这种药物以口服形式服用的剂量才可以被百分之百地生物利用。

28

释放的药物还必须在胃肠液的溶液中完全稳定，并且所有药物必须通过胃肠屏障进入肠系膜循环而不被代谢。

29
Finally, all of the absorbed drug must pass into the systemic circulation without being metabolized on passing through the liver.

最后，所有被吸收的药物都必须进入全身循环，而不经过肝脏代谢

30
Thus any factor which adversely affects either the release of the drug from the dosage form, its dissolution in the gastrointestinal fluids, its stability in the gastrointestinal fluids, its permeation through and stability in the gastrointestinal barrier or its stability in the hepatic portal circulation will influence the bioavailability exhibited by that drug from the dosage form in which it was administered.

因此，任何对药物从剂型中的释放、药物在胃肠液中的溶解、药物在胃肠液中的稳定性、药物在胃肠屏障中的渗透和稳定性或药物在肝门静脉循环中的稳定性产生不利影响的因素，都会影响药物从给药剂型中的生物利用度。

31
Many factors have been found to influence the time course of a drug in the plasma and hence at its site(s) of action.

我们发现很多因素都可以影响药物在血浆中和作用部位的经时过程。

32
These include the foods eaten by the patient, the effect of the disease state on drug absorption, the age of the patient, the site(s) of absorption of the administered drug, the coadministration of other drugs, the physical and chemical properties of the administered drug, the type of dosage form, the composition and method of manufacture of the dosage form and the size of dose and frequency of administration of the dosage form.包括病人吃的食物、疾病状况对药物的吸收影响、病人的年龄、所给药物的吸收部位、所服用的其他药物、所给药物的理化性质、剂型、成分、剂型制备方法、剂量大小和服用频率。
33
Thus, a given drug may exhibit differences in its bioavailability if it is administered in the same type of dosage form by different routes of administration, e.g. an aqueous solution of a given drug administered by the oral and intramuscular routes of administration.因此，如果一种给定药物以不同的给药途径以相同类型的剂型给药，则该给药药物的生物利用度可能存在差异
34
A given drug may also show differences in its bioavailability from one type of dosage form to another when given by the same route, e.g. a tablet, a hard gelatin capsule and an aqueous suspension administered by the peroral route.以相同途径给药时，给定药物的生物利用度也可能不同，例如经口服途径给药片、硬明胶胶囊和水悬液。
35
A given drug might show different bioavailabilities from different formulations of the same type of dosage form given by the same route of administration, e.g. different formulations of an aqueous suspension of a given drug administered by the peroral route.一种给药药物可能具有不同的给药途径给药的不同配方的生物利用度，例如给药的水悬液的不同配方。
36
Variability in the bioavailability exhibited by a given drug from different formulations of the same type of dosage form or from different types of dosage forms etc., can cause patients to be under-or overmedicated.来自同一类型剂型的不同配方或来自不同类型剂型等的给定药物所表现出的生物利用度的可变性，可导致患者用药不足或过量。
37
The result may be therapeutic failure or serious adverse effects particularly in the case of drugs which have a narrow therapeutic range.其结果可能是治疗失败或严重的不良反应，特别是在药物的治疗范围狭窄的情况下。
38
The entry of a drug into the systemic circulation following the administration of a drug product usually involves:the release of the drug from its dosage form into solution in the biological fluids at the absorption site, and the movement of the dissolved drug across biological membranes into the systemic circulation.药物进入体循环后的药物产品通常包括：药物从其剂型释放到溶液的生物液体吸收网站，和溶解药物的运动通过生物膜进入体循环。
39
The study of the various factors which can affect these processes and the application of this knowledge to obtain the expected therapeutic effect from a drug product when it is used by a patient are known as biopharmaceutics.对可能影响这些过程的各种因素的研究，以及应用这些知识来获得当一个被患者使用的药物产品时的预期治疗效果，被称为生物药剂。

06补充练习

The package insert contains detailed drug information compiled and distributed by the drug manufacturer after FDA review and approval. 药品说明书包含药品生产商经过 FDA 审查和批准后编制和分发的详细药品信息。The purpose of the package insert is to provide complete and unbiased prescribing and safety information to health professionals. 说明书的目的是向卫生专业人员提供完整且公正的处方和安全信息。In 1968, a two-line warning placed on the isoproterenol inhaler package is considered the first patient package insert. 1968 年，异丙肾上腺素吸入器包装上放置的两行警告被认为是第一个患者包装说明书。 Although in 1970 the FDA mandated that a separate patient package insert detailing risk and benefits accompany each package of birth control pills, it was not until 1979 that the FDA promulgated the content and format of physician prescribing information inserts (also known as the package insert). 尽管在 1970 年 FDA 规定每个避孕药包装必须附有一份单独的患者说明书，详细说明每个避孕药包装的风险和益处，但直到 1979 年 FDA 才颁布了医生处方信息说明书（也称为包装说明书）的内容和格式。 Within the Warnings section of this document, the term "boxed warning" (black box warning) was used for the first time in FDA labeling requirements. 在本文件的警告部分中，术语“黑框警告”（黑框警告）首次在 FDA 标签要求中使用。Since then, the volume and detail of the package insert has increased significantly. 从那时起，包装说明书的体积和细节显着增加。In the 1980s, the FDA acknowledged that the information included in the package insert had become so lengthy, detailed, and complex that it was difficult for health practitioners to find specific information and to distinguish critical information from less important issues. 在 20 世纪 80 年代，FDA承认包装说明书中包含的信息变得如此冗长、详细和复杂，以至于健康从业者很难找到具体信息并将关键信息与不太重要的问题区分开来。

To address the problem, the FDA conducted research to assess how prescription drug labeling was used by health care practitioners, and to determine which labeling information was considered the most important. 为了解决这个问题，FDA 进行了一项研究，评估医疗保健从业人员如何使用处方药标签，并确定哪些标签信息被认为是最重要的。The studies documented that many practitioners usually find the information they need, but that the process was often time-intensive and clinically inefficient. 研究表明，许多从业者通常都能找到他们需要的信息，但这个过程往往耗时且临床效率低下。In addition, the package insert format disproportionately stressed the occurrence of extremely rare clinical events. 此外，药品说明书格式过分强调了极其罕见的临床事件。As a result, the FDA developed a new package insert format that has three major sections: (1)"The Highlights of Prescribing Information"; (2)"Full Prescribing Information Table of Contents"; and (3)"The Full Prescribing Information"(FPI). 因此，FDA 开发了一种新的包装说明书格式，其中包含三个主要内容：（1）“处方信息要点”； (2)《完整处方信息目录》； (3)“完整处方信息”(FPI)。This new organization of information improves access to critical information and makes the label more user-friendly. 这种新的信息组织方式改善了对关键信息的访问，并使标签更加用户友好。The new label format proposal was issued in December 2000 and, after public meetings and comment by practitioners, a final version became official in June 2006. 新的标签格式提案于 2000 年 12 月发布，经过公开会议和征求意见后经过实践者的努力，最终版本于 2006 年 6 月正式发布。A transition to the new format will not be mandatory for drugs that received FDA approval more than 5 years before the final ruling in June 2006. 对于 2006 年 6 月最终裁决之前 5 年以上获得 FDA 批准的药物，不强制要求过渡到新格式。However, pharmaceutical companies may elect to reformat the package insert for these older drugs. 然而，制药公司可能会选择重新格式化这些旧药物的包装说明书。Drugs approved within the 5-year window must resubmit the package insert in the revised label format during a 3~7-year phase－in period to comply with the new FDA standards. 在5年窗口内批准的药物必须在3~7年的阶段内以修订后的标签格式重新提交说明书，以符合新的FDA标准。Here list some sections selected in the new drug package insert. 这里列出了新药说明书中选取的部分章节。

1
Highlights of Prescribing Information处方信息要点

Most of the information included in the "Highlights of Prescribing Information" section communicates risks or warning information. “处方信息要点”部分中包含的大部分信息传达风险或警告信息。A succinct summary of critical clinical information is presented in a bulleted format that is cross-referenced to the FPI section for more in depth explanation. 关键临床信息的简洁摘要以项目符号格式呈现，与 FPI 部分交叉引用以获得更深入的解释。The organization of the section reflects the priorities and most common patterns of product insert usage as expressed by practitioners during FDA reformatting studies. 该部分的组织反映了从业人员在 FDA 重新格式化研究期间表达的产品说明书使用的优先事项和最常见模式。

2
Black Box Warning黑匣子警告

The black box warning is set apart as the most prominent information included in a product insert. 黑框警告被设置为产品说明书中最突出的信息。Any warning elevated to the status of a black box warning must be bolded (only the heading must be in all capitals, not the text of the warning) and "boxed" by a solid black line on all four sides. 任何提升为黑框警告状态的警告都必须加粗（只有标题必须全部大写，而不是警告文本），并在所有四个边上用黑色实线“装箱”。A black box warning is indicated in the following three situations, but may be used in other situations to highlight warning information that is particularly important to the prescriber: 在以下三种情况下会显示黑框警告，但也可在其他情况下用于突出显示对处方者特别重要的警告信息：

(1) There is an adverse reaction so serious in proportion to the potential benefit from the drug that it is essential that it be considered in assessing the risks and benefits of using the drug. 与该药物的潜在益处相比，不良反应非常严重，因此在评估使用该药物的风险和益处时必须考虑该不良反应。This includes potentially life threatening or permanently disabling adverse reactions. 这包括可能危及生命或永久致残的不良反应。

(2) There is a serious reaction that can be prevented or reduced in frequency or severity by patient selection, careful monitoring, avoiding certain concomitant therapy, addition of another drug or managing patient in a specific manner, or avoiding use in a specific clinical situation. 通过选择患者、仔细监测、避免某些伴随治疗、添加另一种药物或以特定方式管理患者，或避免在特定临床情况下使用，可以预防严重反应或减少其发生频率或严重程度。

(3) FDA approved the drug with restrictions on use and distribution to assure safe use. FDA 批准了该药物，但限制使用和分销以确保安全使用。

A black box warning has implications for the manufacturer, health care provider, and patient. 黑框警告对制造商、医疗保健提供者和患者都有影响。Manufacturer implications include a restriction on the degree of advertising, a potentially negative impact on sales, decreased use of the drug, and an increased risk of litigation. 制造商的影响包括广告力度的限制、对销售的潜在负面影响、药物使用的减少以及诉讼风险的增加。From the provider and patient perspective, the substitution of a drug without a black box warning may actually entail greater expense and exposure to another set of side effects than the use of the drug with a black box warning. 从提供者和患者的角度来看，与使用带有黑框警告的药物相比，替代没有黑框警告的药物实际上可能会带来更大的费用并面临另一组副作用。Further, in the absence of patient awareness of the potential dangers of a drug, when untoward events are precipitated by the drug, there is also an increased risk of litigation. 此外，在患者缺乏对药物潜在危险的认识的情况下，当药物引发意外不良事件时，诉讼的风险也会增加。

3
Recent Major Changes, Indications and Usage, Dosage and Administration, Dosage Forms and Strengths最近的主要变化、适应症和用途、剂量和给药方法、剂型和优点

The "Recent Major Changes" section lists only major changes in the boxed warning, indications and usage, dosage and administration, contraindications and warnings, and precautions sections. “最近的主要变化”部分仅列出了黑框警告、适应症和用法、剂量和给药方法、禁忌症和警告以及注意事项部分的主要变化。The three sections following Recent Major Changes are practical information giving indications and usage, dosage and administration, and dosage forms and strengths. 最近主要变化之后的三个部分是实用信息，提供适应症和用法、剂量和给药方法以及剂型和强度。These informational sections placed after the most serious warning issues have been identified to facilitate practical use of the label, whose major purpose is to provide dosage information for routine use. 在确定了最严重的警告问题之后，再放置这些信息部分，以方便标签的实际使用，标签的主要目的是提供日常使用的剂量信息。

The sections following the routine use information are warning or risk information of high importance. 常规使用信息后面的部分是非常重要的警告或风险信息。 These sections include contraindications, warnings and precautions, adverse reactions, drug interactions, and use in specific populations. 这些部分包括禁忌症、警告和注意事项、不良反应、药物相互作用以及特定人群的使用。

4
Contraindications禁忌症

A drug is classified as contraindicated in the clinical situation for which the risks outweigh any possible therapeutic benefit of the drug. 在临床情况下，一种药物被归类为禁忌药物，其风险超过了该药物任何可能的治疗益处。 Only known hazards, and not theoretical possibilities, can be listed. 只能列出已知的危险，而不是理论上的可能性。 If there are no known contraindications for a drug, "none" must be designated in this section. 如果药物没有已知的禁忌症，则本节中必须指定“无”。 The order in which the contraindications are presented in the text reflects the relative public health risk. 文中禁忌症的排列顺序反映了相对的公共卫生风险。The significance of the contraindications is based on the likelihood of occurrence and the size of the population potentially affected. 禁忌症的重要性取决于发生的可能性和可能受影响的人群的规模。

5
Warnings and Precautions警告和注意事项

When an adverse reaction is considered clinically significant, or when the reaction risk is serious, it will be included in the "Warning and Precautions" section. 当不良反应被认为有临床意义，或反应风险严重时，将纳入“警告和注意事项”部分。 There must be reasonable evidence of a causal relationship between the drug and the reaction. 必须有合理的证据证明药物与反应之间存在因果关系。 The order of the list of adverse reactions (ADRs) reflects their relative public health significance. 不良反应 (ADR) 列表的顺序反映了其相对的公共卫生重要性。The relative seriousness of the reaction and the ability to prevent or mitigate its occurrence are prioritized in this section. 本节优先考虑反应的相对严重性以及预防或减轻其发生的能力。A description of the reaction and outcome, including time to resolution, significant sequelae, estimated risk of ADR, and discussion of any known risk factors for the reaction, are required. 需要对反应和结果进行描述，包括解决时间、重大后遗症、预计的 ADR 风险以及任何已知的反应风险因素的讨论。 Treatment or management strategies for the ADR and discussion of any possible steps to reduce the risk, shorten the duration, or minimize the severity of a reaction are included in this section. 本节包括 ADR 的治疗或管理策略，以及降低风险、缩短持续时间或尽量减轻反应严重程度的任何可能步骤的讨论。

Observed ADRs and expected ADRs are included in the Warnings and Precautions section. 观察到的 ADR 和预期的 ADR 包含在“警告和注意事项”部分中。Observed ADRs are those events that have been observed in association with use of the drug and that are serious or are otherwise clinically significant. 观察到的 ADR 是指观察到的与药物使用相关的严重事件或具有其他临床意义的事件。 "Clinically significant" means that the ADR may require: adjustment of drug dosage or regimen, discontinuation of the drug, supplement treatment with an additional drug, appropriate patient selection to avoid the ADR, avoidance of concomitant therapy which triggers the ADR, evaluation of the patient for medication compliance, and use of alternative laboratory tests. “具有临床意义”是指 ADR 可能需要：调整药物剂量或治疗方案（疗程）、停药、用其他药物补充治疗、选择适当的患者以避免 ADR、避免引发 ADR 的伴随治疗、评估患者的用药依从性以及使用替代实验室测试。

Expected ADRs are events that can be anticipated to occur with a drug, based on observations from other members of the drug class or animal studies. 预期 ADR 是根据药物类别其他成员或动物研究的观察结果，预计药物可能发生的事件。Expected ADRs are appropriate for warnings and precautions if the reaction is clinically serious, indicating that it could have an outcome of death, life-threatening illness, or require hospitalization to treat. 如果反应在临床上严重，表明可能导致死亡、危及生命的疾病或需要住院治疗，则预期 ADR 适合警告和预防措施。

6
Drug Interactions药物相互作用

The "Drug Interactions" section includes concise information about the potential for interaction with other drugs or foods. “药物相互作用”部分包含有关与其他药物或食物相互作用的可能性的简明信息。These include both pharmacokinetic (e.g., food effects, enzyme induction and inhibition) and pharmacodynamic effects (e.g., meperidine with monoamine oxidase inhibitors). 这些包括药代动力学（例如食物效应、酶诱导和抑制）和药效学效应（例如哌替啶与单胺氧化酶抑制剂）。

Use in Specific Populations在特定人群中的使用

This section on "Use in Specific Populations" lists clinically significant or important differences in patient response or use of a drug in specific populations of patients. “在特定人群中使用”这一节列出了患者反应或药物在特定患者人群中使用的临床显着或重要差异。

FPI Contents FPI 内容

The purpose of the table of contents is to reference all the sections and subsections included in the FPI, some of which will not be cross-referenced in the Highlights. 目录的目的是引用 FPI 中包含的所有章节和小节，其中一些不会在要点中交叉引用。The Highlights contains cross-references to the FPI, which contains the full explanatory text for the bulleted summaries and is easily accessed by practitioners to encourage its use and discourage use of the Highlights section as the sole source of information. 《要点》中包含与《公众宣传手册》的交叉引用，《公众宣传手册》中包含对条目式摘要的解释性全文，便于从业人员查阅，从而鼓励使用《公众宣传手册》，并阻止将《要点》部分作为唯一的信息来源。The sections of the FPI coincide with the order of the sections covered in the Highlights section. FPI 的各部分与“要点”部分中涵盖的部分的顺序一致。Also, similar to the Highlights, the most crucial dosing and warning sections are at the beginning of the FPI text. 此外，与要点类似，最重要的剂量和警告部分位于 FPI 文本的开头。 The sections dealing with risk information are grouped together. 处理（涉及）风险信息的部分被分组在一起。The informational sections not dealing with risk are grouped collectively. 不涉及风险的信息部分被集中在一起。 Additional sections in this part of the package insert include drug abuse and dependence, over-dosage, description, clinical pharmacology, non-clinical toxicology, clinical studies, references, how supplied/storage and handling, and patient counseling information. 说明书这部分的其他部分包括药物滥用和依赖性、过量用药、描述、临床药理学、非临床毒理学、临床研究、参考文献、供应/储存和处理方式以及患者咨询信息。

Hospital-based medication errors and preventable adverse drug reactions occur at a rate of 400 000 per year according to a recent Institute of Medicine (IOM) study. 根据医学研究所 (IOM) 最近的一项研究，医院用药错误和可预防的药物不良反应每年发生 40 万起。 These errors are reported to translate into an annual cost of ＄3.5 billion in extra hospital expense. 据报道，这些错误每年会导致 35 亿美元的额外住院费用。The new format changes the landscape of drug information, and the FDA has expressed the hope that these changes would increase effective use of prescription of drugs and decrease medication errors. 新格式改变了药品信息的格局，FDA 表示希望这些变化能够提高药物处方的有效使用并减少用药错误。
