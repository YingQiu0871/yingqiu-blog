3. Drug discovery process：最该背的一条主线

Target identification / target choice

Lead generation

Hit identification

Hit to Lead (H2L)

Lead optimization

Preclinical studies

Clinical studies

Lead generation 最常考两条路：

HTS = High Throughput Screening：大规模化合物库快速筛选。

Rational drug design：已知靶点结构或已知结合信息后，利用 docking / virtual screening / design 来找 hits。

可能题目
Define HTS and rational drug design.
标准答案
HTS screens large libraries to identify initial hits. Rational drug design uses target knowledge or target structure to design or select compounds.
问可能题目：What is the difference between a hit and a lead?
答

标准答案：A hit is an initial active compound identified during screening. A lead is a more optimized compound with better potency and drug-like properties.

4. ADMET / BBB / logP / logD / selectivity：表格题的固定语言

ADMET = absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity。

HEK293T = human kidney cell line；HepG2 = human liver cell line。

BBB
能不能进脑；有时需要，有时过度进入反而增加 CNS side effects。
logP / logD
lipophilicity，影响 membrane permeation、absorption、distribution、metabolism、clearance、toxicity。
selectivity
越 selective，通常 off-target effects 越少，副作用越少。
问可能题目：Why were HEK293T and HepG2 tested?
答

标准答案：To evaluate cytotoxicity in human kidney and liver cells and estimate potential safety liabilities.

问可能题目：Why is BBB important in drug design?
答

标准答案：BBB penetration may be needed for CNS indications, but unnecessary BBB crossing may cause CNS side effects.

问可能题目：Why does logP/logD matter?
答

标准答案：Lipophilicity influences membrane permeability, absorption, distribution, metabolism, clearance and sometimes toxicity.

问可能题目：What does higher selectivity usually mean?
答

标准答案：Higher selectivity usually means fewer off-target effects and fewer side effects.

5. Natural Products 总论：来源、价值、为什么麻烦

5.1 Natural products 是什么

Natural products = 由 living organisms 产生的小分子化学物质，常具有明确的 pharmacological effects。

课件里强调它们来自 evolution 这个 diversity-generating machine，因此具有 privileged scaffolds、biochemical specificity、structural complexity 和 highly diversified chemical space。

问可能题目：What are natural products?
答

标准答案：Natural products are small chemical substances produced by living organisms, often with distinct biological or pharmacological effects.

5.2 Main sources of natural products（2023 直接考过）

Plants 植物

Microbes / microorganisms 微生物

Marine organisms 海洋生物

Animal sources 动物来源

Venoms and toxins 毒液与毒素

Arthropods / insects / spiders（可作为补充）

问可能题目：What are the main sources of natural products?
答

标准答案：The main sources are plants, microorganisms, marine organisms, animal sources, venoms and toxins.

5.3 为什么天然产物重要

结构新颖，chemical space coverage 高。

生物活性强，常常 already biologically validated by natural selection。

历史上贡献了大量重要药物
penicillin、quinine、artemisinin、vinblastine、taxol、ciclosporine、lovastatin、vancomycin、avermectins 等。
问可能题目：Why are natural products important in drug discovery?
答

标准答案：They provide structurally diverse and biologically active scaffolds that can be directly developed or optimized into drugs.

5.4 为什么工业界一度不爱做 NP-based discovery（2024 直接考）

1
Not compatible with fast HTS and industrial timelines。
2
Complex structures and many stereocenters make optimization difficult。
3
Natural extracts are complex mixtures and the active compound may be present in very small amounts。
4
Supply / manufacturing / scale-up difficulties。
5
High probability of rediscovering known compounds; patent issues。
6
Poor cultivability of many microorganisms。
7
Biodiversity / IP / access issues (Rio, Nagoya)。
8
Seasonal or environmental variation in living organisms。
可能题目
Give four reasons why pharmaceutical industry became reluctant to pursue NP-based drug discovery.
标准答案
Natural products were not well suited to fast HTS, often had very complex structures, were present in complex mixtures at low amounts, and caused supply/manufacturing or patent/access issues.

5.5 为什么后来又重新感兴趣

Advanced analytical tools：LC-MS-NMR。

Dereplication
更快识别已知 compounds，避免白做。
NP databases
DNP、MarineLit。

Genomics / metabolomics / post-genomic era tools。

Advanced culture techniques：iChip / in-situ cultivation。

Synthetic biology
解决 supply 问题。

Targeted therapies / ADCs：把 old toxic natural products 重新利用起来。

问可能题目：Why has interest in NP-based discovery returned?
答

标准答案：Because of better analytical tools, dereplication, databases, omics approaches, improved culture methods, synthetic biology and new therapeutic formats such as ADCs.

6. PPT 里最值得会的天然产物案例（这一节很重要）

6.1 Paclitaxel / Taxol

来源
Pacific yew（Taxus brevifolia）。
问题
天然含量极低，约 0.02%；供给困难。
解决思路
从 European yew（Taxus baccata）的 10-DAB 做 semisynthesis；也可 plant-cell fermentation。
问可能题目：Why was paclitaxel supply a challenge and how was it solved?
答

标准答案：Paclitaxel was present in very low amounts in Pacific yew, so industrial production relied on semisynthesis from 10-DAB and plant-cell fermentation.

6.2 Artemisinin

来源
Artemisia annua。
问题
全球需求大，总合成不划算。
解决思路
semisynthesis from artemisinic acid；进一步走向 synthetic biology。
问可能题目：Why was artemisinin supply challenging?
答

标准答案：Demand was high and total synthesis was not profitable, so semisynthesis from artemisinic acid became the practical solution.

6.3 Trabectedin（2024 已考）

来源
Ecteinascidia turbinata（tunicate / sea squirt，海鞘）。
性质
marine-derived polycyclic alkaloid, antitumoral。
题眼
industrial production challenge—not mechanism, but supply and manufacturability。
最稳答案方向
obtain a precursor from a biological / microbial source, then semisynthesis to the final drug。
问可能题目：What is the natural origin of trabectedin?
答

标准答案：Trabectedin was isolated from the marine tunicate Ecteinascidia turbinata.

问可能题目：How was the industrial production challenge of trabectedin addressed?
答

标准答案：By obtaining a precursor from a more accessible biological source and converting it to trabectedin by semisynthesis.

6.4 Halichondrin B / Eribuline（2023 和 2024 都很重要）

Halichondrin B 的来源
marine sponge Halichondria okadai。这个一定要会。
问题
supply challenge very severe；课件甚至给了 aquaculture 产量极低的例子。
解决思路
convergent total synthesis + discovery of a simplified analogue。
最终药物
Eribuline。
适应症
breast cancer（2nd intention），Halaven®。
本质
eribuline 不是完整照搬 halichondrin B，而是保留关键药效区域的 simplified / truncated analogue。
Target
tubulin。
问可能题目：What is the natural origin of halichondrin B?
答

标准答案：Halichondrin B comes from the marine sponge Halichondria okadai.

问可能题目：What drug was inspired by halichondrin B?
答

标准答案：Eribuline.

问可能题目：What are the main structural differences between eribuline and halichondrin B?
答

标准答案：Eribuline is a simplified / truncated analogue that keeps the pharmacophore-relevant part of halichondrin B while removing large structural complexity.

问可能题目：What is the therapeutic use of eribuline?
答

标准答案：Breast cancer.

6.5 Avermectins

来源
Streptomyces avermitilis（土壤放线菌）。
用途
broad-spectrum antiparasitic agent。
典型提醒
soil microorganisms are a prolific source of bioactive natural products.
可能题目
Give an example of an important natural product from soil microorganisms.
标准答案
Avermectins from Streptomyces avermitilis.

6.6 Vancomycin

课件把它放在 terrestrial microorganisms / antibiotics 那一块。

作用：说明 microbes，尤其 actinomycetes，是 antibiotics 的重要来源。

6.7 ADC 相关天然产物案例

课件提到通过 ADC 重新利用 old toxic natural products。

例子
trastuzumab emtansine、brentuximab vedotin。

如果问 renewing interest in NP discovery involved which therapeutic strategy：写 ADC / targeted therapies 非常稳。

问可能题目：Which therapeutic strategy contributed to the renewed interest in natural product discovery?
答

标准答案：Targeted therapies such as antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).

7. 表格题怎么防他一手（2023 和 2024 都考了）

只要看到表格，你就固定按下面 4 步走。

先看 potency
MIC 或 IC50。一般越低越好。
再看 safety
对 human cells 的 toxicity 越低越好；很多时候 IC50 越高越好。
再看 selectivity
目标靶点更强、off-target 更弱更好。
最后看 developability
HLM stability、logD、BBB、overall balance。
问可能题目：How should you choose a lead compound from a medicinal chemistry table?
答

标准答案：Choose the compound showing the best balance between potency, selectivity, stability and safety, not simply the most potent one.

问可能题目：What is a hit molecule?
答

标准答案：A hit is an initial active compound identified after screening.

问可能题目：What is a lead molecule?
答

标准答案：A lead is a more optimized compound with better potency and drug-like properties, suitable for further development.

8. Bioisosterism（2023 直接考过）

定义
用几何/电子性质相似的基团或杂环替换原来的基团，在尽量保留活性的同时改善其他性质。
benefits
improve potency, selectivity, metabolic stability, solubility, toxicity, PK/ADME。
问可能题目：What is bioisosterism?
答

标准答案：Bioisosterism is the replacement of a group or ring by another one with similar steric/electronic properties in order to maintain activity while improving other properties.

问可能题目：What are the benefits of bioisosteric replacement?
答

标准答案：It can improve potency, selectivity, metabolic stability, physicochemical properties and safety.

9. 你最可能被阴的地方：老师换案例不换逻辑

今年不一定再考 halichondrin B / trabectedin 原题，但大概率仍然考“某天然产物的来源 + supply challenge + simplified analogue / semisynthesis”。

今年不一定还是 JAK 或 DNA gyrase 表格，但大概率仍然考“hit / lead / selectivity / ADMET / human-cell safety”。

今年不一定原封不动问 NP reluctancy，但很可能继续问“why not suitable for HTS / industry”或“why renewed interest”。

所以防他一手的方法不是狂猜药名，而是把“题目背后的逻辑模板”背熟。

3. 课件里的主线，你要会背什么

Discovery process
target identification → lead generation → hit identification → hit to lead → lead optimization → preclinical → clinical.

Lead generation 两条主路：HTS（大库筛选）和 rational drug design（基于靶点结构设计/虚拟筛选）。

ADMET
absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity。
Target selectivity
越 selective，off-target effects 越少，副作用通常越少。
Natural products
来源广、结构新颖、活性强，但也常常复杂、难放大、难专利、难兼容 HTS。

你只要把这 5 条背顺，卷子里一半 Main Strategies 问题都能开始写。

4. 高频概念的最稳答法

4.1 HTS vs Rational drug design

HTS = High Throughput Screening：用大量化合物库快速筛选，找到初始 hits。

Rational drug design：已知靶点结构或已知结合信息后，利用 docking、virtual screening 或理性设计来找 hits。

最短英文模板：

HTS screens large libraries to identify initial hits.

Rational drug design uses target knowledge or target structure to design/select compounds.

4.2 Hit / Lead / H2L / Lead optimization

Hit
初筛出来、有初步活性的化合物。
Lead
比 hit 更成熟，综合性质更好，更值得继续开发。
Hit to Lead
把初筛分子往“更像药”的方向推。
Lead optimization
继续提高 potency、selectivity、ADME 和 safety。

最短英文模板：

A hit is an initial active compound identified during screening.

A lead is a more optimized compound with improved potency and drug-like properties.

H2L and lead optimization aim to improve potency, selectivity, ADME and safety.

4.3 ADMET / BBB / logP / logD

ADMET 不是死概念，本质是：药物进不进得去、走不走得到、代不代得掉、会不会太毒。

BBB
如果药要治中枢感染，能过 BBB 是优点；如果不需要进脑，过度跨 BBB 反而可能带来 CNS side effects。
logP / logD
反映 lipophilicity，会影响膜通透、吸收、分布、代谢、清除，有时也影响毒性。

最短英文模板：

ADMET describes how a compound behaves in the body and whether it is safe enough.

BBB penetration may be beneficial for CNS indications but may also increase CNS side effects.

Lipophilicity influences permeability, distribution, metabolism and toxicity.

4.4 Human cell lines / safety

HEK293T = human kidney cell line。

HepG2 = human liver cell line。

这类题最稳写法
用来评估 human cell cytotoxicity，尤其是肾和肝相关的安全风险。

最短英文模板：

These cell lines are used to estimate cytotoxicity in human kidney and liver cells and to flag potential safety liabilities.

4.5 Selectivity

selectivity 越高，通常 off-target activity 越少。

selectivity 常常和 safety 连在一起问。

看到 JAK1/JAK2、human homolog、off-target 这类词，就往“减少副作用、避免非目标作用”上写。

最短英文模板：

Higher selectivity usually means fewer off-target effects and fewer side effects.

7. 表格题怎么防他一手

2023 和 2024 都出了表格题，所以今年继续出这种题的概率很高。你看到表格，不要慌，固定按这 4 步走。

1

2

[A] 2. Core vocabulary / 核心术语完整表

Term / 术语

Exam-ready English / 英文定义

中文理解 / 考场用途

Natural product (NP)

A natural product is a small molecule produced by a living organism, often showing biological or pharmacological activity.

生物产生的小分子，常有药理活性。

Natural-product-inspired drug

A drug designed by retaining key structural/pharmacophoric features of a natural product while simplifying or optimizing the molecule.

天然产物启发式药物，如 Halichondrin B -> Eribulin。

Pharmacophore

The minimal set of structural features required for interaction with the biological target.

药效团；结构简化时通常保留。

Drug discovery process

Target identification/validation -> lead generation -> hit identification -> hit-to-lead -> lead optimization -> preclinical -> clinical development.

完整流程；问 process 就写这条。

Lead generation

The step that identifies starting chemical matter by HTS, rational design, fragments, natural products or phenotypic screening.

找起始化合物。

HTS

High-throughput screening tests large compound libraries to identify initial active hits.

高通量筛选；大量化合物库。

Rational drug design

Drug design guided by target structure, known ligands, docking, virtual screening or SAR.

理性设计；有靶点/结构信息。

Hit

An initial active compound identified in screening; it has measurable activity but is not yet optimized.

初始活性分子，不一定成药。

Lead

A more optimized compound with improved potency, selectivity, ADME and safety, suitable for further development.

更成熟候选物。

Hit-to-lead (H2L)

The optimization phase that converts a hit into a lead by improving potency, selectivity and drug-like properties.

hit 到 lead 的优化。

Lead optimization

Further optimization of potency, selectivity, solubility, metabolic stability, PK and toxicity.

继续优化。

SAR

Structure-activity relationship: how changes in chemical structure affect biological activity.

构效关系。

ADMET

Absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion and toxicity.

体内行为 + 安全性。

Bioisosterism

Replacement of an atom/group/ring by another with similar steric or electronic properties to keep activity while improving properties.

生物电子等排；维持活性，改善 ADME/稳定性/毒性/选择性。

Therapeutic window

The margin between effective and toxic exposure.

治疗窗；表格题选 lead 时常用。

药物发现主要策略——合成药物 Main Strategies of Drug Discovery – Synthetic Drugs

课程与本节概览 Section Overview 本节围绕药物化学的基础工具、药物发现流程和合成药策略展开，内容涵盖药物发现、开发的全流程框架与最新思路。 This section covers medicinal chemistry tools, the drug discovery process, and synthetic drug strategies, summarizing the main procedures and new approaches in drug development.

药物创新发展历程 Timeline of Drug Innovation 药物发现由偶然观察主导转向分子靶点设计，随着科学进步不断提高复杂度与精准性。 Drug discovery has evolved from serendipitous observations to targeted molecular design, growing in complexity and precision with scientific progress.

早年药物如阿司匹林、水杨酸等多基于巧合发现。 Early drugs like aspirin and salicylic acid were often discovered by chance.

随生物及分子机制认知加深，现代药物聚焦分子功能和精准靶向（如抗癌、自免疾病治疗）。 Modern drugs emphasize molecular function and precise targeting (e.g., anticancer and autoimmune disease treatments).

新药开发流程与难点 Drug Development Process and Challenges 新药开发流程包括早期探索、临床前研究、分期临床试验及审批，过程漫长、风险高且成本巨大。 The drug development process covers early exploratory work, preclinical research, staged clinical trials, and regulatory approval, demanding long timeframes, high costs, and high risk.

平均研发周期为10-15年，总投入高达15-20亿欧元。 Average development takes 10–15 years, with up to 1.5–2 billion euros invested.

成功率极低，仅1/5000~1/10000化合物可上市。 Very low success rate – only about 1 in 5000–10000 compounds reaches the market.

最常见失败原因是疗效不足（52%）和安全性问题（24%），其余因商业或操作决策导致。 Main causes of failure: 52% lack of efficacy, 24% safety issues, rest due to business or operational reasons.

药物发现工作流程 Drug Discovery Workflow 药物发现分阶段推进： Drug discovery follows stepwise stages:

靶点识别与初筛
寻找有潜力的生物靶点，对大量化合物进行高通量筛选，获得初筛活性分子（hit）。 Target identification and hit screening: choosing potential biological targets, high-throughput screening (HTS) to identify hit molecules.
初筛到先导化合物
通过理化性质、活性和选择性等结构优化，筛选出结构-活性关系明确、可优化的“先导化合物”。 Hit-to-lead: optimize structure, activity, selectivity to identify lead compounds with clear structure–activity relationships.
先导优化
不断通过结构修饰提高药效、选择性和药物样性，并解决ADMET属性。 Lead optimization: structurally refine leads to improve efficacy, selectivity, and drug-likeness, while solving ADMET issues.
药物候选化合物
最终筛选具备理化、吸收分布、代谢、毒性均优、可用于临床前开发的化合物。 Candidate drug selection: identify molecules with suitable physicochemical, ADME, and toxicity profiles for preclinical development.

结构与活性关系（SAR） Structure–Activity Relationship (SAR) SAR研究通过系统修饰分子结构，考察结构变化对药物生物活性的影响，明确哪些官能团或片段属于药效团（pharmacophore），哪些属于药效团元件（pharmacophoric elements）。 SAR involves systematic modifications of molecular structure to study how changes affect biological activity, identifying pharmacophores and pharmacophoric elements.

药效团指分子中不可更改、决定活性的关键结构。 Pharmacophore: essential structure that must remain unchanged for activity.

药效团元件则可修饰改善活性、选择性或ADMET属性，如在苯并二氮卓类、香豆素等骨架上引入取代基用于调节疏水性、氢键能力等。 Pharmacophoric elements: modifiable fragments to tune activity, selectivity, or ADMET, e.g., in benzodiazepine or coumarin rings, substitutions adjust hydrophobicity, H-bonding, etc.

录音补充
研究人员常根据靶点口袋的疏水或亲水性质、有无特定空间需求，在关键位置引入酰基、羟基、卤素 nhóm等官能团，再通过结构-活性关系测试，验证对活性的影响，逐步推导最优结构。 Audio addition: Researchers modify molecules based on the hydrophobic/hydrophilic or spatial properties of target site, introducing functional groups (acyl, hydroxyl, halogen, etc.), then test SAR to determine the best structure.

药物物化和ADMET属性及评价 Physicochemical/ADMET Properties and Evaluation 对候选化合物需系统评估其物化性质（如分子量、极性表面积、旋转键数、H受体/供体数、LogP等），以及ADMET表现（吸收分布代谢排泄与毒性），确保药代动力学表现优良且无明显毒性。 Comprehensive assessment of candidate compounds: physicochemical (weight, PSA, rotatable bonds, H-bond donors/acceptors, LogP, etc.) and ADMET (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity) to ensure good pharmacokinetics and safety.

药物口服生物利用度常参考Lipinski“五原则”，如MW < 500, HBA < 10, HBD < 5, LogP < 5, PSA < 140 Å²，越接近这些标准则透膜与吸收性能越佳。 Oral bioavailability often follows Lipinski’s Rule of Five (MW < 500, HBA < 10, HBD < 5, LogP < 5, PSA < 140 Å²), better matching improves permeability/absorption.

QSAR/AI等虚拟筛选、ADMETlab等工具常被用于高通量预测药代毒性指标，如溶解度LogS, 分布系数LogD7.4, Caco-2通透性, 人肝毒性H-HT, Ames致突变、皮肤致敏性等，为分子设计阶段提供重要标准。 QSAR, AI-based tools (e.g., ADMETlab) enable high-throughput prediction of drug-likeness, solubility (LogS), distribution (LogD7.4), Caco-2 permeability, hepatotoxicity, Ames mutagenicity, skin sensitization, etc., informing early molecular design.

常见药物设计与发现策略 Drug Discovery & Design Strategies 合成化合物库筛选，天然产物活性筛选，碎片拼接策略、理性药物设计（基于靶点三维结构）、药物重定位、对专利药物的“me-too”仿制与副作用改良等，均是药物创新核心路径。 Typical strategies include synthetic library screening, natural product screening, fragment-based discovery, rational (structure-based) design, drug repositioning, “me-too” variants, and exploiting/optimizing side effects.

结构-活性关系（SAR）与药物理化性质 Structure-Activity Relationship (SAR) & Drug Physicochemical Properties

结构-活性关系（SAR）基本概念 Fundamentals of Structure-Activity Relationship (SAR) 结构-活性关系（SAR）研究药物化学结构与其生物活性或治疗效果之间的关系。 SAR explores the relationship between a drug’s chemical structure and its biological activity or therapeutic effect. 这是新药设计与优化的重要工具。 It is a key tool for new drug design and optimization.

SAR的两大组成 Two Core Parts of SAR SAR通常包括药效团和药效团元件两部分。 SAR usually contains two parts: pharmacophore and pharmacophoric elements.

药效团是分子中必须保持不变、决定药物能与靶点结合并产生生物效应的三维结构。 Pharmacophore refers to the rigid 3D structure/features essential for target binding and activity.

药效团元件则是在药效团骨架上可修饰的官能团，用于调节药效、选择性和理化性质。 Pharmacophoric elements are modifiable groups on the scaffold, tuning activity, selectivity, and physicochemical properties.

苯并二氮卓类SAR分析 SAR Example: Benzodiazepines 以苯并二氮卓为例，其骨架是不可改变的核心药效团，多个取代基则属于药效团元件。 For benzodiazepines, the ring system is the unchangeable pharmacophore, while various substituents are the pharmacophoric elements.

2位酮基（Keto group）对活性至关重要； The keto group at position 2 is crucial for activity;

7位卤素（如Cl）增强与靶点的结合； Halogen (e.g., Cl) at position 7 enhances binding;

5位苯基环增强疏水作用及与靶点的结合； The phenyl ring at position 5 strengthens hydrophobic and binding interactions;

如果某些位置引入羟基可提高分子的极性； Hydroxy group introduction at certain positions raises polarity;

7位的电子吸引基团帮助分子与靶点结合更牢固。 Electron-withdrawing groups at position 7 support stronger target engagement.

氢键能力
氢键能增强药物与靶点结合，为活性提供支持； Hydrogen bonding: strengthens binding to target and supports activity;
疏水性
分子的脂溶性影响其通过细胞膜的能力，也影响靶点结合； Lipophilicity: affects membrane permeability and target binding;
电荷状态
影响分子结合力及生物效应； Charge: modulates binding affinity and biological response;
立体化学
原子排列影响作用专一性和药效。 Stereochemistry: arrangement of atoms impacts specificity and efficacy.

库马林类分子案例补充 SAR Example: Coumarin Compounds 在库马林环等结构中，大量可变化的官能团位置用于调节结构-活性关系，从而优化药物性能。 In coumarin rings, many substituent positions are changeable for SAR, enabling drug performance optimization.

不同取代基影响分子与靶点疏水口袋、极性腔的作用，使化合物具有不同活性。 Different groups affect hydrophobic pockets and polar cavities in the target, yielding various activities.

合理引入取代基和官能团，是药物优化设计的常见策略。 Rational substitution and group introduction is a typical drug design approach.

药物理化性质与五规则 Drug Physicochemical Properties & Rule of Five 药物的理化性质对活性和体内表现（如吸收、透膜等）至关重要。 Drug physicochemical properties critically affect bioactivity and in vivo performance (absorption, permeability, etc.).
