      1.1 定义和四个主要目标 ★★★ 1.1 Definition and four main goals

      课件的定义：化学计量学（chemometrics）是一门化学学科，它用数学、统计和其他方法来设计或选择最优的测量程序和实验，并通过分析化学数据提供最多的相关化学信息。

      The slides' definition: chemometrics is the chemical discipline that uses mathematical, statistical and other methods to design or select optimal measurement procedures and experiments, and to provide maximum relevant chemical information by analysing chemical data.

      它是分析化学的一个分支学科，出现在 70 年代初。

      It is a sub-discipline of analytical chemistry that appeared in the early 1970s.

      课件说它是「统计学、计算机和信息时代的孩子」：没有计算机，就处理不了这么多数据。

      The slides call it "the child of statistics, computers and the information age": without computers, this much data could not be handled.

      定义里有两半：前一半是「设计实验」，后一半是「从数据里提取信息」。

      The definition has two halves: the first is "designing experiments" and the second is "extracting information from data".

      1.3 目标一：实验设计（DoE） ★★★ 1.3 Goal 1: experimental design (DoE)

      因素（factor） 就是实验参数，比如温度、时间、试剂量。

      A factor is an experimental parameter, such as temperature, time or the amount of reagent.

      传统做法 OFAT（One Factor At A Time，一次一个因素）：只改变一个因素，其他因素都固定在中间值。

      The traditional approach, OFAT (One Factor At A Time): vary only one factor while all the others are held at their intermediate value.

      课件的蛋黄酱（mayonnaise）例子有三个因素，各三个水平：

      The slides' mayonnaise example has three factors with three levels each:

      OFAT 就是把三个因素分别、独立地测试，课件指出它有两个问题：

      OFAT tests the three factors separately and independently, and the slides point out two problems with it:

      1. 多米诺效应（domino effect）：一个实验失败，后面依赖它的结论全部受影响。
      2. The domino effect: if one experiment fails, every conclusion that depends on it is compromised.
      3. 看不到交互作用（interaction）：因素是一个个单独改的，所以永远发现不了「两个因素一起变时才出现」的效果。
      4. Interactions cannot be seen: because the factors are changed one at a time, an effect that appears only when two factors change together can never be found.

      交互作用是什么？ 如果因素 A 的效果在因素 B 的不同水平下不一样，就说 A 和 B 有交互作用。

      What is an interaction? If the effect of factor A is different at different levels of factor B, A and B are said to interact.

      全因子设计 2³（full factorial design）：三个因素各取低、高两个水平，所有组合都做一遍（2 × 2 × 2 = 8 个），再加一个中心点（central point），一共 9 个实验。

      The 2³ full factorial design: each of the three factors takes a low and a high level, every combination is run (2 × 2 × 2 = 8), and a central point is added, giving 9 experiments in total.

      1.4 目标二：分类（癌细胞例子） ★★★ 1.4 Goal 2: classification (cancer cells)

      数据：683 位病人的细胞，每个细胞测了 10 个描述符（descriptors），要区分良性和恶性。

      The data: cells from 683 patients, each described by 10 descriptors, and the task is to distinguish benign from malignant.

      经典方法：每个变量单独比较良性组和恶性组，结果 10 个变量全部有显著差异，所以看起来区分很简单。

      The classical approach: each variable is compared on its own between the benign and malignant groups, and all 10 variables differ significantly, so the distinction seems simple.

      但经典方法默认了两件事：变量之间互相独立，而且每个变量事先就被认为是相关的。

      But the classical approach assumes two things: that the variables are independent of each other, and that each variable is relevant a priori.

      化学计量学方法（PCA）：所有变量一起分析，Dim1 解释 65.55%，Dim2 解释 8.62%，两个轴加起来约 75%。

      The chemometric approach (PCA): all variables are analysed together; Dim1 explains 65.55% and Dim2 explains 8.62%, about 75% for the two axes together.

      PCA 揭示了更复杂的真实情况：

      PCA reveals a more complex real situation:

      • 10 个变量里有 9 个在第一轴上高度相关，说明它们其实在重复同一种信息，并不是 10 个独立的证据。
      • Nine of the 10 variables are strongly correlated on the first axis, which means they largely repeat the same information rather than being 10 independent pieces of evidence.
      • 剩下的「有丝分裂」（mitoses）这个变量对应第二轴，它解释了恶性细胞在第二轴方向上的分散。
      • The remaining variable, mitoses, corresponds to the second axis and explains the spread of the malignant cells along that axis.
      • 树状图（dendrogram）根据个体之间的距离把病人一层一层聚成组。
      • The dendrogram groups the patients level by level according to the distances between individuals.

      这个例子要记住的道理：单变量检验说「都显著」，并不代表我们理解了数据；多变量方法能看到变量之间的相关结构，这是单变量方法看不到的。

      The lesson of this example: when univariate tests say "all significant", that does not mean we understand the data; multivariate methods can see the correlation structure between variables, which univariate methods cannot.

      1.5 目标三：校准与预测（从单波长到 PLS） ★★★ 1.5 Goal 3: calibration and prediction (from one wavelength to PLS)

      校准（calibration） 是用已知浓度的标准样品建立信号和浓度的关系，预测（prediction） 是反过来用这个关系由未知样品的信号求浓度。

      Calibration builds the relationship between signal and concentration using standards of known concentration; prediction goes the other way, using that relationship to find an unknown sample's concentration from its signal.

      经典单波长校准
      Classical single-wavelength calibration      		 PLS 回归
      PLS regression
      用了什么
      What it uses      		 只用最灵敏的一个波长（课件例子是 550 nm）
      Only the single most sensitive wavelength (550 nm in the slides)      		 降维以后的整条光谱
      The whole spectrum after dimension reduction
      怎么建模
      How the model is built      		 一元线性回归
      Simple linear regression      		 找使 y（浓度）和 X（光谱）协方差最大的方向
      Finds the directions that maximise the covariance between y (concentration) and X (spectra)
      样品里混进别的物质
      A foreign substance in the sample      		 发现不了，悄悄给出一个错误浓度
      Goes unnoticed and silently gives a wrong concentration      		 预测之前就能发现光谱「不对劲」
      Detects that the spectrum looks wrong before predicting
      性能
      Performance      		 一般
      Ordinary      		 更稳健、更准确，还能同时预测多个性质
      More robust and accurate, and can predict several properties at once

      PLS 和 PCA 最根本的区别：PCA 只看 X，PLS 找方向时同时看 X 和 y。

      The most fundamental difference between PLS and PCA: PCA looks only at X, while PLS uses both X and y when it looks for directions.

      课件的 PLS 例子：混合物里有三个成分，用了 100 个真实样品建模。

      The slides' PLS example: mixtures with three components, modelled with 100 real samples.

      用真实样品
      Using real samples      		 说明
      Explanation
      优点
      Advantage      		 样品是真的，包含了实际会遇到的所有变化
      The samples are real, so they contain all the variation met in practice
      缺点
      Drawback      		 需要一个参考方法（reference method）先测出每个样品的真实浓度
      A reference method is needed to measure each sample's true concentration first

      怎么判断模型好：画「预测值 对 参考值」的图，点越贴近斜率 1、截距 0 的对角线，预测越准。

      课件外补充：为什么不把几百个波长直接塞进普通多元回归？因为相邻波长几乎一样（共线性，collinearity），系数会极不稳定；PLS 先降维就避开了这个问题。

      Not in the slides: why not put hundreds of wavelengths straight into ordinary multiple regression? Because neighbouring wavelengths are almost identical (collinearity), so the coefficients become very unstable; PLS avoids this by reducing the dimensions first.

      1.6 过拟合与交叉验证 ★★★ 1.6 Overfitting and cross-validation

      PLS 要决定用几个成分（潜变量），课件用 RMSE（均方根误差）曲线来选。

      PLS has to decide how many components (latent variables) to use, and the slides choose it with an RMSE (root mean square error) curve.

      曲线
      Curve      		 随成分数增加
      As components are added      		 意思
      Meaning
      校准误差（calibration）
      Calibration error      		 一直下降
      Keeps falling      		 模型越复杂，对建模样品拟合得越好，所以不能用它来选成分数
      The more complex the model, the better it fits the modelling samples, so it cannot be used to choose the number of components
      验证误差（validation）
      Validation error      		 先降后升
      Falls then rises      		 上升的部分就是过拟合；最低点对应最好的成分数
      The rising part is overfitting; the lowest point gives the best number of components

      过拟合（overfitting）：模型太复杂，把建模数据里的噪声也学进去了，对老样品很准，对新样品很差。

      Overfitting: the model is too complex and has also learned the noise in the modelling data, so it is accurate on the old samples and poor on new ones.

      就像死背练习题答案的学生，练习题全对，换一道新题就不会。

      It is like a student who memorises the answers to practice questions: perfect on the practice set, lost on a new question.

      课件用的是完全留一交叉验证（full leave-one-out cross-validation）：每次拿出一个样品当「新样品」，用剩下的样品建模去预测它，所有样品轮一遍。

      The slides use full leave-one-out cross-validation: each time one sample is taken out as a "new sample", the model is built on the rest to predict it, and this is repeated for every sample.

      这个例子最后选了 3 个成分，正好对应混合物里的三个成分。

      This example finally chose 3 components, which matches the three components in the mixture.

      1.7 目标四：数据提取（代谢组学） ★★ 1.7 Goal 4: data extraction (metabolomics)

      用 HPLC/HRMS（液相色谱 / 高分辨质谱）分析生物样品，会得到几万个信号。

      Analysing biological samples by HPLC/HRMS (liquid chromatography / high-resolution mass spectrometry) gives tens of thousands of signals.

      其中绝大多数是身体本来就有的内源性物质，并不是疾病标志物。

      Most of them are endogenous substances that the body always contains, not disease markers.

      用 PCA 衍生的方法，可以找回对照组和病理组的分组，并找出在病人身上表达偏高或偏低的变量。

      Methods derived from PCA can recover the control and pathological groups and pick out the variables that are over- or under-expressed in the patients.

      最后只有这些「有意义的信号」才拿去数据库检索鉴定，省去了逐个鉴定几万个峰的工作。

      In the end only these signals of interest are identified by database search, which saves identifying tens of thousands of peaks one by one.

      一句话总结：化学计量学放弃「理解每个因素」，换来用全部变量一起去优化实验（DoE）、分类样品（PCA）、准确预测（PLS，要用交叉验证防过拟合）和从海量信号里提取有用信息。

      One-sentence summary: Chemometrics gives up on understanding each factor in exchange for using all variables together to optimise experiments (DoE), classify samples (PCA), predict accurately (PLS, with cross-validation against overfitting) and extract useful information from huge numbers of signals.

      4.1 回归在做什么 ★★★ 4.1 What regression does

      分析化学里最常见的回归（regression）就是校准曲线（calibration curve）：用几个已知浓度的标准样品（x）和测到的信号（y）画一条直线。

      The most common regression in analytical chemistry is the calibration curve: a straight line through the signals (y) measured for a few standards of known concentration (x).

      以后测到未知样品的信号，就能从直线上反推出它的浓度。

      Later, when an unknown sample's signal is measured, its concentration can be read back from the line.

      最小二乘法（least squares）的意思是：在所有可能的直线里，选让各点到直线的竖直距离平方和最小的那一条。

      Least squares means choosing, out of all possible lines, the one that makes the sum of the squared vertical distances from the points to the line as small as possible.

      每个点到直线的竖直距离叫残差（residual）。

      The vertical distance from each point to the line is called the residual.

      4.3 Fisher 检验 ★★★ 4.3 The Fisher test

      思路：y 的总变化可以分成两部分，被直线解释掉的部分（回归），和直线解释不了的部分（残差）。

      The idea: the total variation in y can be split into two parts, the part explained by the line (regression) and the part the line cannot explain (residual).

      课件里的量
      Quantity in the slides      		 意思
      Meaning
      SCEreg（回归平方和）
      SCEreg (regression sum of squares)      		 被直线解释掉的变化
      The variation explained by the line
      SCEres（残差平方和）
      SCEres (residual sum of squares)      		 直线解释不了的变化
      The variation the line cannot explain
      S²reg = SCEreg / 1 回归方差（自由度为 1）
      The regression variance (1 degree of freedom)
      S²res = SCEres / (n − 2) 残差方差（估计了斜率和截距两个参数，所以减 2）
      The residual variance (two parameters, slope and intercept, were estimated, so subtract 2)
      F = S²reg / S²res 被解释的方差 ÷ 没被解释的方差
      Explained variance ÷ unexplained variance

      课件的两个假设：

      The two hypotheses in the slides:

      • H0：回归方差并不显著大于残差方差（直线没有真正解释 y）。
      • H0: the regression variance is not significantly greater than the residual variance (the line does not really explain y).
      • H1：回归方差显著大于残差方差（回归有意义，斜率显著不为 0）。
      • H1: the regression variance is significantly greater than the residual variance (the regression is meaningful and the slope is significantly different from 0).

      怎么读：F 很大（p < 0.05）就拒绝 H0，说明 x 和 y 之间存在真实的线性关系。

      How to read it: if F is large (p < 0.05), reject H0, which means there is a real linear relationship between x and y.

      直觉就是「信号 ÷ 噪声」：直线解释的部分远大于剩下的随机误差，这条线才可信。

      The intuition is "signal ÷ noise": the line can be trusted only when the part it explains is much bigger than the remaining random error.

      4.4 外推（课件外补充） ★ 4.4 Extrapolation (not in the slides)

      在校准范围内预测叫内插（interpolation），可靠；超出校准范围预测叫外推（extrapolation），不可靠。

      Predicting inside the calibration range is called interpolation and is reliable; predicting outside it is called extrapolation and is unreliable.

      原因是直线关系只在测过的范围内被验证过，范围外可能弯曲或饱和，R² 再高也说明不了范围外的情况。

      The reason is that the straight-line relationship has only been checked within the measured range; outside it the response may curve or saturate, and however high R² is, it says nothing about what happens outside.

      一句话总结：回归用最小二乘直线描述 x 和 y 的关系；斜率必须不为 0，R² 说明解释了多少，Fisher 检验（F = S²reg / S²res）判断回归方差是否显著大于残差方差。

      One-sentence summary: Regression describes the x–y relationship with a least-squares line; the slope must be non-zero, R² shows how much is explained, and the Fisher test (F = S²reg / S²res) decides whether the regression variance is significantly greater than the residual variance.

      5.5 预处理：第一步缩放，第二步中心化 ★★★ 5.5 Pretreatment: scaling first, then centering

      第一步：缩放（scaling）。

      Step 1: scaling.

      不同变量的数值范围不一样，范围大的变量方差就大。

      Variables have different numerical ranges, and a variable with a large range has a large variance.

      PCA 找的是方差最大的方向，所以方差大的变量会压过方差小的变量。

      PCA looks for the directions of largest variance, so variables with large variance overpower those with small variance.

      The slides' example: in HER, cholesterol ranges from 2 to 1252 mg and age from 12 to 73 years; without scaling, cholesterol dominates everything, not because it matters more but because its numbers are larger.

      最常用的是单位方差缩放（UV scaling）：算出每一列的标准差 sₖ，把这一列乘以 1/sₖ，于是每一列的方差都等于 1。

      The most common is unit variance (UV) scaling: compute each column's standard deviation sₖ and multiply the column by 1/sₖ, so every column has a variance of 1.

      课件的形象说法：UV 缩放把「长」的变量压短，把「短」的变量拉长。

      The slides' image: UV scaling shrinks the "long" variables and stretches the "short" ones.

      第二步：中心化（centering）。

      Step 2: centering.

      算出每个变量的均值，再从数据里减掉。

      Compute each variable's mean and subtract it from the data.

      缩放加中心化以后，所有变量「一样长」，均值都是 0。

      After scaling and centering, all variables have equal "length" and a mean of 0.

      Why square: deviations are positive and negative (−2 and +2), so adding them directly cancels out to 0; squaring makes them all positive, and the square root at the end brings the units back.

      为什么除以 n − 1：和回归里的自由度是同一个道理，平均值是用这几个数自己算出来的，用掉了 1 个自由度；有的软件除以 n，数据多时两者几乎没区别。

      Why divide by n − 1: the same idea as the degrees of freedom in regression; the mean was computed from these same numbers, which uses up 1 degree of freedom; some software divides by n, and with plenty of data the difference is negligible.

      5.8 PC1 和 PC2 是怎么找到的 ★★★ 5.8 How PC1 and PC2 are found

      先只看两个变量：身高和体重，每个人是平面上的一个点。

      Take only two variables first, height and weight; each person is one point on a plane.

      高的人一般也重，所以点会排成一个斜着的椭圆形点云；预处理以后，点云的中心正好在原点。

      Taller people are usually heavier, so the points form a slanted, oval-shaped cloud; after pretreatment the centre of the cloud sits exactly at the origin.

      找 PC1：转一根直线 Finding PC1: rotate a line

      想象一根穿过中心的直线，可以像钟表指针一样转；每转到一个角度，就把所有点垂直投影到这根线上。

      Imagine a straight line through the centre that can turn like the hand of a clock; at each angle, every point is dropped perpendicularly onto the line.

      有的角度，点在线上挤成一小团；有的角度，点在线上铺得很开。

      At some angles the points bunch up on the line; at others they spread out widely.

      铺得最开（方差最大）的那个方向就是 PC1。在这个例子里，它沿着椭圆的长轴，可以理解为「整体个子大小」。

      The direction where they spread out most (largest variance) is PC1. In this example it runs along the long axis of the oval and can be read as "overall body size".

      这时点离直线的距离（残差）也最小，所以课件说 PC1 同时满足：方差最大、分散最大、残差最小，这三条其实是同一件事。

      At this angle the distances from the points to the line (residuals) are also smallest, which is why the slides say PC1 has maximum variance, maximum dispersion and minimum residuals; the three are really the same thing.

      找 PC2：和 PC1 垂直 Finding PC2: perpendicular to PC1

      PC2 必须和 PC1 垂直，在这个条件下再找点铺得最开的方向。

      PC2 must be perpendicular to PC1, and under that condition it is again the direction where the points spread out most.

      两个变量时，垂直的方向只剩一个，所以 PC2 就是椭圆的短轴，可以理解为「偏瘦还是偏壮」。

      With two variables only one perpendicular direction is left, so PC2 is the short axis of the oval, which can be read as "slim or stocky".

      垂直是为了让 PC2 只装 PC1 没装的新信息，不重复。

      Being perpendicular means PC2 holds only new information that PC1 does not, with no repetition.

      14 个变量时也一样 The same with 14 variables

      14 个变量就是 14 维空间，画不出来，但道理完全一样：PC1 是铺得最开的方向，PC2 和它垂直、铺得第二开，然后是 PC3、PC4……

      With 14 variables it is a 14-dimensional space that cannot be drawn, but the idea is exactly the same: PC1 is the most spread-out direction, PC2 is perpendicular to it and the second most spread out, then PC3, PC4…

      5.9 用一个小例子把名词串起来：身高和体重 ★★★ 5.9 One small example that ties the terms together: height and weight

      这里的数字只是帮助理解，是为了看清每个名词指的是什么。

      The numbers here are only an aid to understanding, so you can see what each term refers to.

      两个变量身高和体重已经标准化（均值 0、方差 1），假设它们的相关系数是 0.8：高的人大多也重。

      The two variables, height and weight, are already standardized (mean 0, variance 1), and suppose their correlation is 0.8: tall people are mostly heavy too.

      每个变量方差是 1，所以总信息量（总方差）= 1 + 1 = 2。

      Each variable has variance 1, so the total information (total variance) = 1 + 1 = 2.

      特征向量 = 新轴的「配方」= 载荷 Eigenvector = the "recipe" of a new axis = loadings

      • PC1 = 0.71 × 身高 + 0.71 × 体重：一半身高加一半体重，意思是「整体个子大小」。
      • PC1 = 0.71 × height + 0.71 × weight: half height plus half weight, meaning "overall body size".
      • PC2 = 0.71 × 身高 − 0.71 × 体重：身高减体重，意思是「高而瘦」还是「矮而壮」。

      This set of coefficients (0.71, 0.71) is the eigenvector, also called the loadings; the larger a coefficient (positive or negative), the more that variable matters for the axis.

      看配方还能看出垂直：PC1 是「加」，PC2 是「减」，两者不重复信息。

      5.12 怎么描述一张 PCA 图 ★★★ 5.12 How to describe a PCA plot

      按这四步来描述，就不会漏掉要点：

      3. 说个体：哪些个体聚在一起，谁是离群点，几组沿哪个方向分开。

      5. 说变量：哪些变量同向（正相关），哪些反向（负相关），哪个箭头短。
      6. Describe the variables: which point the same way (positively correlated), which point opposite ways (negatively correlated), and which arrow is short.

      6.1 数据库和科学数据库 ★★★ 6.1 Databases and scientific databases

      数据库（database）：为了方便访问、管理和更新而组织起来的信息，可以看成一个有组织的信息集合。

      A database is information set up for easy access, management and updating; it can be thought of as an organized collection of information.

      数据库用来存储、维护和访问各种数据，把关于人、地点或事物的信息集中在一处，方便观察和分析。

      Databases are used to store, maintain and access any sort of data, gathering information on people, places or things in one place so it can be observed and analysed.

      数据库的类型：按内容可以分为文献型（bibliographic）、全文型（full text）、数值型（numeric）和图像型（images）；在计算机领域，常按组织方式来分类。

      Types of databases: by content they can be bibliographic, full text, numeric or images; in computing they are often classified by their organizational approach.

      科学数据库（scientific database）：一个计算机化的、有组织的相关数据集合，可以用于科学研究和长期保管（long-term stewardship）。

      A scientific database is a computerized, organized collection of related data that can be accessed for scientific inquiry and long-term stewardship.

      它的价值在于整合：把不同来源、不同类型的数据集结合起来，用新的方式、常常是跨学科地分析，让新的科学问题成为可能。

      Its value lies in integration: combining dissimilar datasets and analysing them in new ways, often across disciplines, which makes new kinds of scientific inquiry possible.

      6.2 数据管理 ★★ 6.2 Data management

      数据管理（data management）：接收（ingesting）、存储、组织和维护一个机构所产生和收集的数据的过程。

      Data management is the process of ingesting, storing, organizing and maintaining the data created and collected by an organization.

      它由多种功能组成，共同目的是保证数据准确（accurate）、可用（available）、可访问（accessible）。

      It combines several functions whose common aim is to make sure the data are accurate, available and accessible.

      课件外补充：常听到的 FAIR 原则（可发现、可访问、可互操作、可复用）是同一个思路，课件没有讲，知道名字就行。

      6.5 RDF 和 SPARQL ★★★ 6.5 RDF and SPARQL

      缩写
      Abbreviation      		 全称
      Full name      		 是什么
      What it is
      RDF Resource Description Framework 用「主语－谓语－宾语」三元组（triple）描述数据的格式，很多三元组连起来就是一张图
      A format that describes data as subject–predicate–object triples; many linked triples form a graph
      SPARQL SPARQL Protocol and RDF Query Language 课件定义：在 RDF 数据库里搜索用的查询语言
      The slides' definition: the query language used to search in an RDF database

      课件里的三元组就是 DAAP 本体里的关系，比如：某次实验 — hasSlide（有载玻片）— 某张载玻片；某个细胞 — hasMeasure（有测量）— 某条光谱。

      The triples in the slides are the relations of the DAAP ontology, for example: a campaign — hasSlide — a slide; a cell — hasMeasure — a spectrum.

      课件的 SPARQL 例子：找出培养基 M1 C1 下所有细胞的光谱。

      The slides' SPARQL example: find all cell spectra for the M1 C1 culture medium.

      查询的思路是顺着三元组一步步走：实验 → 它的载玻片 → 载玻片的培养基是 STD、载玻片上的细胞 → 细胞的测量（光谱）。

      The query works by following the triples step by step: campaign → its slides → slides whose medium is STD and the cells on them → the cells' measurements (spectra).

      select distinct ?campaign ?spc
where {
  ?campaign project:hasSlide ?slide .
  ?slide project:medium project:STD ;
         project:hasCell ?cell .
  ?cell project:hasMeasure ?spc .
}

      不用会写代码，只要能看懂：带问号的是要找的未知量，每一行是一个三元组条件。

      You do not need to be able to write it, only to understand it: the terms with a question mark are the unknowns to find, and each line is a triple condition.

      一句话：RDF 负责「怎么存」，SPARQL 负责「怎么问」。

      In one sentence: RDF handles "how to store", and SPARQL handles "how to ask".
