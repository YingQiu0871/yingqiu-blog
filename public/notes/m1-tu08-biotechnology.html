      Upstream process (USP)

      → Cell banking (MCB/WCB)

      → Concentration

      → Viral removal / inactivation (if needed)

      → Formulation

      → Sterile filtration

      → Filling / lyophilization

      → 浓缩

      → 纯化（各类层析 / 过滤 / 沉淀）

      → 配制

      → 无菌过滤

      QC of Therapeutic Proteins（Taverna & Smadja + mock Q3/Q4）

      3.1 四大必控项目 = mock Q4 标准答案

      4 main characteristics to be controlled

      IdentityConfirm that the batch contains the correct therapeutic protein and structural variants.→ 身份：确认批次确实是目标蛋白（原始结构 + 相关变体）。

      PurityBiopharmaceutical must be free from contaminating substances (host cell proteins & DNA, degraded forms, aggregates, process-related impurities…).→ 纯度：去除宿主蛋白 / DNA、降解产物、聚集体、工艺残留等。

      Dosage (Content / Assay)Determine accurate concentration of active protein in drug substance / product to ensure correct dose delivered.→ 含量：准确测定活性成分浓度，保证给药剂量正确。

      StabilityCheck how the product reacts to stress (heat, pH changes, shaking, light) to decide the storage conditions and how long it remains stable.→ 稳定性：考察对温度、pH、剪切、光等的敏感性，确定贮存条件和有效期。

      👉 建议背成一句：

      “Identity–Purity–Dosage–Stability / 身份-纯度-含量-稳定性”。

      3.2 主要杂质类别（Cervarix Q5 也考）

      Categories of impurities (protein products & vaccines)

      Product-related impuritiesDegraded protein (fragmented, oxidized, deamidated), misfolded variants, aggregates.→ 与产物相关：降解片段、氧化 / 去酰胺变体、构象异常、聚集体。

      Host cell–related impuritiesHost cell proteins (HCP), host cell DNA/RNA, lipids, endotoxins.→ 宿主相关：宿主蛋白、宿主 DNA/RNA、脂类、内毒素等。

      Process-related impuritiesChromatography leachable, solvents, salts, detergents, media components.→ 工艺相关：层析配体脱落、溶剂、盐、表面活性剂、培养基成分残留。

      Microbial contaminantsBacteria, fungi, adventitious viruses, mycoplasma.→ 微生物污染：细菌、真菌、外源病毒、支原体。

      用于 Vaccines 2022 Q5：

      “Any micro-organisms; product-related; process-related; host cell–related” —— 就是这 4 类。

      3.3 关键 QC 技术 & 对应用途（可用于简答）

      可以背一个表式：

      ① Identity（身份）

      Peptide mapping：enzymatic digestion + RP-HPLC fingerprint

      Mass spectrometry (MS)：accurate mass of protein/peptides

      IEF：separate proteins according to pI

      👉 中文提示：肽图 + 质谱 + 等电聚焦，用来确认是不是对的蛋白。

      ② Purity / variants（纯度 & 变体）

      SDS-PAGE：separate proteins by size → main band, fragments

      SEC：size-exclusion → aggregates and fragments

      Ion-exchange chromatography (IEX)：separate by charge → charge variants

      中文提示：SDS-PAGE 看大小；SEC 看聚集体；IEX 看电荷变体。

      ③ Glycosylation（糖基化）

      Monosaccharide / sialic acid assays：overall sugar & sialic-acid level

      Released glycan mapping：profile of released N-glycans

      Glycopeptide mapping：site-specific glycosylation

      👉 中文提示：单糖/唾液酸定量 + 游离糖链图谱 + 糖肽图谱。

      ④ Dosage / Activity（含量 & 活性）

      UV / colorimetric assays：total protein content

      ELISA：specific quantification of active protein

      Cell-based bioassays：biological activity / potency

      👉 中文提示：UV/比色测总蛋白，ELISA 测特异蛋白，细胞实验看活性。

      3.4 Mock exam Q3：Peptide mapping

      Peptide mapping – definition & rolePeptide mapping is an identity control method where the protein is enzymatically digested (e.g. trypsin) and the resulting peptides are separated (RP-HPLC) and compared to a reference profile, possibly coupled with MS.→ 肽图：用酶解（如胰蛋白酶）把蛋白切成一系列肽段，通过 RP-HPLC（±MS）分离检测，将色谱 / 质谱指纹图与对照品比较，用于身份鉴别 + 结构完整性评估。

      答题骨架（1–2 句话）：

      Peptide mapping = enzymatic digestion + chromatographic separation of peptides to obtain a fingerprint chromatogram/mass pattern and compare with a reference, for identity and structural integrity control.

      Glycosylation & Biobetters（mock Q1 + Biobetters slides）

      4.1 Glycosylation 重要性（QC & EPO）

      Why glycosylation is important• Solubility & stability• Proper folding• Bioactivity: receptor recognition, cell targeting• Pharmacokinetics: serum half-life, clearance (asialoglycoprotein receptor)• Immunogenicity→ 糖基化决定蛋白的溶解性 / 稳定性、正确折叠、生物活性、血药浓度-时间曲线（半衰期、清除）、免疫原性。

      N- vs O-glycosylation• N-glycosylation: Asn in consensus sequence Asn-X-Ser/Thr• O-glycosylation: Ser / Thr→ N-糖：Asn-X-Ser/Thr；O-糖：Ser 或 Thr 残基。

      4.2 Mock Q1：3 strategies to improve protein via glycosylation

      Strategy 1 – Add new glycosylation sites (sequence engineering)

      Introduce additional N-glycosylation consensus sequences in the protein sequence to increase sialylation and size.

      Example: Darbepoetin (EPO biobetter) – extra glycosylation sites → longer half-life.→ 策略 1：通过点突变增加 N-糖基化位点（Asn-X-Ser/Thr），提高糖基化和唾液酸水平，延长半衰期。如 Darbepoetin。

      Strategy 2 – Fusion with glycosylated domains / peptides

      Fuse therapeutic protein with a glycosylated peptide region rich in Ser/Thr.

      Example: CTP fusion (C-terminal peptide of hCG) or Fc fusion (IgG Fc is heavily glycosylated).→ 策略 2：与富含 Ser/Thr 的糖蛋白片段融合（如 CTP-hGH、Fc 融合蛋白），增加 O-/N-糖链和分子大小。

      Strategy 3 – Choose / engineer host cells for better glycosylation

      Use mammalian cells (e.g. CHO) instead of bacteria/yeast to obtain complex, sialylated glycans.

      Host cell glycoengineering to humanize glycosylation (reduce immunogenic glycans).→ 策略 3：选择或工程化宿主细胞（CHO、人细胞等），获得更接近人类的复杂糖链、减少免疫原性、改善 PK。

      4.3 Biobetters & half-life extension（mock Q2）

      • Biobetters definition
        Biobetters (biosuperiors) are improved versions of existing biological drugs, belonging to the same class but with better PK/PD, efficacy, safety, or convenience.
        → Biobetters = 在同一靶点 / 同类的改良版生物药，在 PK/PD、疗效、安全性或用药便利性上更好。
      • General strategies（可直接背 list）
        1. Modify coding sequence (mutations, fusion proteins, humanization, new glycosylation sites)
        2. Non-translational engineering (PEGylation, polymer conjugation, lipidation)
        3. Host cell engineering (glycoengineering)
        4. New formulations and routes of administration
          → 四大类策略：编码序列改造 / 非翻译修饰（PEG、聚合物、脂化）/ 宿主工程 / 新剂型与给药途径。

      4.4 Mock Q2：Extend IFN-γ half-life（2 策略示范）

      Strategy A – PEGylation

      Covalent attachment of polyethylene glycol chains increases hydrodynamic radius, reduces renal clearance, and proteolysis.

      Example: Peginterferon-alpha products.→ PEG 化：共价 PEG 修饰，增大水动力半径，减少肾清除和蛋白水解，延长半衰期。

      Strategy B – Fusion to albumin or Fc

      Create a fusion protein with human serum albumin (HSA) or IgG Fc domain.

      Benefits: larger size, FcRn recycling, longer circulation time.

      Example: Albuferon (IFN-α–albumin fusion), Fc-fusion biologics.→ 与白蛋白 / Fc 融合：增大分子、利用 FcRn 循环回收，提高半衰期，如 Albuferon。

      Erythropoietin (EPO) Case Study（mock + case PDF）

      5.1 生理作用 & 适应证

      Physiological effect of EPOEPO is a glycoprotein cytokine produced mainly by the kidney; it stimulates erythropoiesis by promoting proliferation and differentiation of erythroid progenitors in bone marrow.→ 作用：肾脏分泌的糖蛋白细胞因子，促进骨髓红系祖细胞增殖与分化，增加红细胞生成。

      Indications of rEPO therapy• Renal failure–associated anemia• Anemia of chronic disease / inflammation• Anemia associated with cancer and chemotherapy• Support of erythropoiesis (major surgery, bone marrow transplantation)→ 适应证：肾性贫血、慢性病 / 炎症性贫血、肿瘤及化疗相关贫血、大手术和造血干细胞移植中的促红细胞生成治疗。

      5.2 结构特点 & 糖基化（case Part 1）

      Structural characteristics of EPO• ~30 kDa glycoprotein, ~165 amino acids• Several N- and O-linked glycans (3 N-glycosylation + 1 O-glycosylation site)→ 结构：约 30 kDa、165 aa 的糖蛋白，含 3 个 N 糖位点 + 1 个 O 糖位点。

      Post-translational modifications• Glycosylation is the major PTM: crucial for activity, half-life & solubility.→ PTM：以糖基化为主，决定活性与 PK。

      Required host cell type for PTMEPO requires mammalian cells (e.g. CHO) providing complex, sialylated N-glycans; bacteria or simple yeasts cannot perform appropriate glycosylation.→ 必须用哺乳动物细胞（如 CHO）表达以获得合适的人样糖基化；细菌 / 简单酵母不行。

      5.3 Host cell choice（mock case Q a）

      Order of choice based on glycosylation quality

      1
      Mammalian cells (CHO, BHK, human cell lines) – complex, sialylated glycans
      2
      Insect cells – some complex glycans but less human-like
      3
      Yeast – high-mannose-type glycans (more immunogenic)
      4
      Bacteria – no glycosylation→ 选择顺序：哺乳动物细胞 > 昆虫细胞 > 酵母 > 细菌。

      答题可以简化成：

      “CHO or other mammalian cells, because they provide complex, sialylated glycosylation required for EPO activity and long half-life.”

      Vaccines & Cervarix 例题（Turbica Vaccine slides + 2022 exam）

      6.1 基本定义 & 分类

      Vaccine definition (WHO)Suspensions of killed or attenuated microorganisms, antigenic proteins, synthetic constructs or other biomolecular derivatives administered for prevention, amelioration or treatment of diseases.→ 疫苗：灭活 / 减毒微生物、抗原蛋白或其它生物大分子，用于预防或治疗疾病。

      Main vaccine strategies（课件图）• Live attenuated• Inactivated (killed)• Sub-unit (purified or recombinant antigens, polysaccharidic, conjugated)• VLP (virus-like particles)• Vector vaccines (replicating / non-replicating viral vectors)• DNA / mRNA vaccines→ 记关键词：减毒、灭活、亚单位 / 多糖 / 结合、VLP、载体疫苗、DNA、mRNA。

      6.2 Cervarix® – 2022 真题

      题干关键信息：

      L1 protein from HPV types 16 and 18

      Recombinant production in insect cells

      Aluminium adjuvant

      Q1 – Vaccine category

      Cervarix is a recombinant subunit vaccine based on VLPs (virus-like particles) composed of L1 proteins from HPV 16 and 18.

      → 分类：重组亚单位 VLP 疫苗。

      Q2 – Role of aluminium adjuvant

      Aluminium salts act as adjuvant to enhance immune response by promoting antigen uptake and activation of antigen-presenting cells and prolonging antigen release.→ 铝佐剂：增强 APC 激活和抗原递呈、延长抗原暴露，从而增强和持久化免疫应答。

      问Q3 – Why insect cells?
      答

      L1 proteins must self-assemble into VLPs with correct conformation, requiring eukaryotic folding and PTM; insect cells allow proper assembly, unlike bacteria.→ 必须在昆虫细胞等真核系统中表达，才能正确折叠并自组装成 VLP，诱导有效免疫。

      问Q4 – Where is L1 produced?
      答

      Because there is a cell lysis step in process, L1 is produced intracellularly and released after lysis.→ L1 在细胞内表达，通过细胞裂解释放。

      Q5 – Impurity categories

      直接用上面 QC 的 4 类（细菌 / 病毒；产品相关；工艺相关；宿主相关）。

      问Q6 – How many processes?
      答

      Two independent production processes (one for L1 type 16 and one for L1 type 18), then mixing and formulation.→ 两条独立工艺链，分别生产 16 型和 18 型 L1，然后混合配制。

      Somatic Cell Therapy & ATMPs（mock MCQ1 + Chaput slides）

      7.1 核心概念（MCQ 必考点）

      Somatic cellAny cell of the body except germ line cells.→ 体细胞：除生殖细胞外的所有细胞。

      Somatic cell therapyTherapeutic use of living somatic cells, autologous, allogeneic or xenogeneic, manipulated before reintroduction.→ 体细胞治疗：将处理过的体细胞（自体 / 异体 / 异种）回输，用于治疗。

      Autologous vs allogeneic• Autologous: patient’s own cells; minimal immune reaction; but individualized manufacturing for each patient.• Allogeneic: donor cells; easier large-scale production and “off-the-shelf”; but requires HLA compatibility and immunosuppression.→ 自体：免疫排斥少，但每人一批；异体：可规模化和库存，但存在免疫排斥和 HLA 匹配问题。

      Cell-based therapyAdministration of living cells to patients.→ 细胞治疗 = 给病人输入活细胞。

      7.2 ATMP 分类（必背 3 大类）

      Viral Vectors for Gene Therapy（Bonte slides）

      8.1 基本概念

      Viral vector definitionGenetically modified virus used to transfer and express its own or foreign genes.→ 病毒载体：经过基因改造，用于递送并表达目标基因的病毒。

      Main objectives of viral vectors• Protein production in cell culture• Vaccination• Gene therapy• Anticancer (oncolytic) applications→ 应用：重组蛋白生产、疫苗、基因治疗、溶瘤治疗。

      8.2 Desired characteristics（背三大块）

      Targeting & expression• Adaptable cellular tropism, high transduction efficiency• Controllable expression level and stability (integration vs episomal)

      Safety• Minimal toxicity & immunogenicity• Low recombination & low RCV (replication competent virus) generation

      Production• Easy and cost-effective production• High viral titer achievable→ 三大块：靶向 + 表达 / 安全性 / 生产性。

      8.4 QC of viral vectors（大题可能会让你列）

      Identity & purity• DNA identity: sequencing, restriction map• Physical titration: photometry• Biological titration: plaque assays, cytopathic effect, (RT-)PCR• Empty capsid assessment: photometry or density gradients

      Activity• In vitro transgene transfer efficiency• Expression of protein of interest: SDS-PAGE, Western blot, ELISA, immunocytochemistry• Functional studies in cell / animal models

      Safety / sterility• Sterility tests for bacteria, fungi• Endotoxin (LAL)• Mycoplasma detection (culture or PCR)

      Extracellular Vesicles（EV，Obeid 课）

      12.1 定义 & 分类（必背）

      Definition

      Extracellular vesicles are membrane-enclosed nanoparticles released by almost all cell types and found in all body fluids.

      → 细胞外囊泡是几乎所有细胞都会释放、存在于各种体液中的膜性纳米颗粒。

      Main classes of EVs（记 3 类 + 尺寸 + 来源）

      Exosomes – 30–150 nm, derived from endosomal multivesicular bodies (MVBs) that fuse with the plasma membrane.→ 外泌体：约 30–150 nm，来源于多囊泡体（MVB）与细胞膜融合后释放。

      Microvesicles / Microparticles – 100–1000 nm, formed by outward budding and fission of the plasma membrane.→ 微囊泡 / 微粒：约 100–1000 nm，由细胞膜外向出芽形成。

      Apoptotic bodies – 500–5000 nm, released by blebbing of apoptotic cells.→ 凋亡小体：约 500–5000 nm，由凋亡细胞起泡、断裂产生。

      Typical markers（只要举几个）

      Exosomes: tetraspanins (CD9, CD63, CD81), ESCRT proteins (Alix, TSG101), Rab GTPases, HSPs.→ 外泌体：四跨膜蛋白（CD9/63/81）、ESCRT 蛋白（Alix、TSG101）等。

      Microvesicles: integrins, selectins, phosphatidylserine (Annexin V binding).→ 微囊泡：富含整合素、选择素、外翻磷脂酰丝氨酸（可被 Annexin V 识别）。

      Apoptotic bodies: nuclear fragments, histones, chromatin.→ 凋亡小体：常含有核碎片、组蛋白、染色质。

      考点模式：

      “Name the three main types of EVs and their origin.”

      → 答：exosomes（endosomal）、microvesicles（plasma membrane budding）、apoptotic bodies（apoptotic blebbing）。

      12.2 组成 & 生物学功能

      Composition

      EVs contain a specific combination of lipids, proteins, and nucleic acids that reflect the cell of origin.

      → EV 携带的脂质、蛋白和核酸具有**“来源细胞指纹”**。

      Lipids: cholesterol, sphingomyelin, phosphatidylserine-rich membrane.→ 脂质：胆固醇、鞘磷脂、磷脂酰丝氨酸等。

      Proteins: tetraspanins, ESCRT components, integrins, adhesion molecules, enzymes.→ 蛋白：四跨膜蛋白、ESCRT 成分、整合素、黏附分子、酶等。

      Nucleic acids: mRNA, miRNA, lncRNA, rRNA, and sometimes DNA.→ 核酸：mRNA、miRNA、长链非编码 RNA、rRNA，甚至 DNA 片段。

      Functions

      EVs mediate short- and long-distance intercellular communication by transferring bioactive cargo to recipient cells.

      → EV 通过“运送货物”（蛋白/脂质/核酸）实现近距离和远距离的细胞间通信。

      They can:

      Modulate gene expression and signaling in target cells.→ 调节受体细胞的基因表达和信号通路。

      Participate in immune regulation, angiogenesis, coagulation, tissue repair, and tumor progression.→ 参与免疫调节、血管生成、凝血、组织修复以及肿瘤进展等。

      12.3 EV-based therapy vs Cell-based therapy（优缺点比较）

      Advantages of EV-based therapy

      EVs are non-replicating and cannot divide, reducing the risk of tumor formation compared to cell therapy.→ EV 不会增殖，相比细胞治疗肿瘤形成风险更低。

      Lower immunogenicity and easier to store, freeze, and standardize.→ 免疫原性相对较低，更易于冷冻保存与标准化生产。

      Better tissue penetration due to their small size and ability to cross biological barriers.→ 体积小，可穿越多种生物屏障，组织渗透性更好。

      Limitations / Challenges

      Heterogeneity in EV populations and difficulty to fully define composition.→ 群体高度异质，难以完全表征。

      Large-scale, GMP-compliant production and purification are technically challenging.→ 放大生产和 GMP 纯化难度大。

      Lack of harmonized regulatory guidelines compared to classical biologics.→ 相比传统生物药，监管框架尚不完善。

      典型问法：

      “Cite two advantages of EV-based therapies compared to cell-based therapies, and one major challenge.”

      → 优点：更安全（不增殖）、免疫原性低、易保存 / 标准化、组织渗透性好；

      → 挑战：大规模生产和纯化困难、群体异质、缺乏统一监管标准。

      12.4 EV 的临床应用潜力（生物标志物 & 治疗）

      EVs as biomarkers

      Because EVs circulate in blood and other fluids and carry cell-type-specific cargo, they are attractive non-invasive biomarkers (“liquid biopsy”).

      → EV 存在于血液等体液中、携带来源细胞特异“指纹”，非常适合作为无创液体活检生物标志物。

      They have been associated with many diseases (cancer, cardiovascular, neurodegenerative and inflammatory diseases).

      → 在肿瘤、心血管、神经退行性、炎症性疾病中均有数量或组成改变。

      Therapeutic use（记 3 大类）

      Therapeutic EVs can be:

      1
      Native EVs from therapeutic cells (e.g. MSC-derived exosomes with anti-inflammatory properties).→ 来自治疗性细胞（如 MSC）的“天然 EV”，本身具有免疫调节 / 组织修复作用。
      2
      EVs from genetically modified cells expressing specific therapeutic proteins or RNAs.→ 来源细胞经过基因修饰，使 EV 携带特定蛋白或 RNA。
      3
      Engineered EVs loaded with drugs or nucleic acids (e.g. siRNA, miRNA mimics).→ 后期工程化装载小分子药物或核酸（siRNA、miRNA 模拟物等）的 EV。

      可以背一句：

      “EVs can be used both as diagnostic biomarkers in liquid biopsy and as therapeutic carriers delivering proteins, lipids, or nucleic acids to target tissues.”

      → EV 既可作为液体活检的诊断标志物，也可作为递送蛋白 / 脂质 / 核酸的治疗载体。

      12.5 EV 的制备与纯化方法（一句话记忆）

      Main isolation methods (exam-style list)

      Differential ultracentrifugation and density gradient centrifugation.→ 差速 + 密度梯度离心。

      Size-exclusion chromatography (SEC).→ 体积排阻色谱（SEC）。

      Ultrafiltration / tangential-flow filtration (TFF).→ 超滤 / 切向流过滤。

      Polymer-based precipitation methods.→ 聚合物沉淀法。

      Immunoaffinity capture and microfluidic-based platforms.→ 免疫亲和捕获、微流控平台。

      一句：

      “EV isolation methods combine size-based, density-based and affinity-based strategies such as ultracentrifugation, SEC, ultrafiltration and immunoaffinity capture.”

      → EV 纯化一般结合粒径、密度和亲和性原理，如超速离心、SEC、超滤和免疫亲和捕获。

      12.6 EV 的表征 & 质控（QC）

      Physical characterization

      Size distribution and concentration: Nanoparticle Tracking Analysis (NTA), Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS), Tunable Resistive Pulse Sensing (TRPS).→ 物理表征：NTA、DLS、TRPS 测粒径和浓度。

      Morphology: Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM), cryo-EM, Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM).→ 形态：透射电镜、冷冻电镜、原子力显微镜。

      Molecular characterization

      Surface and cargo proteins: Western blot, flow cytometry, mass spectrometry.→ 蛋白质：Western blot、流式细胞术、质谱。

      Nucleic acids: qPCR, RNA-seq for miRNA/mRNA.→ 核酸：qPCR、RNA-测序。

      Purity & safety

      Assess co-isolated proteins, lipoproteins, and non-EV particles; test for sterility and endotoxin.→ 纯度与安全：评估共分离的蛋白 / 脂蛋白等杂质，并进行无菌与内毒素检测。

      可能的简答模板：

      “To characterize EVs, we combine nanoparticle tracking analysis and electron microscopy for size and morphology, Western blot or flow cytometry for EV markers, and nucleic-acid-based methods to analyse their RNA cargo.”

      → EV 表征通常结合 NTA+电镜看粒径和形态，WB/流式看标志蛋白，再用核酸检测方法分析 RNA 货物。

      13.1 Ion-exchange chromatography（IEX）原理（EPO 纯化常考）

      Definition & principle Ion-exchange chromatography separates proteins according to their net charge at a given pH by reversible binding to oppositely charged groups on the resin. → 离子交换色谱根据蛋白在特定 pH 下的净电荷差异来分离，通过与载体上**相反电荷的官能团可逆结合。

      How it works（一句话骨架） The protein of interest binds to the charged resin, impurities are washed out, then the protein is eluted by increasing salt concentration or changing pH. → 目标蛋白先吸附在带电填料上，杂质被洗脱掉，然后通过升高盐浓度或改变 pH把目标蛋白解吸洗脱下来。

      13.2 SDS-PAGE：原理 + 如何用来看纯度

      Principle SDS-PAGE separates proteins mainly according to their molecular weight: SDS coats proteins with a uniform negative charge, so separation depends on size when they migrate in the polyacrylamide gel. → SDS-PAGE 主要按分子量分离蛋白：SDS 给蛋白包上一层均一负电荷，在聚丙烯酰胺凝胶中迁移速度主要取决于大小。

      Purity assessment from a gel A pure protein gives a single band at the expected molecular weight, whereas impurities appear as additional bands at different sizes or as smears. → 单一、清晰的一条带（在预期分子量位置）代表比较纯；多条带或拖尾 / 抹带说明存在其他蛋白或降解产物。

      如果考题给三条泳道（supernatant / intermediate / final）：

      Crude supernatant: many bands → very impure.

      Intermediate: fewer bands → increased purity.

      Final purified: single major band → high purity. → 用话术：“从左到右杂带减少，最终样品只剩一条主带，说明纯度逐步提高。”

      13.3 如何证明“无某种微生物污染”（sterility / specific test）

      Generic answer To verify that the batch is not contaminated with a given micro-organism, we perform sterility tests by incubating the product in growth media, and we can combine this with a specific detection method such as PCR or immunoassay targeting this organism. → 要证明批次没有被某种微生物污染，可以：

      1
      做无菌检查：在适当培养基中培养，观察是否有生长；
      2
      再加一个特异性检测（如针对该微生物的 PCR 或免疫检测）。

      你答题时可以写成两点：

      Sterility test in appropriate culture media.

      Specific PCR / immunological assay for this microorganism. → 无菌检查 + 针对该菌的 PCR / 免疫检测。

    The concept of transformation - The Giffrick experiment

    • 1928: Frederick Griffith demonstrate transformation in Streptococcus pneumoniae
    • Pathogenicity: Due to key enzyme involved in the synthesis of bacterial capsule
    • Two different alleles:
      • Smooth allele (S): smooth surface → pathogenic
      • Rough allele (R) → rough surface → non-pathogenic
      Treatment 1
      Living S strain (pathogenic) → Die
      Treatment 2
      Living R strain (non-pathogenic) → Lives
      Treatment 3
      Heat treated S strain → Lives
      Treatment 4
      Heat treated S + Living R → Dies

      → A chemical substance from 1 cell has the ability to transform another cell

      DNA is the nature of the transforming substance

      1944
      Avery, MacLeod & McCarty

      Culture S. pneumoniae (S strain - pathogenic)

      Heat kill, homogenize + R strain → Transformation occurs

      Treat with protease + R strain → Transformation occurs

      Treat with ribonuclease + R strain → Transformation occurs

      Treat with deoxyribonuclease + R strain → Don’t transform

      → The chemical nature of the transforming substance is DNA

                                        Two types of glycosylation

                                        N-glycosylation

                                        Happens on Asparagine (Asn).

                                        Requires the motif Asn-X-Ser/Thr (X ≠ Pro).

                                        Starts in the ER, continues in the Golgi.

                                        A big sugar tree is added all at once (Glc₃Man₉GlcNAc₂).

                                        All N-glycans share a common core (Man₃GlcNAc₂).

                                        Glycans are large and branched.

                                        Common on antibodies (Fc region), hormones, secreted proteins.

                                        Important for
                                        folding, stability, trafficking, immune effector functions, half-life.

                                        Very important in QC: glycan mapping, CZE, HILIC, MS.

                                        Strong impact on PK/PD.

                                        The 3 Types of N-Glycans

                                        • Oligomannose N-glycans
                                        • Mostly mannose residues on the core.
                                        • The structure stays “mannose-rich.”
                                        • Not many additional sugars (Gal, GlcNAc, Sialic acid).
                                        • Early glycan processing stage.
                                        • Complex N-glycans
                                          • Mannose residues are trimmed
                                          • The glycan is extended with:
                                            • GlcNAc
                                            • Galactose
                                            • Sialic acid
                                            • Fucose

                                            One branch looks like high-mannose

                                            The other branch looks complex

                                            O-glycosylation

                                            Happens on Serine or Threonine (Ser/Thr).

                                            No specific consensus sequence.

                                            Occurs only in the Golgi.

                                            Sugars are added one-by-one, not pre-assembled.

                                            The first sugar is GalNAc.

                                            O-glycans are shorter and more variable.

                                            Common in mucins, membrane glycoproteins, some receptors.

                                            Important for
                                            protease resistance, local stability, cell–cell interactions.

                                            QC relies on glycopeptide LC-MS/MS, or β-elimination to release O-glycans.

                                            More limited impact on PK but important for local structure and protection.

                                            问How do charge variant analysis of monoclonal antibodies by IEX-HPLC contribute to their QC?
                                            答
                                            • Charge variant analysis of mAbs
                                              • mAbs are heterogeneous molecules. Even if they have the same amino acid sequence, they can differ in charge because of:
                                                • PTMs (deamidation, C-terminal lysine clipping, glycosylation, sialylation)
                                                • Chemical degradation (oxidation, N-terminal pyroglutamate)
                                                • Manufacturing conditions (temperature, pH, cell culture process)

                                                Separate molecules based on net surface charge

                                                Cation exchange (CEX): binds positively charged variants (common for mAbs) (column is negatively charged)

                                                Anion exchange (AEX): binds negatively charged variants (column is positively charged)

                                                Elutes variants from weakest to strongest binding

                                                Confirms product identity & consistency: Each commercial mAb has a characteristic charge-variant profile → Ensure batch-to-batch consistency

                                                Detect critical quality attributes (CQAs): Many charge variants directly affect the potency, stability & immunogenicity (acidic variants may indicate fragmentation, basic variants may indicate amidation or incomplete processing)

                                                Monitor degradation during manufacturing & storage: Detect changes caused by temperature stress, freeze-thaw cycles, oxidation → Used in stability studies

                                            问What does size exclusion chromatography (SEC-HPLC) bring to the purity control of therapeutic proteins?
                                            答
                                            • Size exclusion chromatography:
                                              • Diffusion through a column matrix of porous beads (no adsorption)
                                              • Bigger molecules can only move around the beads at a slow pace → travel slower
                                              • Smaller molecules can travel around and between the pores of the beads → travel faster
                                              • Enables detection of impurities that can affect the safety & stability of proteins
                                              • Detection with UV detector → Chromatogram with distinct peaks
                                              • Strong reproducibility, no need binding interactions
                                              • Lower selectivity compared to other methods, limited separation for similar-sized proteins

                                              Quantification of percentage of monomer vs. impurities

                                              Detection of aggregation (dimer, trimer, or oligomer formation)

                                              Detection of protein fragmentation

                                            问What kind of chemical degradation variants of therapeutic proteins can be controlled by RP-HPLC?
                                            答
                                            • RP-HPLC:
                                              • Reverse-phase high performance liquid chromatography
                                              • Uses hydrophobic stationary phase & hydrophilic mobile phase
                                              • Separation mechanism:
                                                • For small molecules:
                                                  • Continuous partitioning, separated based on hydrophobic interaction
                                                  • Hydrophilic molecules elute first, more hydrophobic ones elute later

                                                  Adsorb to the hydrophobic phase

                                                  Desorb from the surface at critical organic modifier concentration → gradient elution

                                                  Can detect chemical degradation variants that alter protein hydrophobicity, such as oxidation, deamidation, isomerization, N/C-terminal clipping, peptide-bond hydrolysis, and certain soluble aggregates or glycation forms → Appear as shifted or additional peaks in the chromatogram and are key QC markers in therapeutic protein analysis

                                            答

                                              • A rapid protein fingerprinting method
                                              • Uses an analytical technique to characterize the primary structure of proteins
                                              • The analyzed therapeutic protein is denatured, linearized, then digested with enzymatic reactions
                                              • RP-HPLC is performed, then the peak heights & location are compared to the reference sequence to verify protein identity
                                              • Protocol:
                                                • Evaporate & dissolve IgG

                                            问Why can capillary gel electrophoresis be used for both identity and purity control of therapeutic proteins?
                                            答

                                              • Separates proteins by their molecular size in a sieving polymer matrix
                                              • Similar to other electrophoresis techniques but with higher resolution
                                              • This size-based separation allows CGE to verify what the protein is (identity) and which impurities are present (purity)

                                                • High resolution: detection of low-abundance variants
                                                • Highly quantitative: directly reflects mass fraction, enabling precise purity determination
