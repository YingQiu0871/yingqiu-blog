Exam format and time plan (confirmed by the professor)

2小时；两部分：固体剂型 + 液体剂型；每部分：若干短题 + 1道综合题；闭卷。EN: 2 hours; two parts: solid forms + liquid forms; each part: short questions + one problem; closed-book.

推荐分配：0–30 min 做固体短题；30–60 min 做液体短题；60–105 min 固体大题；105–150 min 液体大题；最后10 min 全卷检查。EN: Suggested time: 0–30 min solid short Qs; 30–60 min liquid short Qs; 60–105 min solid problem; 105–150 min liquid problem; last 10 min check.
中文
闭卷写法：所有计算题必须写单位；所有机理题必须写“因果链（原因→影响→对策/结论）”。EN: Closed-book writing: always show units in calculations; for mechanism questions write a causal chain (cause → effect → fix/conclusion).

Lectures 1–3 essentials (必须背：Drug cycle + Biopharmacy + strategy)

中文
药物生命周期（Drug cycle）= 从想法/发现 → 研发与临床 → 上市 → 市场与药物警戒（pharmacovigilance）→ 专利到期/仿制药竞争。EN: Drug cycle = from discovery → development/clinical → launch → marketing & pharmacovigilance → patent expiry/generics.
中文
关键阶段（按答题顺序写）：Discovery（筛选/优化候选）→ Pre-clinical（体外/动物：有效性+ADME+毒理）→ Clinical phases I–III（人体：安全性→剂量→有效性）→ Registration/Authorization → Launch。EN: Stages to cite: Discovery → Preclinical (in vitro/in vivo; ADME & tox) → Clinical I–III → Authorization → Launch.
中文
专利一句话：专利通常在研发早期申请；专利期内才能回本；到期后仿制药进入，价格下降。EN: Patent: filed early; exclusivity enables profitability; after expiry generics enter and price drops.
中文
药物警戒一句话：上市后持续收集/评估不良反应，更新风险控制（Phase IV）。EN: Pharmacovigilance = post-marketing safety monitoring and risk management (Phase IV).
中文
制剂设计的总目标：把药以可接受的方式送到作用部位，疗效最大、副作用最小。EN: Formulation aim: deliver drug to site of action with maximal efficacy and minimal side effects.
中文
口服特点（常考短答）：优点—方便、可固体/液体、大剂量；缺点—pH/酶降解、吸收部位差异、首过效应。EN: Oral route: convenient (solid/liquid, large doses) but subject to pH/enzymes, variable absorption site, and first-pass metabolism.
中文
生物利用度（bioavailability）答题模板：F 受“释放/溶出（in solution）+ 渗透过膜 + 代谢/外排”共同限制。EN: Bioavailability is limited by dissolution/release, permeability, and metabolism/efflux.
中文
BCS 四类（必须能写全）：I 高溶解/高渗透；II 低溶解/高渗透；III 高溶解/低渗透；IV 低/低。EN: BCS: I (high S/high P), II (low S/high P), III (high S/low P), IV (low S/low P).
中文
BCS→策略（最赚分）：II 类优先‘提溶出/提溶解度’（减粒径、盐型、无定形、表活/固分散）；III 类优先‘提渗透/减少外排’（促渗、抑制外排、前药/改途径）。EN: Strategy: BCS II → boost solubility/dissolution (size reduction, salts, amorphous, surfactant/solid dispersion); BCS III → boost permeability/limit efflux (enhancers, efflux inhibition, prodrug/route change).

Part A — Solid forms

A-Prep. Preformulation of solid forms (固体前处方：粉体/晶型/稳定性) — short questions

中文
前处方的目的：在设计制剂与工艺前，先搞清 API 的关键固体性质（溶出、流动、压片性、稳定性）→ 决定配方与工艺。EN: Preformulation clarifies solid API properties (dissolution, flow, compactability, stability) to choose formulation + process.
中文
粒径分布（PSD）为什么重要：粒径↓ → 溶出↑；但太细 → 静电/团聚、流动差、易分层。EN: PSD matters: smaller particles ↑ dissolution; too fine may worsen flow and increase agglomeration/segregation.
中文
粒径测定（会选/会写）：Sieving（>40 μm，需样品量大）；Laser diffraction（0.1–2000 μm，快、重复性好）；Microscopy/SEM（看形态）。EN: Particle size: sieving (>40 μm), laser diffraction (0.1–2000 μm), microscopy/SEM (shape).
中文
晶型/固态性质：晶体结构决定熔点、溶解度、溶出速率、机械性质；多晶型/水合物/无定形会显著改变这些参数。EN: Solid state: crystal form controls melting point, solubility, dissolution rate, and mechanical properties; polymorphs/hydrates/amorphous forms can change them markedly.
中文
粉体密度与流动（必须会算/会判断）：bulk density ρb、tapped density ρt；Carr index = (ρt−ρb)/ρt×100%；Hausner ratio = ρt/ρb。Carr 小、Hausner 接近 1 → 流动更好。EN: Flow indices: ρb, ρt; Carr = (ρt−ρb)/ρt×100%; Hausner = ρt/ρb. Lower Carr and Hausner closer to 1 indicate better flow.
中文
含水与吸湿性：水分会影响流动、压片、降解（如水解）；需要控制干燥/包装与储存条件。EN: Moisture/hygroscopicity affects flow, compaction, and degradation (e.g., hydrolysis); control drying, packaging, storage.
中文
相容性/稳定性筛查：API+辅料混合后做加速应力（温度/湿度/光）；常用 DSC/XRPD/FTIR 看晶型变化与相互作用。EN: Compatibility/stability: stress API–excipient blends (T/RH/light); use DSC/XRPD/FTIR to detect solid-state changes/interactions.

A0. One master chain (use this to explain any oral solid issue)

中文
口服固体制剂表现 = 崩解/释放 → 溶出（进入溶液态）→ 吸收（过膜）→ 暴露/疗效。任何“BA低/起效慢/疗效差”先从这条链拆原因。
EN
Oral solid performance = disintegration/release → dissolution (must be in solution) → absorption (membrane crossing) → exposure/efficacy. Use this chain to diagnose any issue.

A1. Dissolution (溶出) — equation, sink, and method

中文
核心方程（背！）：dC/dt = (D·A/h)·(Cs − Ct)。D扩散系数；A表面积；h扩散层厚度；(Cs−Ct)为驱动力。EN: Core equation: dC/dt = (D·A/h)·(Cs − Ct). D diffusion; A surface area; h boundary layer; (Cs−Ct) driving force.
中文
提速三招（背！）：A↑（粒径↓/比表面积↑）；Cs↑（调pH/盐型/表活/无定形/固分）；h↓（搅拌↑/流体动力学更强）。EN: Speed-up levers: increase A (smaller particles); increase Cs (pH/salt/surfactant/amorphous/solid dispersion); decrease h (stronger agitation/hydrodynamics).
中文
Sink condition：实验介质需保证最终浓度远低于 Cs（常用 Cs/3，或更严格 Cs/10）。否则溶出变慢，曲线难区分。EN: Sink condition: keep final concentration far below Cs (often Cs/3 or stricter Cs/10). Otherwise dissolution slows and profiles lose discrimination.
中文
溶出装置（认识即可）：basket、paddle、flow-through cell；条件常见：37°C；转速 50–100 rpm更有区分度；必要时脱气。EN: Dissolution apparatus: basket, paddle, flow-through cell; common conditions: 37°C; 50–100 rpm is more discriminating; degas if needed.
中文
介质与释放类型：IR常用单一pH；肠溶/延迟释放常用“两步法”（酸段2h→pH 6.8段）；缓释需多个时间点描述释放窗口。EN: Medium & release: IR uses single pH; enteric/delayed often uses 2-step (acid 2h → pH 6.8); ER requires multiple time points defining release window.
中文
表面活性剂：可用于润湿，但方法学上需避免“增溶掩盖问题”；常写成“use surfactant below CMC for wetting”。EN: Surfactants: can improve wetting; avoid masking by excessive solubilization; write 'use surfactant below CMC for wetting'.

A2. Disintegration vs dissolution vs absorption (short-question answers)

中文
如果崩解慢：崩解剂不足/类型不当；过度润滑（Mg stearate包覆）；片剂太硬/孔隙率低；包衣阻水；黏合剂过多。EN: If disintegration is slow: insufficient/wrong disintegrant; over-lubrication (Mg stearate coating); too hard/low porosity; hydrophobic coating; too much binder.
中文
如果崩解正常但溶出慢：通常是Cs低（难溶/晶型）或A不够（粒径大）或h大（搅拌弱），而不是崩解问题。EN: If disintegration OK but dissolution slow: usually low Cs (poor solubility/polymorph), low A (large particles), or large h (weak hydrodynamics).
中文
如果溶出快但吸收差：多考虑渗透性/外排（P-gp）/代谢首过；策略：促渗、前药、改变给药途径。EN: If dissolution is fast but absorption poor: consider permeability/efflux/first-pass; strategies: permeation enhancers, prodrugs, alternative routes.

A3. Tabletting — key definitions + Heckel

中文
Compression：压力使粉床致密化形成片剂。Compressibility：体积/孔隙率减少能力。Compactability：形成足够机械强度能力。EN: Compression: densification under stress. Compressibility: ability to reduce volume/porosity. Compactability: ability to build mechanical strength.
中文
Heckel：ln(1/(1−D)) = K·P + A。D相对密度，P压力。用途：比较材料塑性/脆性变形趋势（写出‘线性段’即可）。EN: Heckel: ln(1/(1−D)) = K·P + A (D relative density, P pressure). Use: compare plastic/brittle densification (mention linear region).
中文
压片四阶段写法：重排/碎裂 → 塑性变形 → 弹性变形 → 卸压回弹（回弹过大→capping/lamination）。EN: 4-stage writing: rearrangement/fragmentation → plastic deformation → elastic deformation → recovery (excess recovery → capping/lamination).

A4. Tablet QC — calculations you must be able to do

中文
抗张强度：σ = 2F/(π·D·e)。F(N)，D(mm)，e(mm)。注意单位一致。EN: Tensile strength: σ = 2F/(π·D·e). Keep consistent units (F in N; D,e in mm).
中文
脆碎度：F% = (m0−m)/m0 × 100；常用标准：F% < 1%。EN: Friability: F% = (m0−m)/m0 × 100; typical limit: < 1%.
中文
必须能写出 QC 清单（短题常考）：外观、片重差异、含量/均匀度、硬度/强度、脆碎度、崩解、溶出、必要时水分。EN: You must list QC tests: appearance, weight variation, assay/content uniformity, hardness/strength, friability, disintegration, dissolution, moisture if needed.

A5. Common tablet defects (short answers)

中文
Capping/lamination：空气夹带、弹性回弹、压力释放太快、含水不当；对策：预压、降低转速、优化含水、改造粒。EN: Capping/lamination: trapped air, elastic recovery, too fast decompression, wrong moisture; fixes: pre-compression, lower speed, optimize moisture, granulate.
中文
Sticking/picking：潮湿、润滑不足、冲头表面问题；对策：干燥/降粘、加润滑、抛光或换冲头。EN: Sticking/picking: moisture, insufficient lubrication, punch surface; fixes: dry/adjust binder, add lubricant, polish/change tooling.

A6. Blending (混合) — ordered mix and segregation

中文
Random mix vs ordered mix：随机混合靠概率均匀；有序混合靠细粉吸附到大颗粒（低剂量API常用）。EN: Random mix is probabilistic uniformity; ordered mix is adhesion of fines on carriers (useful for low-dose API).
中文
离析三原因（必背）：trajectories（粒径/密度导致运动轨迹不同）、percolation（细粉下渗）、elutriation（细粉被气流带走）。EN: 3 segregation causes: trajectories, percolation of fines, elutriation by airflow.
中文
润滑剂要点（高分句）：Mg stearate 过度混合会包覆颗粒表面→压片结合变差/硬度下降/溶出变慢；因此通常最后加入且短时间混合。EN: High-score line: overmixing Mg stearate coats particles → weaker bonding, lower hardness, slower dissolution; therefore add last and mix briefly.

A7. Granulation (制粒) — S* and wetting states

中文
S*（饱和度）= V_solution / V_interparticle。区间：<0.25 成核；≥0.25 稳定生长；0.9–1.0 crumb growth；≥1 过湿成浆。EN: S* = V_solution / V_interparticle. Ranges: <0.25 nucleation; ≥0.25 steady growth; 0.9–1.0 crumb growth; ≥1 slurry/overwetting.
中文
液桥/润湿状态写法：pendular（少量液桥）→ funicular（孔隙接近填满）→ capillary（完全填满）→ droplet（过湿）。EN: Wetting states: pendular → funicular → capillary → droplet (overwet).
中文
湿法制粒大题模板：目的→方法选择→粘结剂与喷液→混合/剪切→干燥（流化床/烘箱）→整粒→关键参数（S*、功率曲线、含水）。EN: Wet granulation problem template: goal → method → binder & spraying → mixing/shear → drying → milling/sieving → CPPs (S*, power curve, moisture).

A8. Freeze drying (冻干) — full bilingual answer you can memorize

中文
冻干三步：Freezing → Primary drying（升华去除冰，主要水分）→ Secondary drying（脱附去除结合水，降低残余水分）。
EN
3 steps: Freezing → Primary drying (sublimation removes ice, most water) → Secondary drying (desorption removes bound water, lowers residual moisture).
中文
关键温度：结晶体系看 Te（共晶温度）；无定形体系看 Tg'；超过限制温度会软化/融化并导致 collapse（塌陷）。EN: Key temperatures: crystalline system uses Te; amorphous system uses Tg'; exceeding limits causes softening/melting and collapse.
中文
必须写出的控制句：Primary drying 时产品温度必须低于 Tc（或低于 Te/Tg' 对应限制温度），否则孔结构塌陷→复溶变慢→质量下降。EN: Mandatory control sentence: during primary drying, product temperature must stay below Tc (or Te/Tg' limit); otherwise pore collapse slows reconstitution and degrades quality.
中文
三阶段与关键控制（综合题写法）—①Freezing：控制冷却速率与成核→冰晶大小→孔结构。快冻→小冰晶→孔小、升华阻力大但结构细；慢冻→大冰晶→孔大、更易干燥但结构粗。EN: 3 stages & controls (problem writing)—(1) Freezing: control cooling rate & nucleation → crystal size → pore structure. Fast freezing → small crystals → small pores, higher resistance but finer structure; slow freezing → large crystals → large pores, easier drying but coarser.
中文
②Primary drying（升华）：控制 shelf temperature + chamber pressure；提高 shelf T 可加快升华，但必须避免产品温度超过 Tc/Te/Tg'。EN: (2) Primary drying (sublimation): control shelf temperature + chamber pressure; higher shelf T speeds drying but must not push product T above Tc/Te/Tg'.
中文
③Secondary drying：升温去除结合水→残余水分降低→稳定性提高（抗水解/抗氧化）；对生物大分子避免过热失活。EN: (3) Secondary drying: raise temperature to remove bound water → lower residual moisture → better stability; avoid overheating biomolecules.

Solid-forms 'problem' universal template (write this in the exam)

中文
写作顺序：1) 目标（IR/ER/enteric + CQA）；2) API性质（solubility/pKa/logP/稳定性/剂量）；3) 选择工艺（混合→制粒→压片→包衣/冻干）；4) 辅料（作用：稀释/黏合/崩解/润滑/助流/包衣聚合物）；5) CPP（S*、压片压力、混合时间、包衣增重、冻干T/P）；6) QC（片重/含量/硬度/脆碎/崩解/溶出/稳定性）。EN: Writing order: (1) goal (IR/ER/enteric + CQAs); (2) API properties; (3) process route; (4) excipients & functions; (5) CPPs; (6) QC tests.

Part B — Liquid forms

B1. Preformulation checklist (liquids)

中文
必须检查：溶解度随pH变化、pKa、logP/logD、化学稳定性（氧化/水解/光）、物理稳定性（沉降/絮凝/破乳）、微生物风险与防腐剂、包装相容性。EN: Check: solubility vs pH, pKa, logP/logD, chemical stability (oxidation/hydrolysis/light), physical stability, microbiology & preservatives, container compatibility.
中文
溶解度不足的‘六联招’（闭卷背）：调pH/盐型/共溶剂/表活胶束/络合(环糊精)/改为混悬或干粉复溶。EN: 6 solubility levers: pH, salt, cosolvent, surfactant micelles, complexation (cyclodextrin), switch to suspension or dry powder for reconstitution.

B2. pKa and ionization (short Q writing)

中文
必背句：弱酸 pH > pKa 更电离；弱碱 pH < pKa 更电离。电离↑→水溶性↑；但电离↑→膜渗透性↓。EN: Memorize: weak acids ionize at pH > pKa; weak bases ionize at pH < pKa. More ionization → higher solubility but lower permeability.
中文
题型：给 pH 与 pKa，让你判断主要形态，并解释为什么调 pH 能提高溶解度（因为增加电离比例）。EN: Typical: given pH and pKa, decide dominant form; explain why pH adjustment increases solubility (increases ionized fraction).

B3. Tonicity (isotonicity) — calculation template

中文
等渗目的：减少注射/滴眼刺激，避免溶血；参照 0.9% NaCl。EN: Isotonicity: reduce irritation; avoid hemolysis; reference 0.9% NaCl.
中文
E-value算法模板：先算 API 的 NaCl 当量贡献（C_API×E），再用 0.9%−该贡献 = 需要补加的 NaCl 当量；再按等渗剂当量换算质量。EN: E-value template: isotonic contribution of API (C_API×E), remainder = 0.9% minus contribution, then convert using tonicity agent equivalent.

B4. Suspensions — Stokes and redispersibility

中文
Stokes：V = 2r^2(ρ_p−ρ_m)g/(9η)。让沉降慢：r↓、η↑、密度差↓。EN: Stokes: V = 2r^2(ρ_p−ρ_m)g/(9η). Reduce V by r↓, η↑, density difference↓.
中文
Flocculated vs deflocculated：絮凝沉降快但松散易再分散；非絮凝沉降慢但易结硬沉淀(caking)。EN: Flocculated settles faster but redisperses; deflocculated settles slower but may cake.
中文
综合题写法：先写‘控制粒径 + 提高黏度 + 控制絮凝 + 防腐’四件套，再写 QC：粒径分布、黏度、沉降体积比、再分散性、含量、微生物限度。EN: Problem writing: control size + increase viscosity + control flocculation + preservation; QC: size distribution, viscosity, sedimentation volume, redispersibility, assay, microbial limits.

B5. Emulsions — O/W vs W/O, HLB, and instability

中文
O/W：水为连续相；W/O：油为连续相。Bancroft rule：表活更溶于哪相，哪相更可能为连续相。EN: O/W water continuous; W/O oil continuous. Bancroft: phase where surfactant is more soluble becomes continuous.
中文
HLB：用于选择乳化剂（高HLB偏O/W，低HLB偏W/O）。混合：HLB_mix = Σ(f_i·HLB_i)。EN: HLB selects emulsifiers (higher HLB favors O/W; lower favors W/O). Blend rule: HLB_mix = Σ(f_i·HLB_i).
中文
失稳四件套：creaming、flocculation、coalescence、breaking。对策模板：粒径↓、黏度↑、合适表活（类型+浓度）、避免温度循环。EN: Instability: creaming, flocculation, coalescence, breaking. Fix template: smaller droplets, higher viscosity, right surfactant type/concentration, avoid thermal cycling.
中文
界面张力测量（可能作为加分题）：Vonnegut（旋转滴）γ = ω^2Δρ d^3 / 32（认识即可，重点是‘表活降低γ→更易形成乳滴’）。EN: Interfacial tension bonus: Vonnegut (spinning drop) γ = ω^2Δρ d^3 / 32; key idea: surfactants lower γ → easier droplet formation.

B6. Microemulsion vs nanoemulsion (short Q)

中文
Microemulsion：热力学稳定、透明/各向同性、可自发形成，但需高表活/助表活；Nanoemulsion：动力学稳定，通常高能量制备（高压均质）。EN: Microemulsion: thermodynamically stable, transparent/isotropic, often spontaneous but needs high surfactant/co-surfactant; Nanoemulsion: kinetically stable, high-energy prep (HPH).
