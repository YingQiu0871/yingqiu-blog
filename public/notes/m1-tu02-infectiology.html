Cytoplasm / 细胞质

2. Gram-positive vs Gram-negative｜革兰阳性 vs 革兰阴性

Item｜项目

Gram-positive｜革兰阳性

Gram-negative｜革兰阴性

Cell wall

Thick PG, 15-80 nm; several layers

Thin but strong PG, around 10 nm; single layer

Outer membrane

Absent

Present; asymmetric; LPS in outer leaflet

Periplasm

Not the same as G- periplasm

Clear periplasmic space with soluble proteins + PG

Special polymers

WTA anchored in PG; LTA anchored in membrane

LPS = Lipid A + core + O-antigen

Main immune/clinical point

PG fragments, TA/LTA, lysozyme resistance

Lipid A = endotoxin; TLR4; permeability barrier

Minimal answer / 最简答案：Gram-positive bacteria have a thick peptidoglycan layer and teichoic/lipoteichoic acids, but no outer membrane. Gram-negative bacteria have a thin peptidoglycan layer in the periplasm and an outer membrane containing LPS.

★★★ Cell wall vs cell envelope ｜ cell wall 通常强调 PG 层；cell envelope 包括 membrane + wall + periplasm/outer membrane 等整体包膜。
★★★ Peptidoglycan function: shape + protection against osmotic rupture + scaffold for surface structures. ｜ PG 功能：维持形态、抗渗透压裂解、作为表面结构锚定框架。
★★★ Lipid A of LPS is the endotoxin. ｜ LPS 的 Lipid A 才是内毒素活性部分。

3. Gram-negative surface, LPS and appendages｜革兰阴性表面、LPS和附属结构

3.1 Inner membrane, periplasm and outer membrane｜内膜、周质、外膜

★★★ Inner membrane
cytoplasmic membrane, symmetric phospholipid bilayer, selective transport and protein translocation. ｜ 内膜：细胞质膜，对称磷脂双层，负责选择性营养运输和蛋白转运。
★★★ Periplasm
contains soluble proteins, thin PG mesh and membrane-derived oligosaccharides. ｜ 周质：含可溶性蛋白、薄PG网和膜来源寡糖。
★★★ Outer membrane
asymmetric bilayer; inner leaflet phospholipids; outer leaflet LPS. ｜ 外膜：非对称双层；内侧叶是磷脂，外侧叶是LPS。
★★ Porins and OM proteins regulate permeability and can serve as phage or nutrient receptors. ｜ 孔蛋白和外膜蛋白调控通透性，也可作为噬菌体/营养物受体。
★★ Braun lipoprotein Lpp covalently links OM to PG and helps control periplasm width/mechanics. ｜ Braun脂蛋白Lpp共价连接外膜和PG，维持周质宽度和包膜机械性质。

3.2 LPS structure and endotoxin｜LPS结构与内毒素

Part｜部分

Composition｜组成

Exam function｜考试功能

Lipid A

Conserved phosphorylated glucosamine disaccharide + fatty acid chains

Hydrophobic anchor; endotoxin active component; PAMP for TLR4

Core oligosaccharide

Inner core Kdo + heptoses; outer core hexoses

Links Lipid A to O-antigen; stabilizes LPS

O-antigen

Repeating sugar units, highly variable

Serotyping, immune interaction, complement evasion; non-toxic

★★★ LPS is a major OM component and permeability barrier. ｜ LPS是外膜主要成分，也是通透性屏障。
★★★ Endotoxin effects: pyrogenic, immune activation, high fever, sepsis, toxic shock. ｜ 内毒素效应：致热、激活免疫、高热、败血症、毒性休克。
★★★ LPS recognition: LBP binds LPS → CD14/MD-2 → TLR4 recognition/dimerization/endocytosis. ｜ 识别链：LBP结合LPS → CD14/MD-2 → TLR4识别、二聚化和内吞。
★★ MyD88 pathway
IRAK/TRAF6 → NF-kB and AP-1 → inflammatory cytokines. ｜ MyD88通路：IRAK/TRAF6 → NF-kB和AP-1 → 炎症细胞因子。
★★ TRIF pathway
TRAF3 → IRF3 → type I interferons IFNα/IFNβ. ｜ TRIF通路：TRAF3 → IRF3 → I型干扰素IFNα/IFNβ。
★★ Parenteral pharmaceutical products must control pyrogenic endotoxin; LAL assay is classic. ｜ 注射用药品需要控制内毒素热原，LAL试验是经典检测。
★★ Polymyxins/colistin bind negatively charged LPS, displace Mg2+/Ca2+, disorganize OM and increase permeability. ｜ 多黏菌素/黏菌素结合带负电LPS，置换Mg2+/Ca2+，破坏外膜并提高通透性。

3.3 O-antigen high-yield points｜O抗原高频点

★★ O-antigen is immunodominant but non-toxic. ｜ O抗原免疫显性，但不是毒性活性部分。
★★ Smooth strains have O-antigen; rough strains lack O-antigen. ｜ 光滑型有O抗原，粗糙型缺乏O抗原。
★★ Basis for serological classification: O, H(flagellar), and sometimes K(capsule) antigens. ｜ 血清分型依据：O抗原、H鞭毛抗原，有时还有K荚膜抗原。
★ Molecular mimicry: H. pylori O-antigen can resemble Lewis X blood group antigens. ｜ 分子模拟：幽门螺杆菌O抗原可类似Lewis X血型抗原。

3.4 Pili/fimbriae and flagella｜菌毛/伞毛与鞭毛

★★★ Pili/fimbriae
thin hair-like protein tubes; adhesion, invasion, biofilm, DNA uptake, gene transfer. ｜ 菌毛/伞毛：细的蛋白管；黏附、侵袭、生物膜、DNA摄取、基因转移。
★★ Chaperone-usher pili: common in E. coli, Shigella, Salmonella; UPEC type I pili/FimH binds bladder; P pili/PapG binds kidney. ｜ 伴侣-usher菌毛：常见于E. coli等；UPEC I型菌毛/FimH偏膀胱，P菌毛/PapG偏肾脏。
★★ Type IV pili
dynamic polymerization/depolymerization; adhesion, biofilm, DNA uptake, microcolonies. ｜ IV型菌毛：动态聚合/去聚合；黏附、生物膜、DNA摄取、微菌落。
★★★ F pilus/T4SS
conjugation, donor-recipient contact, horizontal gene transfer, antibiotic resistance spread. ｜ F菌毛/T4SS：接合、供受体接触、水平基因转移、耐药传播。
★★★ Flagella
long whip-like motility organelles; also adhesion, biofilm, protein export and innate immune recognition. ｜ 鞭毛：长鞭状运动结构；也参与黏附、生物膜、蛋白输出和先天免疫识别。
★★ Flagellum structure: filament(flagellin), cap FliD, hook, basal body/motor, rings, rod, stator MotA/MotB, export apparatus. ｜ 鞭毛结构：丝状体(flagellin)、帽FliD、钩、基体/马达、环、杆、定子MotA/MotB、输出装置。
★★ Flagellin is a PAMP recognized by TLR5; signaling activates MAPK/NF-kB and pro-inflammatory cytokines. ｜ 鞭毛蛋白是PAMP，被TLR5识别，激活MAPK/NF-kB和促炎因子。

4. Gram-positive surface and peptidoglycan｜革兰阳性表面与肽聚糖

4.1 Gram-positive surface components｜革兰阳性表面成分

★★★ Gram-positive surface = cytoplasmic membrane + thick PG + WTA/LTA + surface proteins + optional S-layer/capsule. ｜ 革兰阳性表面=细胞质膜+厚PG+WTA/LTA+表面蛋白+可选S层/荚膜。
★★★ WTA
anionic polymers, polyol repeats, covalently bound in PG. ｜ WTA壁磷壁酸：阴离子聚合物，多元醇重复单位，共价结合在PG中。
★★★ LTA
anchored in cytoplasmic membrane via glycolipid anchor. ｜ LTA脂磷壁酸：通过糖脂锚定在细胞质膜。
★★ Surface proteins mediate colonization/virulence, act as adhesins/proteases, and include PG hydrolases/autolysins. ｜ 表面蛋白介导定植/毒力，可为黏附素/蛋白酶，也包括PG水解酶/自溶素。
★★ Anchoring
LPXTG motif via sortase; LysM/SPOR/WXL/SH3b non-covalent; SLH/CWB domains for polysaccharide anchoring. ｜ 锚定：LPXTG经sortase共价锚定；LysM等非共价；SLH/CWB用于多糖锚定。

4.2 Peptidoglycan composition and synthesis｜PG组成和合成

★★★ PG is a rigid glycan-peptide mesh determining bacterial shape and protecting against external aggressors. ｜ PG是刚性糖肽网，决定形态并保护细菌免受外界损伤。
★★★ Backbone
alternating GlcNAc and MurNAc; peptide side chain attached to MurNAc. ｜ 骨架：GlcNAc和MurNAc交替；MurNAc上连接肽侧链。
★★★ Typical peptide includes L-Ala, D-Glu, meso-DAP or L-Lys, D-Ala-D-Ala. ｜ 典型肽链包括L-Ala、D-Glu、meso-DAP或L-Lys、D-Ala-D-Ala。
★★ Cross-linking/reticulation gives mechanical strength; cross-link patterns differ by species. ｜ 交联/网状化提供机械强度，不同菌种交联模式不同。
★★★ Cytoplasmic stage: precursor synthesis → MraY/MurG → Lipid I/Lipid II. ｜ 胞质阶段：前体合成 → MraY/MurG → Lipid I/Lipid II。
★★★ Extra-membrane stage: PBPs catalyze transglycosylation and transpeptidation. ｜ 膜外阶段：PBP催化转糖基和转肽反应。
★★ Cell division
PG synthesis/remodeling concentrates at septum under divisome control; antibiotics targeting PG stop division. ｜ 细胞分裂：PG合成/重塑集中在隔膜处，受divisome控制；抗PG合成药物阻断分裂。

4.3 Antibiotic targets and resistance｜抗生素靶点与耐药

★★★ Penicillins/β-lactams target PBPs responsible for PG cross-linking. ｜ 青霉素/β内酰胺靶向PBP，阻断PG交联。
★★★ Penicillin resistance: β-lactamase production or PBPs with lower sensitivity. ｜ 青霉素耐药：产生β内酰胺酶或选择低敏感性PBP。
★★★ Glycopeptides target D-Ala-D-Ala and block PG cross-linking/reticulation. ｜ 糖肽类靶向D-Ala-D-Ala，阻断PG交联/网状化。
★★ Glycopeptide resistance: D-Ala-D-Ala → D-Ala-D-Lac or D-Ala-D-Ser; reduced affinity. ｜ 糖肽耐药：D-Ala-D-Ala变为D-Ala-D-Lac或D-Ala-D-Ser，亲和力下降。
★★★ Four broad antibiotic targets: cell wall synthesis, protein synthesis, nucleic acid synthesis, folate metabolism. ｜ 四大抗生素靶点：细胞壁、蛋白合成、核酸合成、叶酸代谢。
★★★ Four broad resistance mechanisms: enzymatic inactivation, target modification, efflux, decreased permeability. ｜ 四大耐药机制：酶灭活、靶点改变、外排、通透性降低。

4.4 PG recycling, host recognition and lysozyme｜PG回收、宿主识别和溶菌酶

★★ PG is continuously remodeled by PG hydrolases during growth and division. ｜ 生长分裂时PG持续被PG水解酶重塑。
★★ Hydrolases include glucosaminidase, endopeptidases, carboxypeptidase, muramidase/lytic transglycosylase, amidase. ｜ 水解酶包括葡萄糖胺苷酶、内肽酶、羧肽酶、胞壁酸酶/裂解转糖基酶、酰胺酶。

7. Horizontal gene transfer and mobile elements｜水平基因转移与移动遗传元件

★★★ Gene flow increases microbial diversity and may affect metabolism, antigenic composition, virulence and antibiotic resistance. ｜ 基因流增加微生物多样性，可影响代谢、抗原组成、毒力和抗生素耐药。
★★★ Foreign DNA must be stably acquired by homologous recombination, autonomous replication as plasmid, or integration; otherwise it is diluted/lost. ｜ 外源DNA必须通过同源重组、自主复制质粒或整合稳定存在，否则会被稀释/丢失。
★★★ Homologous recombination: RecA-mediated double crossing-over, substitution/allelic exchange. ｜ 同源重组：RecA介导双交换，实现替换/等位基因交换。

7.1 Mobile elements｜移动元件

★★★ Transposons
DNA elements with transposase; move without sequence homology; can carry resistance or virulence genes. ｜ 转座子：带transposase的DNA元件；不需序列同源即可移动；可携带耐药/毒力基因。
★★ Transposon properties: move chromosome ↔ plasmid; not replicative; can modify gene expression positively or negatively. ｜ 转座子特性：可在染色体和质粒间移动；不自我复制；可正/负调控基因表达。
★★ Integrons
gene capture and expression systems; intI integrase + attI site + Pc promoter; cassettes often promoterless resistance ORFs. ｜ 整合子：基因捕获和表达系统；intI整合酶+attI位点+Pc启动子；盒常为无启动子的耐药ORF。
★★ Integrons are not mobile by themselves but often sit on transposons or plasmids. ｜ 整合子本身通常不能移动，但常位于转座子或质粒上。
★★★ Plasmids
extrachromosomal DNA, non-essential, autonomous replication; circular usually; copy number 1 to >100. ｜ 质粒：染色体外DNA，非必需，自主复制；通常环状；拷贝数1到>100。

8. Gene expression and regulation｜基因表达与调控

★★ Bacterial genes
average ~1000 bases, usually no introns, many genes per bacterium, high coding density. ｜ 细菌基因平均约1000碱基，通常无内含子，编码密度高。
★★★ Operon
DNA segment with genes under the same promoter; one mRNA can encode several proteins. ｜ 操纵子：多个基因受同一启动子控制；一条mRNA可编码多个蛋白。
★★ In bacteria, transcription and translation can be coupled. ｜ 细菌中转录和翻译可耦联进行。
★★ ORF
open reading frame; one codon = 3 bases; common start ATG, sometimes TTG; stop codons TAA/TGA/TAG. ｜ ORF开放阅读框；1个密码子=3个碱基；起始常ATG，有时TTG；终止TAA/TGA/TAG。
★★★ Transcription stages: initiation, elongation, termination. ｜ 转录阶段：起始、延伸、终止。
★★★ Sigma factor forms RNAP holoenzyme and directs RNA polymerase to promoter regions. ｜ σ因子形成RNA聚合酶全酶并引导其识别启动子。
★★★ E. coli sigma70 promoter consensus: -35 TTGACA and -10 TATAAT. ｜ E. coli σ70启动子共识序列：-35 TTGACA 和 -10 TATAAT。
★★ Termination
intrinsic hairpin + U-rich sequence, Rho-dependent termination, or Mfd-associated activity. ｜ 终止：内源性发夹+U-rich，Rho依赖，或Mfd相关。
★★ Translation initiation uses RBS/Shine-Dalgarno sequence. ｜ 翻译起始依赖RBS/Shine-Dalgarno序列。

8.1 Regulation levels｜调控层次

★★★ Constitutive genes are continuously expressed; regulated genes are expressed only under specific conditions/stimuli. ｜ 组成型基因持续表达；调控型基因仅在特定条件/刺激下表达。
★★★ Alternative sigma factors redirect transcription under stress/developmental conditions; often controlled by anti-sigma factors. ｜ 替代σ因子在应激/发育条件下重定向转录；常由anti-σ因子控制。
★★ Transcription factors bind DNA near promoter or polymerase; HTH and RHH are important groups. ｜ 转录因子结合启动子附近DNA或聚合酶；HTH和RHH是重要类型。
★★★ Lac operon
without lactose, LacI repressor binds operator and blocks lacZYA; with lactose, repression is relieved. ｜ 乳糖操纵子：无乳糖时LacI阻遏子结合operator阻断lacZYA；有乳糖时解除阻遏。
★★ Riboswitches regulate transcription termination/antitermination or translation depending on ligand binding. ｜ 核糖开关根据配体结合调控转录终止/抗终止或翻译。
★★ Hfq binds RNA and helps sRNAs pair with target mRNAs; it can suppress or protect protein synthesis. ｜ Hfq是RNA结合蛋白，帮助sRNA与靶mRNA配对，可抑制或保护蛋白合成。
★★ Post-translational regulation: proteolytic cleavage, phosphorylation, acetylation, methylation, disulfide bridge formation. ｜ 翻译后调控：蛋白水解切割、磷酸化、乙酰化、甲基化、二硫键形成。
★★ Two-component systems: sensor histidine kinase detects stimulus and phosphorylates response regulator, often an HTH DNA-binding protein. ｜ 双组分系统：组氨酸激酶感受刺激并磷酸化反应调节蛋白，后者常为HTH DNA结合蛋白。

9. Host-pathogen interaction: cytoskeleton hacking｜宿主-病原相互作用：细胞骨架劫持

★★ Cytoskeleton types: microfilaments(actin), microtubules(tubulin), intermediate filaments(various), septins. ｜ 细胞骨架类型：微丝actin、微管tubulin、中间丝多种蛋白、septins。

10. Bacterial spores｜细菌孢子

★★★ Bacterial spores are dormant cellular forms of Gram-positive bacteria with high stability and extreme resistance. ｜ 细菌孢子是革兰阳性菌的休眠细胞形态，稳定性高，耐受极端条件。
★★★ Not a reproduction form. It is a survival form. ｜ 不是繁殖形式，而是生存形式。
★★ Major spore-forming human pathogens: B. anthracis, B. cereus, C. tetani, C. botulinum, C. perfringens, C. difficile. ｜ 主要人类致病产孢菌：炭疽芽孢杆菌、蜡样芽孢杆菌、破伤风梭菌、肉毒梭菌、产气荚膜梭菌、艰难梭菌。

10.1 Spore structure｜孢子结构

★★★ Core
DNA, RNA, enzymes, ATP/NADH; very low water; lower pH; contains Ca-DPA and SASP. ｜ 核心：DNA、RNA、酶、ATP/NADH；水分极低、pH较低；含Ca-DPA和SASP。

★★★ Inner membrane is very low permeability and protects the core even against water. ｜ 内膜通透性极低，甚至阻止水进入核心。
★★★ Cortex is a thick special PG layer with muramic-δ-lactam; degraded during germination. ｜ 皮层是厚的特殊PG层，含muramic-δ-lactam；萌发时被降解。
★★ Coat is made of several protein layers; basal layer, inner coat and outer coat; protects against chemicals and enzymes. ｜ 孢衣由多层蛋白组成：基底层、内孢衣、外孢衣；抵抗化学物和酶。
★★ Exosporium exists in B. cereus, B. thuringiensis, B. anthracis, but not B. subtilis. ｜ 外孢层存在于B. cereus、B. thuringiensis、B. anthracis，不存在于B. subtilis。

Describe the Gram-negative envelope.：The Gram-negative envelope consists of an inner/cytoplasmic membrane, a periplasmic space containing thin peptidoglycan, and an asymmetric outer membrane. The outer leaflet of the outer membrane contains LPS, composed of Lipid A, core oligosaccharide and O-antigen. It provides a permeability barrier and contributes to endotoxin activity through Lipid A.

Describe the Gram-positive surface.：The Gram-positive surface contains a cytoplasmic membrane and a thick peptidoglycan layer. Wall teichoic acids are anchored in peptidoglycan, whereas lipoteichoic acids are anchored in the membrane. Surface proteins, S-layer and capsule may contribute to adhesion, immune evasion and virulence.

Explain why Lipid A is important.：Lipid A is the conserved active component of LPS endotoxin. It is recognized after transfer by LBP to CD14/MD-2/TLR4. TLR4 signaling activates MyD88-dependent NF-kB/AP-1 pathways and TRIF-dependent IRF3 signaling, leading to cytokines, fever and possibly sepsis or toxic shock.

Compare transformation, conjugation and transduction.：Transformation is uptake of free DNA by competent bacteria. Conjugation requires direct cell-to-cell contact, usually through a pilus and conjugative plasmid. Transduction is bacteriophage-mediated DNA transfer and may be generalized or specialized.

Explain peptidoglycan as an antibiotic target.：Peptidoglycan is a glycan-peptide mesh essential for bacterial shape and osmotic protection. PBPs catalyze transglycosylation and transpeptidation during PG synthesis. β-lactams inhibit PBPs, whereas glycopeptides bind D-Ala-D-Ala and block cross-linking.

Explain spore resistance.：Spore resistance depends on low core water content, Ca-DPA, SASP proteins protecting DNA, DNA repair enzymes, low-permeability inner membrane, cortex, coat and sometimes exosporium. These structures protect against heat, UV, chemicals, lysozyme and disinfectants.

Explain cholera toxin mechanism.：Cholera toxin binds GM1, is internalized and transported to the ER. The A1 chain enters the cytoplasm and ADP-ribosylates Gsα, keeping adenylate cyclase active. cAMP increases, PKA activates CFTR chloride secretion, and water follows into the intestinal lumen, causing watery diarrhea.

Explain zipper vs trigger entry.：Zipper entry requires close receptor-mediated contact, such as cadherin or integrin recognition, and produces minor actin rearrangement. Trigger entry uses bacterial T3SS effectors to induce massive actin rearrangement and macropinocytosis, allowing several bacteria to enter.

4. Gram-negative bacterial surface

Gram-negative envelope 可以分成三个层次来背：inner membrane -> periplasm with thin PG -> outer membrane with LPS。

• Inner membrane
cytoplasmic membrane, symmetric phospholipid bilayer；负责 selective nutrient transport 和 protein translocation。
• Periplasm
含 soluble proteins 与薄层 peptidoglycan mesh；帮助抵抗 turgor pressure，并维持 cell shape。
• Outer membrane
asymmetric bilayer；inner leaflet 是 phospholipids，outer leaflet 是 LPS；它是重要的 permeability barrier。

LPS 结构 / Structure of LPS

Lipid A：保守的疏水锚定部分，是 endotoxin 的 active component。

Core oligosaccharide：内核常含 Kdo 和 heptoses，外核多为 hexoses。

O-antigen：可变的 repeating sugar units，是 serogroup/serotyping 的重要基础。

O-antigen 的考点 / High-yield points

• Highly variable
不同菌株之间差异大，所以常用于 serological classification。

• Immunodominant but non-toxic：免疫上很显眼，但真正的 endotoxin active component 是 Lipid A，不是 O-antigen。

• 可以帮助 complement evasion：O-antigen chain elongation 可使 C3b / MAC deposition 远离表面。

Lipid A / endotoxin

• Lipid A 是 PAMP，可被 LBP -> CD14 / MD-2 -> TLR4 识别。

• TLR4 signaling 包括 MyD88-dependent pathway 和 TRIF-dependent pathway。

• 后果
cytokine release, fever, strong innate immune activation；严重时可到 sepsis / toxic shock。

最简答案 / Minimal answer

The Gram-negative outer membrane is asymmetric and contains LPS in its outer leaflet.

Lipid A is the endotoxin-active component of LPS and activates innate immunity through TLR4 signaling.

O-antigen is highly variable and contributes to serotyping and immune interaction.

Cell surface appendages in Gram-negative bacteria

• Pili / fimbriae
较细，常与 adhesion、biofilm、DNA uptake、gene transfer 相关。

• Chaperone-usher pili：常见于 E. coli / Shigella / Salmonella；UPEC type I pili 与膀胱黏附相关，P pili 与肾脏相关。

• Type IV pili
动态聚合/去聚合，可参与 adhesion、biofilm、DNA uptake、microcolony formation。

• Conjugative pilus / F pilus：horizontal gene transfer 的关键结构之一。

• Flagella
长鞭状结构，主要负责 motility，但也参与 adhesion、biofilm 和 virulence。

Flagella 高频句

• Flagella are not only motility organelles; they can also contribute to adhesion, biofilm formation, protein export, and innate immune recognition.

5. Gram-positive bacterial surface and peptidoglycan biology

Gram-positive surface 的关键词是：thick PG, teichoic acids, lipoteichoic acids, surface proteins, S-layer, capsule。

• Cytoplasmic membrane + thick peptidoglycan layer。

• Wall teichoic acids (WTA)：anchored in PG；通常是 anionic polymers。

• Lipoteichoic acids (LTA)：anchored in the membrane via glycolipid anchor。

• Surface proteins
可通过 sortase 识别的 LPXTG motif 共价连接到 PG，也可通过 LysM 等 motif 非共价结合。

• Some species have an S-layer；它是最外层的 protein crystalline network。

• Capsule 可位于最外层，帮助 immune evasion，尤其是抗吞噬。

Peptidoglycan composition

• PG 的 backbone 由 N-acetylglucosamine (GlcNAc) 和 N-acetylmuramic acid (MurNAc) 重复组成。

• MurNAc 上连 peptide side chain；典型末端是 D-Ala-D-Ala。

• Cross-linking 决定细胞壁的机械强度；不同菌种的 cross-linking pattern 可以不同。

最简答案 / Minimal answer

Peptidoglycan is a rigid mesh made of glycan chains cross-linked by peptides.

It determines bacterial shape and protects the cell from osmotic rupture.

Gram-positive bacteria have a thick peptidoglycan layer and teichoic/lipoteichoic acids.

PG synthesis & antibiotics

• Cytoplasmic stage：形成前体，再经 MraY / MurG 形成 Lipid I / Lipid II。

• Extramembrane stage：由 PBP 催化 transglycosylation 与 transpeptidation，完成 PG 聚合与交联。

• Penicillins / β-lactams：靶向 PBPs，阻断 PG cross-linking。

• Glycopeptides
靶向 D-Ala-D-Ala，阻断 reticulation / cross-linking。

耐药机制 / Resistance mechanisms

Against penicillins: β-lactamase production or selection of PBPs with lower sensitivity.

Against glycopeptides: target modification such as D-Ala-D-Ala -> D-Ala-D-Lac or D-Ala-D-Ser.

PG recycling and host defense

• 生长和分裂时 PG 持续重塑；多种 PG hydrolases 分别切不同键。

• Lysozyme 可切开 GlcNAc 与 MurNAc 之间的键。

• 某些菌通过 N-deacetylases 改造 PG，降低 lysozyme 效果；C. difficile 的 PgdA 是课件中的例子。

思路分析 / Logic

• PG 在考试里不是孤立知识点。它同时连着：cell shape、Gram+ structure、antibiotic target、host recognition、lysozyme resistance。

• 答题时尽量把“结构 + 功能 + 药物 + 宿主反应”串起来，分数会更完整。

6. Host recognition of bacteria: PAMPs, PRRs, TLR4, NOD1, NOD2

这一块是细菌学和免疫学的交叉高频区。你要会的不只是名词，而是“谁识别谁，随后发生什么”。

• PAMPs
lipopolysaccharide, peptidoglycan, lipopeptides, flagellin, lipoarabinomannan, bacterial DNA motifs。
• PRRs
scavenger receptors, C-type lectin receptors, TLRs, NLRs/NOD-like receptors, DNA sensors。
• 核心逻辑
PAMP recognition -> signaling cascade -> transcription factors -> cytokines / inflammation。

TLR4 and lipid A

• Lipid A is recognized after transfer by LBP to CD14 / MD-2 / TLR4 complex。

• TLR4 dimerization and endocytosis trigger two pathways: MyD88-dependent and TRIF-dependent。

• MyD88-dependent signaling activates IRAK / TRAF6 / TAK1 / IKK -> NF-kB and AP-1 -> pro-inflammatory genes。

• TRIF-dependent signaling can activate IRF3 -> type I interferons (IFNa / IFNb).

最简答案 / Minimal answer

Lipid A is the endotoxin-active component of LPS and is recognized by the CD14/MD-2/TLR4 system.

TLR4 signaling activates NF-kB, AP-1 and IRF3, leading to inflammatory cytokine and type I interferon production.

Pathological outcomes include fever, sepsis and toxic shock.

NOD1 and NOD2

• NOD1 senses iE-DAP (D-Glu-meso-diaminopimelic acid).

• NOD2 senses muramyl dipeptide (MDP).

• Both are cytosolic sensors and lead to NF-kB activation and inflammation.

Inflammasome / NLRC4 and others

• Flagellin and some secretion-system-associated signals can activate NLRC4-related inflammasome pathways。

• Inflammasome activation links bacterial sensing to caspase activation and inflammatory mediator maturation。

• 在复习层面，记住“flagellin -> NLRC4”这个配对就很值分。

易混点 / Common confusion

• TLR4 主要联想到 LPS / Lipid A；NOD1/NOD2 联想到 PG fragments。

• O-antigen 是可变的免疫学部分，但 endotoxin active component 是 Lipid A，不是 O-antigen。

• NOD1 对 iE-DAP，NOD2 对 MDP，这两个不要记反。

7. Horizontal gene transfer and mobile genetic elements

这部分建议你按两层来背
第一层是 DNA acquired 后如何稳定存在；第二层是 DNA 怎么从一个菌到另一个菌。

• 外源 DNA 进入细菌后，要么通过 homologous recombination 取代原位序列，要么作为 plasmid autonomous replication，要么作为 integrative DNA 插入；否则会被稀释丢失。

• 转座子、integron、plasmid 都是高频移动遗传元件；它们常和 resistance / virulence 相关。

Homologous recombination

• RecA is the key protein in homologous recombination.

• Double crossover leads to substitution / allelic exchange and allows donor DNA to become stably inherited.

Transposons, integrons and plasmids

• Transposons
依赖 transposase；可在 chromosome 与 plasmid 间移动；不要求 sequence homology；可携带 resistance or virulence genes。
• Integrons
gene capture and expression system；依赖 integrase；常收集 promoterless gene cassettes，特别是 resistance genes；integrons 本身 often are not self-mobile，但常位于 transposons 或 plasmids 上。
• Plasmids
distinct from chromosomal DNA, non-essential for survival, autonomous replication；copy number can vary widely。

最简答案 / Minimal answer

Transposons are mobile DNA elements that move without requiring extensive sequence homology and may carry resistance or virulence genes.

Integrons capture and express gene cassettes, often antibiotic-resistance cassettes.

Plasmids are extrachromosomal DNA molecules capable of autonomous replication.

机制

本质

高频关键词

Transformation

Free DNA uptake by competent bacteria

Natural competence; single-stranded DNA enters and may recombine via RecA

Conjugation

Direct contact between donor and recipient

Pilus/F plasmid/tra genes; important for plasmid and resistance spread

Transduction

Bacteriophage-mediated DNA transfer

Generalized = non-specific; Specialized = genes near phage attachment site

How to distinguish them in an exam

• Transformation
关键词是 competent bacteria, free DNA, RecA。
• Conjugation
关键词是 direct contact, pilus, F factor, tra genes。
• Transduction
关键词是 bacteriophage；generalized vs specialized。

8. Gene expression and regulation in bacteria

这部分是典型机制题区域。你至少要会 promoter、sigma factor、operon、lac operon、riboswitch、Hfq。

• Bacterial genes often lack introns and can be grouped in operons.

• One promoter can drive one polycistronic mRNA and therefore several proteins.

• In bacteria, transcription and translation are coupled.

Promoter, transcription and sigma factors

• Promoter recognition is the first step of transcription initiation.

• For the classical sigma 70 promoters in E. coli, the consensus sequences are around -35 TTGACA and -10 TATAAT.

• Sigma factors bind the core RNA polymerase and direct it to promoter regions.

• Alternative sigma factors allow transcription of specific stress- or developmental-response genes.

最简答案 / Minimal answer

Sigma factors determine promoter recognition by RNA polymerase.

Alternative sigma factors redirect transcription under specific conditions such as stress or sporulation.

An operon is a group of genes under the control of a single promoter, producing one polycistronic mRNA.

Operon and lac operon

• Operon = several genes under one promoter.

• lacI encodes the repressor; in the absence of lactose, the repressor binds the operator and blocks transcription of lacZ, lacY, lacA.

• When lactose is present, repression is relieved and the operon can be expressed.

Other levels of regulation

• Transcription factors can positively or negatively regulate transcription by binding DNA near promoters or interacting with RNA polymerase.

• Riboswitches regulate transcription termination/antitermination or translation depending on ligand binding.

• Hfq is an RNA-binding protein that helps regulatory small RNAs pair with target mRNAs and can inhibit or protect translation.

• Post-translational regulation includes proteolytic cleavage, phosphorylation, acetylation, methylation, and disulfide bond formation.

思路分析 / Logic

• 如果题目问 global regulation，不要只写 operon；应至少提 alternative sigma factors + transcription factors + riboswitch + Hfq/sRNA。

• 如果题目问 lac operon，关键不是讲代谢，而是讲 repression / derepression 逻辑。

9. Host-pathogen interaction and cytoskeleton hijacking

这部分很容易被讲得很复杂，但考试里通常抓几个大机制：actin、Rho GTPases、entry into cells、intracellular survival、actin-based motility。

• Actin exists as G-actin and F-actin; polymerization, depolymerization and treadmilling are dynamic processes.

• Small Rho-family GTPases such as Rho, Rac and Cdc42 regulate actin remodeling.

• Many bacterial virulence factors directly or indirectly modify actin or these small GTPases.

Two entry mechanisms: zipper and trigger

• Zipper mechanism
细菌紧贴膜面，识别受体如 integrins/cadherins，使用宿主 signal transduction；actin reorganization 相对较小；通常一次进入一个细菌。

• Trigger mechanism：通过 TTSS/T3SS effectors 诱导 massive actin rearrangement 和 macropinocytosis；可同时内化多个细菌。

最简答案 / Minimal answer

Zipper entry relies on close receptor-mediated interaction and limited actin rearrangement.

Trigger entry relies on bacterial effectors, strong actin remodeling and macropinocytosis.

Salmonella and intracellular survival

• Salmonella trigger invasion involves bacterial effectors acting as GEFs for Rac/Cdc42, leading to WASP and Arp2/3 activation.

• During intracellular survival, actin helps maintain vacuole integrity, microtubules control membrane dynamics, and intermediate filaments help perinuclear localization.

• 课件里一个关键结论是
Salmonella simultaneously interferes with microfilaments, microtubules and intermediate filaments.

Actin-based motility

• Listeria uses ActA, a WASP mimic, to recruit Arp2/3, G-actin and Ena/VASP, producing actin tails.

• This allows intracellular movement and spread to adjacent cells.

• Some extracellular pathogens such as EPEC can also induce actin polymerization beneath attachment sites, forming pedestals.

10. Bacterial spores

孢子这一块非常适合出结构题和机制题。建议按 definition -> layers -> sporulation -> germination -> clinical examples 的顺序背。

• Bacterial spores are dormant cellular forms of Gram-positive bacteria.

• They are highly stable, can survive for years, and resist many harsh environmental conditions.

• Spore-forming pathogens are epidemiologically important in foodborne disease, environmental persistence and hospital transmission.

Spore structure

• 主要层次
core -> inner membrane -> primordial peptidoglycan / germ cell wall -> cortex -> coat -> exosporium (only in some species).
• Core
含 DNA, RNA, enzymes, ATP/NADH；water content 很低，pH 较低。

• DPA (dipicolinic acid) with Ca2+：显著降低核心含水量，是耐受性的重要来源。

• SASP proteins
small acid-soluble proteins，结合并保护 DNA，尤其抗 UV damage。

最简答案 / Minimal answer

The spore core contains DNA, enzymes, low water content, Ca-DPA and SASP proteins.

The cortex is a specialized peptidoglycan layer and the coat is a multilayered protein shell.

Some species also possess an exosporium outside the coat.

Cortex and coat

• Cortex 是厚的特殊 peptidoglycan；其中大量 MurNAc residues undergo modification and muramic-d-lactam formation。

• Coat is made of several protein layers and contains many spore-specific proteins.

• Exosporium 并非所有孢子都有；B. anthracis, B. cereus 等可有，B. subtilis 没有。

Sporulation: stages and regulation

• Sporulation is triggered by prolonged nutrient deprivation.

• Key developmental logic：asymmetric division produces a forespore and a mother cell；engulfment follows；then cortex and coat are synthesized；finally mother cell lyses and releases the mature spore.

• Spo0A phosphorylation is the decision point for entry into sporulation.

• Alternative sigma factor cascade：SigF and SigE appear first in forespore/mother cell respectively；later SigG and SigK act in later stages.

一定要会写的流程 / Short workflow

Nutrient deprivation -> Spo0A activation -> asymmetric septation -> engulfment -> cortex synthesis -> coat synthesis -> mother-cell lysis -> spore release

Germination

• Dormant spores sense favorable conditions through germinant receptors.

• 典型过程
ion release -> Ca-DPA release -> activation of cortex-lytic enzymes -> cortex hydrolysis -> core rehydration -> metabolism resumes -> vegetative cell outgrowth。

• For B. subtilis, L-alanine is a classic germinant；for C. difficile, taurocholate and glycine are important germinants.

Clinical examples of major spore-forming pathogens

• Bacillus anthracis -> anthrax; spores persist in soil for decades.

• Bacillus cereus -> food poisoning, especially associated with contaminated food such as rice.

• Clostridium botulinum -> botulism; spores survive pasteurization and can germinate in anaerobically stored foods.

• Clostridium perfringens -> diarrheal toxic infection and gas gangrene.

• Clostridium tetani -> tetanus; wound contamination by spores.

• Clostridioides difficile -> pseudomembranous colitis after gut dysbiosis.

Why spores are resistant

• Low core water content protects against wet heat.

• SASP and DNA repair systems protect genome integrity.

• Inner membrane, coat and exosporium increase resistance to chemicals, lysozyme and disinfectants.

11. Bacterial toxins

毒素章节最容易出“分类 + 举例 + 机制”题。不要只背名字，要记“target + biochemical activity + clinical effect”。

先分两大类 / First big distinction

Endotoxin: cell-associated LPS released after bacterial disruption; active component = Lipid A.

Exotoxin: protein toxin synthesized by bacteria and delivered to target cells.

Toxin genes can be located on chromosome, plasmids, bacteriophages or pathogenicity islands, which explains why strains of the same species may be toxigenic or non-toxigenic.

11.1 Pore-forming toxins (PFTs)

• PFTs are the largest class of bacterial toxins; they bind to membranes, oligomerize and form pores.

• The pore alters membrane permeability and allows ions or molecules to pass.

• Two structural groups：a-PFTs and b-PFTs.

• a-PFT examples
colicins, Cytolysin A (ClyA).
• b-PFT examples
S. aureus alpha-hemolysin, PVL, aerolysin-family toxins including C. perfringens epsilon-toxin.

最简答案 / Minimal answer

Pore-forming toxins bind membranes, oligomerize and create transmembrane pores.

This causes membrane permeabilization, ion imbalance, cell injury and sometimes cell lysis.

11.2 Membrane-damaging sphingomyelinases and phospholipases

• These toxins are esterases that hydrolyze membrane lipids.

• Consequences include tissue destruction, plasma membrane disruption, nutrient release, inflammatory mediator production, and escape from phagosomes.

• Sphingomyelinases act on sphingomyelin; phospholipases act on phospholipids.

• Bacillus cereus SMase C contributes to early immune evasion.

• ExoU of Pseudomonas aeruginosa is a patatin-like phospholipase A2 delivered by T3SS.

• C. perfringens alpha-toxin (PLC/CPA) damages membranes and promotes severe inflammation and myonecrosis.

11.3 Toxins with intracellular targets

• General shared process：binding to receptor -> internalization -> translocation of catalytic domain -> intracellular enzymatic activity.

• Three common organizational families in the slides：ABRT, AB5, and AnB7 toxins.

ABRT toxins

• Botulinum toxins
prevent neurotransmitter release at the neuromuscular junction -> flaccid paralysis.
• Tetanus toxin
blocks inhibitory interneurons in the CNS -> spastic paralysis.

• Both are proteases targeting SNARE-related proteins such as SNAP25, VAMP or syntaxin depending on toxin type.

• Diphtheria toxin ADP-ribosylates elongation factor 2 (EF2), stopping protein synthesis.

• Large clostridial toxins such as C. difficile toxins A and B inactivate Rho-family GTPases and disrupt actin cytoskeleton.

AB5 toxins

• Cholera toxin binds GM1 ganglioside, reaches the ER, and ADP-ribosylates Gs alpha.

• This keeps adenylate cyclase active -> increases cAMP -> activates PKA -> stimulates CFTR chloride secretion -> watery diarrhea.

• Heat-labile toxin of E. coli is closely related to cholera toxin.

• Shiga/Shiga-like toxins and pertussis toxin also belong to AB5-type toxins in the course classification.

AnB7 toxins

• Anthrax toxin is tripartite：protective antigen (PA) + edema factor (EF) + lethal factor (LF).

• PA binds receptor and forms the pore; EF is an adenylate cyclase that increases cAMP (edema); LF is a zinc protease that cleaves MAPKK (lethality/apoptotic signaling).

• Some actin ADP-ribosylating binary toxins are also grouped here.

高频例子一页记法 / One-line recall

Botulinum -> SNARE cleavage -> no ACh release -> flaccid paralysis

Tetanus -> inhibitory interneuron blockade -> spastic paralysis

Diphtheria -> EF2 inactivation -> stop protein synthesis

Cholera -> Gs activation / cAMP increase -> chloride and water secretion

Anthrax -> PA pore + EF edema + LF MAPKK cleavage

13. 常见短答题模板 / Short-answer templates

Describe the Gram-negative envelope.

• 最简答案
Inner membrane + periplasm with thin peptidoglycan + outer membrane with LPS. Mention Lipid A, core and O-antigen, and say the OM is a permeability barrier.

Explain why Lipid A is important.

• 最简答案
It is the endotoxin-active component of LPS, recognized by the CD14/MD-2/TLR4 system, and induces strong innate immune activation, fever and possibly sepsis.

What is peptidoglycan and why is it important?

• 最简答案
A glycan-peptide mesh that determines shape and protects against osmotic lysis; also a major antibiotic target and immune-stimulating structure.

Compare transformation, conjugation and transduction.

• 最简答案
Transformation = free DNA uptake; conjugation = direct cell-to-cell transfer often through a pilus/plasmid; transduction = phage-mediated transfer.

What is an operon?

• 最简答案
A set of genes controlled by one promoter and expressed as one polycistronic mRNA.

How do bacterial spores resist harsh conditions?

• 最简答案
Low core water, Ca-DPA, SASP proteins, inner membrane, cortex, coat and sometimes exosporium all contribute.

• 最简答案
AB5 toxin binds GM1, A1 reaches cytosol, ADP-ribosylates Gs alpha, increases cAMP, activates PKA and CFTR, causing chloride and water secretion.

Differentiate botulinum and tetanus toxins.

• 最简答案
Both are neurotoxins that cleave SNARE-related targets, but botulinum blocks neurotransmitter release causing flaccid paralysis, whereas tetanus blocks inhibitory interneurons causing spastic paralysis.

Common traps / 容易失分的补充点

• 不要把“所有 Gram− 都是高度耐药菌”写死。更稳的说法是：Gram− bacteria often show lower permeability because of the outer membrane and may display important intrinsic resistance.

• 不要把 endotoxin 写成“any toxin from Gram− bacteria”。更准确：endotoxin = Lipid A of LPS。

• 写 exotoxin 例子时，至少带一个功能标签：tetanus = spastic paralysis；botulinum = flaccid paralysis；cholera = secretory diarrhea。

• 写 horizontal gene transfer 时，不要忘了 phage：transduction = phage-mediated gene transfer。

★★★★★ Part III. Core synthesis topics / 第三部分：综合题主干

★★★★★ 1. Virus structure and modes of transmission / 病毒结构与传播方式

Minimal scoring answer / 最得分答案：A virus is an obligate intracellular parasite containing either DNA or RNA, surrounded by a capsid and sometimes by an envelope. The capsid protects the genome and may participate in attachment. The envelope carries glycoproteins involved in attachment and fusion, but because it is fragile in the environment, enveloped viruses are usually transmitted by close contact, droplets, blood, sexual or vertical routes, whereas naked viruses are more resistant and are often transmitted indirectly or by the fecal–oral route. / 病毒是专性细胞内寄生物，只含一种核酸（DNA 或 RNA），外有衣壳，有些还有包膜。衣壳保护基因组并可参与附着；包膜上的糖蛋白参与结合和融合，但包膜在环境中较脆弱，因此包膜病毒更常经近距离接触、飞沫、血液、性传播或母婴传播，而无包膜病毒更耐环境，更常见于间接接触或粪口传播。

1) EN: Start with genome + capsid ± envelope. 中: 先从 genome + capsid ± envelope 开始。

2) EN: Name the function of each structure, not only the names. 中: 写出每个结构的功能，不要只罗列名称。

3) EN: Link envelope fragility to epidemiology. 中: 把 envelope 的脆弱性直接连到流行病学后果。

4) EN: If asked about entry points, always give body site + virus example. 中: 若问 entry points，一定写“进入部位 + 病毒例子”。

Safe examples / 稳定例子：Respiratory = influenza / RSV / SARS-CoV-2; fecal–oral = HAV / HEV / rotavirus; blood = HIV / HBV / HCV; sexual = HIV / HSV-2; vertical = HIV / HBV / CMV / HSV / rubella; vector-borne = dengue / Zika / yellow fever.

• EN
Common trap: never say one virion contains both DNA and RNA as its genome. 中: 常见陷阱：绝不要说同一个 virion 同时含 DNA 和 RNA 作为基因组。
• EN
Common trap: do not stop at “enveloped viruses are fragile”; continue with “therefore they are usually transmitted by ...”. 中: 常见陷阱：不要只写“包膜病毒脆弱”，要继续写“因此它们通常通过……传播”。

★★★★★ 2. Persistent infection / 持续性感染

Minimal scoring answer / 最得分答案：Persistent infection is a long-term infection in which the viral genome is maintained in the host because the virus is not cleared by the immune system. The two main categories are latent infection and chronic infection. / 持续性感染是指病毒基因组在宿主体内长期保留，因为免疫系统没有将其清除。两大类型是 latent infection（潜伏感染）和 chronic infection（慢性感染）。

• EN
Latent infection: the viral genome persists with no or minimal production of infectious particles; reactivation can later restore productive replication. 中: 潜伏感染：病毒基因组持续存在，但感染性颗粒几乎不产生或极少；之后可再激活。
• EN
Chronic infection: continuous or repeated production of virus over long periods, with long-term transmission and inflammation. 中: 慢性感染：长期持续或反复产生病毒，伴长期传播和炎症。
• EN
Classic latent examples: HSV / VZV in sensory neurons, HCMV in myeloid cells, EBV in memory B cells. 中: 经典潜伏例子：HSV / VZV 在感觉神经元；HCMV 在髓系细胞；EBV 在 memory B cells。
• EN
Classic chronic examples: HBV, HCV, HIV. 中: 经典慢性感染例子：HBV、HCV、HIV。

Viral factors to mention / 必提病毒因素：latency, sanctuary sites, interference with antigen presentation, antigenic variation, replication in immune cells, low cytopathic effect.

Host factors to mention / 必提宿主因素：immunodeficiency, extreme ages, pregnancy, comorbidities, genetic factors.

• EN
Common trap: latency is not eradication; the genome remains. 中: 常见陷阱：latency 不是被清除；基因组仍然存在。
• EN
If you cite HIV, the safer wording is chronic infection with reservoirs, not “purely latent”. 中: 如果写 HIV，更稳的表达是“带有 reservoirs 的慢性感染”，而不是“纯潜伏”。

★★★★★ Part IV. High-yield short-answer bank / 第四部分：高频简答题答案银行

★★★★★ 1. Influenza / 流感病毒

• EN
Family: Orthomyxoviridae. Enveloped, helical nucleocapsid, segmented ssRNA(-). Influenza A and B usually have 8 segments. 中: 家族：Orthomyxoviridae。有包膜、螺旋核衣壳、分节段负链单股 RNA。Influenza A 和 B 通常有 8 个节段。
• EN
Envelope proteins to remember: HA, NA and M2; matrix protein M1 lies under the envelope. 中: 要记住的包膜相关蛋白：HA、NA、M2；包膜下是 M1 matrix。
• EN
HA has three exam-safe roles: receptor binding to sialic acid, membrane fusion in endosomes, and major immunogenicity / target of neutralizing antibodies. 中: HA 三个稳得分作用：与唾液酸受体结合、在内体中介导膜融合、是主要免疫原和中和抗体靶点。
• EN
H = hemagglutinin; N = neuraminidase. Human subtypes commonly cited: H1N1 and H3N2. 中: H = hemagglutinin；N = neuraminidase。人群中最常写的亚型：H1N1 和 H3N2。
• EN
Antigenic drift = point mutations, mainly in HA; antigenic shift = reassortment and occurs only in influenza A. 中: Antigenic drift = 点突变，主要在 HA；antigenic shift = reassortment，只发生在 influenza A。
• EN
Because influenza is a negative-strand RNA virus, its genome is not directly translatable and the virion must carry its own RNA-dependent RNA polymerase. 中: 因为流感是负链 RNA 病毒，其基因组不能直接翻译，所以 virion 必须自带 RNA-dependent RNA polymerase。
Treatment / 治疗
supportive care in mild disease; neuraminidase inhibitor such as oseltamivir is the classic named antiviral; prevention = seasonal vaccine.

One-line answer / 一句话背诵：Influenza is an enveloped segmented ssRNA(-) virus with HA and NA on the envelope; HA binds sialic acid, mediates fusion, and is the main neutralizing-antibody target.

★★★★★ 2. HIV / 人类免疫缺陷病毒

• EN
Family Retroviridae, genus Lentivirus. Enveloped retrovirus with a diploid RNA genome, matrix p17, cone-shaped capsid p24, RT, integrase and protease. 中: Retroviridae 家族，Lentivirus 属。有包膜逆转录病毒，具有双拷贝 RNA、matrix p17、锥形 capsid p24、RT、integrase 和 protease。
• EN
Target cells: CD4 T lymphocytes mainly; also monocytes/macrophages, dendritic-lineage cells and microglia. 中: 靶细胞：主要是 CD4 T 淋巴细胞；还包括单核/巨噬细胞、树突状细胞谱系和 microglia。
• EN
Receptor and coreceptors: CD4 first, then CCR5 or CXCR4. 中: 受体和共受体：先 CD4，再 CCR5 或 CXCR4。
• EN
Transmission: sexual, blood-borne, and mother-to-child including pregnancy, delivery and breastfeeding. 中: 传播：性传播、血液传播、母婴传播，包括妊娠、分娩和哺乳。
• EN
Natural history: primary infection → clinical latency → AIDS. 中: 自然病程：初感染 → 临床潜伏期 → AIDS。
• EN
Marker evolution: viral load very high in primary infection, lower at set point during clinical latency, then rises again in AIDS; CD4 falls early, partially recovers, then progressively declines and becomes very low in AIDS. 中: 指标变化：初感染时 viral load 很高，临床潜伏期降至 set point，AIDS 再上升；CD4 先下降、部分回升，然后持续下降，AIDS 时很低。
• EN
Why HIV is highly variable: low fidelity RT / no proofreading, huge replication dynamics, and recombination during reverse transcription. 中: HIV 高变异原因：RT 低保真且无校对、复制量极大、逆转录时发生重组。
Treatment / 治疗
combination antiretroviral therapy (ART). Key exam words = reverse transcriptase inhibitors, integrase inhibitors, protease inhibitors.
Reservoirs / 储存库
lymph nodes, GALT, blood, CNS and other tissues; persistence continues despite therapy.

★★★★★ 3. HBV and HCV / HBV 与 HCV

HBV / 乙肝病毒

• EN
Family Hepadnaviridae. Enveloped virus with an icosahedral capsid and a partially double-stranded circular DNA genome of about 3.2 kb. 中: Hepadnaviridae 家族。有包膜、二十面体 capsid，基因组为约 3.2 kb 的部分双链环状 DNA。
• EN
HBV receptor: NTCP, coherent with hepatic tropism. 中: HBV 受体：NTCP，与肝嗜性一致。
• EN
Key replication logic: rcDNA enters the nucleus → becomes cccDNA → cccDNA transcribes viral RNAs including pgRNA → pgRNA is packaged with polymerase → reverse transcription makes the negative DNA strand → RNase H degrades pgRNA → an RNA oligo primes incomplete positive-strand DNA synthesis. 中: 关键复制逻辑：rcDNA 入核 → 变为 cccDNA → cccDNA 转录产生包括 pgRNA 在内的病毒 RNA → pgRNA 与 polymerase 被包装 → 逆转录生成负链 DNA → RNase H 降解 pgRNA → 残余 RNA 片段引发不完整正链 DNA 合成。
• EN
cccDNA persists in hepatocyte nuclei and explains persistence / reactivation. 中: cccDNA 在肝细胞核内持续存在，是持续感染和再激活的重要原因。
• EN
Useful markers: HBsAg, anti-HBs, anti-HBc IgM, HBeAg, anti-HBe, HBV DNA. 中: 有用标志物：HBsAg、anti-HBs、anti-HBc IgM、HBeAg、anti-HBe、HBV DNA。
Treatment / 治疗
tenofovir or entecavir are the classic named agents; pegylated interferon may also be cited.

Most likely trap / 最易失分：never write HBV as a “standard dsDNA virus” without saying partially double-stranded and reverse transcription from pgRNA.

HCV / 丙肝病毒

• EN
Family Flaviviridae, genus Hepacivirus. Enveloped ssRNA(+) virus of about 9.6 kb. 中: Flaviviridae 家族，Hepacivirus 属。有包膜正链单股 RNA，约 9.6 kb。
• EN
Its genome is translated as one polyprotein, then cleaved by host and viral proteases into structural and non-structural proteins. 中: 其基因组先翻译成一个 polyprotein，再由宿主和病毒蛋白酶切割成结构蛋白和非结构蛋白。
• EN
Blood-borne transmission and frequent chronicity are the two simplest exam tags. 中: 血源传播和高慢性化倾向，是最简洁的两个考试标签。
• EN

Virus structure and modes of transmission / 病毒结构与传播方式

English

A virus is an obligate intracellular parasite containing either DNA or RNA, surrounded by a capsid, with or without an envelope. The capsid protects the genome and may participate in attachment. The envelope carries viral glycoproteins involved in attachment and fusion, but it also makes the virus fragile in the environment; therefore enveloped viruses are usually transmitted by close contact, droplets, blood, sexual or vertical routes, whereas naked viruses are more resistant and are often transmitted by fecal–oral route or indirect contact.

中文

病毒是专性细胞内寄生物，只含一种核酸（DNA 或 RNA），外有 capsid，有些还有 envelope。capsid 保护基因组，也可参与附着；envelope 上有 glycoproteins，负责附着和融合，但也让病毒对热、干燥和清洁剂更敏感。因此包膜病毒更常经近距离接触、飞沫、血液、性传播和母婴传播；无包膜病毒更耐环境，更常见于粪口传播或经污染物间接传播。

Core explanation / 核心解释

Core structure = genome + capsid; together they form the nucleocapsid. Some viruses additionally have an envelope.

Genome can be DNA or RNA, but never both in the same virus particle.

Capsid roles
protect the genome, participate in attachment for naked viruses, and help intracellular transport of the viral genome.

Two major capsid symmetries: helical and icosahedral. Animal viruses with helical capsids are always enveloped.

Envelope = lipid bilayer acquired by budding through a cellular membrane; it carries spikes/glycoproteins for receptor binding and membrane fusion.

The exam-favorite structural element is the envelope: when present, it greatly influences transmission because it is fragile in the environment.

Common transmission routes to mention: respiratory, fecal–oral, sexual, blood-borne, vertical, iatrogenic, and vector-borne.

Common portals of entry to mention: respiratory tract, alimentary tract, urogenital tract, skin/conjunctiva, and inoculation by bite/needle/vector.

Answer logic / 答题思路

1
Start with the universal definition: virus = DNA or RNA + capsid ± envelope.
2
Name the function of each structure rather than just listing them.
3
Shift immediately from structure to epidemiology: explain that environmental resistance depends strongly on the envelope.
4
Give two contrasted examples: influenza/HIV as enveloped viruses versus rotavirus/HAV or adenovirus/rhinovirus as more resistant viruses.
5
Finish by linking transmission route to entry portal, for example droplets → respiratory tract, fecal–oral → alimentary tract, blood/sex → skin–mucosa–urogenital route.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

Do not say viruses can contain both DNA and RNA in one virion.

Do not only say 'enveloped viruses are fragile'; add the epidemiological consequence.

When asked about entry points, give body sites and examples together.

Persistent infection / 持续性感染

English

A persistent infection is a long-term infection in which the viral genome remains in the host because the virus is not cleared by the immune system. The two main categories are latent infection and chronic infection.

中文

持续性感染是指病毒长期保留在宿主体内，因为免疫系统没有将其清除。两大类型是 latent infection（潜伏感染）和 chronic infection（慢性感染）。

Core explanation / 核心解释

Latent infection
viral genome persists in cells, often with little or no production of infectious particles during latency; reactivation can later restore productive replication.

Classic latent viruses: herpesviruses. HSV and VZV persist in neurons; HCMV in myeloid cells; EBV in memory B cells.

Chronic infection
continued production of viral particles over long periods with ongoing transmission and chronic inflammation.

Classic chronic viruses in this module: HBV and HCV; HIV also persists for years with stable reservoirs despite therapy.

Viral factors favoring persistence: low cytopathic effect, latency programs, interference with antigen presentation, replication in immune cells, persistence in sanctuary sites, antigenic variation.

Host factors favoring persistence: weak or inappropriate immune response, immunodeficiency, extreme ages, pregnancy, comorbidities, genetic factors.

Answer logic / 答题思路

1
Begin with a one-line definition.
2
Separate latent infection from chronic infection clearly; the examiner expects the difference in virus production.
3
Give at least one DNA-virus example for latency and one RNA or chronic-hepatitis example for chronic persistence.
4
Add both viral and host factors explicitly because the 2022 synthesis question asked for them.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

Do not confuse latency with complete eradication; the viral genome remains.

Do not define chronic infection without mentioning continued virus production.

If you cite HIV, avoid saying HIV is purely latent; better say HIV has chronic infection with cellular and anatomical reservoirs.

Influenza virus / 流感病毒

English

Influenza virus is an enveloped, segmented negative-sense single-stranded RNA virus with a helical nucleocapsid. Its envelope contains hemagglutinin (HA) and neuraminidase (NA), and each genome segment is associated with the polymerase complex PA/PB1/PB2. Hemagglutinin binds sialic acid, mediates fusion in endosomes, and is a major target of neutralizing antibodies.

中文

流感病毒是有包膜、分节段、负链单股 RNA 病毒，具有螺旋核衣壳。包膜上有 HA 和 NA，每个节段都伴随 PA/PB1/PB2 polymerase complex。HA 负责结合唾液酸受体、介导内体中的融合，并且是主要中和抗体靶点。

Core explanation / 核心解释

Family
Orthomyxoviridae. Human seasonal disease is mainly caused by influenza A and B.
Genome
segmented ssRNA(-); the lecture repeatedly emphasizes 8 segments for influenza A and B.

Because the genome is negative-sense RNA, influenza must carry its own RNA-dependent RNA polymerase in the virion.

Envelope proteins to remember: HA, NA, and M2; matrix protein M1 lies under the envelope.

Hemagglutinin has three exam-relevant roles: receptor binding, membrane fusion, and major immunogenicity.

H = hemagglutinin; N = neuraminidase. Human influenza A subtypes commonly encountered are H1N1 and H3N2.

Antigenic drift = accumulation of point mutations, mainly in HA and to a lesser extent NA, causing seasonal epidemics.

Antigenic shift = reassortment of genome segments between two influenza A viruses; this may cause pandemics and occurs only in influenza A.

Answer logic / 答题思路

1
If asked for structure, answer from outside to inside: envelope with HA/NA/M2 → matrix M1 → helical RNP → segmented RNA associated with polymerase.
2
If asked for HA role, always give three points, not one.
3
If asked why negative-strand viruses carry polymerase, say their genome is not directly translatable and must first be transcribed into mRNA.
4
If asked about drift versus shift, contrast point mutation with reassortment and mention that shift is pandemic-prone and limited to influenza A.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

Do not write that HA and NA are inside the capsid; they are envelope spikes.

Do not forget to mention polymerase with each segment.

Do not confuse antigenic shift with simple mutation.

HIV / 人类免疫缺陷病毒

English

HIV is an enveloped retrovirus with a diploid RNA genome, a cone-shaped capsid, matrix, and gp120/gp41 envelope spikes. It infects CD4+ cells using CD4 plus a coreceptor, mainly CCR5 or CXCR4. Its natural history includes primary infection, clinical latency, and AIDS.

中文

HIV 是有包膜逆转录病毒，具有双拷贝 RNA 基因组、锥形 capsid、matrix，以及 gp120/gp41 包膜刺突。它通过 CD4 受体及 CCR5 或 CXCR4 共受体感染 CD4+ 细胞。自然病程包括初感染、临床潜伏期和 AIDS 阶段。

Core explanation / 核心解释

Classification
Retroviridae, genus Lentivirus.

Main structural elements: envelope, matrix p17, capsid p24, nucleocapsid, reverse transcriptase, integrase, protease, gp120 and gp41.

Main target cells
CD4 T lymphocytes; also monocytes/macrophages, Langerhans cells, microglia and dendritic-lineage cells.

Transmission routes: sexual, blood-borne, and mother-to-child (pregnancy, delivery, breastfeeding).

CCR5 is associated with R5/macrophage-tropic viruses; CXCR4 with X4/T-cell-tropic viruses.

HIV is highly variable because reverse transcriptase has no proofreading, replication is extremely intense, and recombination occurs during reverse transcription.

Primary infection
high viremia and falling CD4 count. Clinical latency: viral load falls to a set point while CD4 cells gradually decline. AIDS: viral load rises again and CD4 count becomes very low.

Persistence is supported by long-lived reservoirs in lymphoid tissues, gut-associated lymphoid tissue, blood, CNS and other anatomical compartments.

Answer logic / 答题思路

1
For 'why is HIV highly variable?', give all three causes: RT errors, replication dynamics, recombination.
2
For the natural history question, always describe both markers together: viral load and CD4 count.
3
For structure, name at least RT, integrase, protease and the envelope spike gp120/gp41.
4
For tropism, write CD4 first and then the coreceptor.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

Do not say HIV has DNA in the infectious particle; it enters as RNA and reverse-transcribes later.

Do not forget breastfeeding when listing vertical transmission.

When discussing clinical latency, avoid implying the virus is inactive; replication persists.

HBV and HCV / 病毒性肝炎：HBV 与 HCV

English

HBV is an enveloped virus with a partially double-stranded circular DNA genome. After entry into hepatocytes, the genome is converted into cccDNA in the nucleus; cccDNA produces viral RNAs, including pgRNA, which is reverse-transcribed inside nucleocapsids to generate the partially double-stranded DNA genome. HCV is an enveloped positive-sense RNA virus whose 9.6 kb genome is translated into one polyprotein cleaved into structural and non-structural proteins.

中文

HBV 是有包膜病毒，基因组为部分双链环状 DNA。进入肝细胞后，基因组在细胞核中转变为 cccDNA；cccDNA 转录出包括 pgRNA 在内的多种病毒 RNA，随后在核衣壳内进行逆转录，形成部分双链 DNA。HCV 是有包膜正链 RNA 病毒，其约 9.6 kb 的基因组先翻译成一个 polyprotein，再被切割为结构蛋白和非结构蛋白。

Core explanation / 核心解释

HBV
family Hepadnaviridae; enveloped, icosahedral capsid, partially dsDNA circular genome (~3.2 kb).
HBV receptor
NTCP, consistent with liver tropism.
HBV nuclear step
relaxed circular DNA is repaired into cccDNA, a persistent minichromosome that can remain in hepatocytes and reactivate.

HBV replication logic: cccDNA → transcription of pgRNA and other RNAs → packaging with polymerase → reverse transcription to make (-) DNA → RNase H degrades pgRNA → residual RNA primes incomplete (+) DNA synthesis.

HBV markers worth remembering: HBsAg, anti-HBs, anti-HBc IgM, HBeAg, anti-HBe, HBV DNA.

HCV
family Flaviviridae, genus Hepacivirus; enveloped, ssRNA(+), about 9.6 kb, many genotypes/subtypes, quasi-species.

HCV protein expression strategy: one ORF, one polyprotein, cleaved by host and viral proteases into mature proteins.

HCV is mainly blood-borne and frequently becomes chronic; anti-HCV antibodies are not protective.

Answer logic / 答题思路

1
For HBV replication, write the nuclear cccDNA step before the reverse-transcription step; that is the most tested detail.
2
Do not stop at 'HBV replicates by reverse transcriptase'; explain where the RNA intermediate comes from.
3
For HCV structure, name the family, genome polarity, and polyprotein strategy.
4
When asked about chronicity, note that newborns have a much higher chronicity risk for HBV than adults, whereas HCV often chronicizes across infections.

Do not call HBV a standard dsDNA virus without mentioning that the genome is only partially double-stranded.

Do not write that cccDNA can be easily cleared; persistence is precisely why HBV is difficult.

For HCV, do not say there are multiple separate mRNAs for each protein at the start; the core answer is polyprotein cleavage.

English

Dengue virus is transmitted by Aedes mosquitoes and exists as four serotypes. English

Herpesviruses are enveloped linear double-stranded DNA viruses with an icosahedral capsid, a tegument and an envelope. They establish lifelong latency and may reactivate later. High-yield examples are HSV-1, HSV-2, VZV, HCMV and EBV.

Common structure
linear dsDNA genome, icosahedral capsid, tegument, envelope with glycoproteins.
Subfamilies
alpha-, beta- and gammaherpesvirinae.

Figure analysis method / 图像分析题通用模板

English

Most virology figure analyses in the uploaded papers ask whether a host factor is proviral or antiviral. The answer must first describe the experimental setup and readout, then interpret the controls, then conclude whether loss of the factor decreases or increases infection, and finally state whether rescue/trans-complementation restores the phenotype.

中文

你给的几套图像分析题，本质都在问某个宿主因子对病毒感染到底是促进还是抑制。答题顺序应是：先写实验设计和 readout，再解读 controls，然后判断敲低/敲除后感染是下降还是上升，最后再看 rescue / trans-complementation 是否把表型补回来。

Core explanation / 核心解释

Core recurring examples in the uploaded exams: HCV–RACK1, ZIKV–Axl, DENV–DPM1.

Always identify the model: cell line, infection conditions, MOI, time post-infection, whether the factor was knocked down, knocked out or rescued.

Know the readouts
RT-qPCR = relative viral RNA; Western blot / immunoblot = protein level or validation of knockdown/rescue; flow cytometry with anti-envelope antibody = percentage of infected cells; anti-dsRNA staining = viral RNA replication intermediates; DAPI = nuclei/cell counting.
Know the controls
mock/no virus, parental cells, non-targeting siRNA (siNT), positive controls, rescue/trans-complementation.

Know the logic of causality: if KO/siRNA lowers infection and rescue restores it, the factor is proviral or a host-dependency factor; if loss of the factor increases infection, it is antiviral.

MOI = multiplicity of infection. siRNA reduces expression; siNT is the negative control; KO means durable gene deletion.

Answer logic / 答题思路

Q1 style
describe the timeline, conditions, techniques, controls and what is measured.
Q2 style
explain the result panel by panel and always comment on both the experiment and the controls.
Q3 style
give the biological conclusion in one sentence: 'protein X is required for efficient infection' or 'protein X restricts infection'.
Bonus logic
if rescue restores the parental phenotype, it strengthens specificity and excludes many off-target interpretations.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

Do not jump straight to the conclusion without first naming what each panel actually measures.

Do not say qPCR measures infectious particles; it measures viral nucleic acid level.

Do not forget that anti-dsRNA staining is used as a marker of replication intermediates in RNA-virus infection.

These topics are less repeatedly tested in the uploaded virology exams, but they are directly present in the course slides and can help in synthesis questions, examples, or oral recall. 这些部分不是最优先，但在综合题举例、短答补充或考场联想时很有用。

1
RSV and bronchiolitis / RSV 与毛细支气管炎

RSV is a negative-sense ssRNA virus of Pneumoviridae with envelope proteins F, G and SH. The F protein is the key fusion protein.

Bronchiolitis is almost always viral in infants; RSV is the major cause, followed by rhinovirus, metapneumovirus, adenovirus and others.

Severe infant bronchiolitis signs to remember: wheezing, rapid or labored breathing, poor feeding, dehydration, cyanosis and lethargy.

Diagnosis is mainly clinical; severe cases can be tested by nasopharyngeal swab and RT-PCR.

Prevention now matters: nirsevimab (passive monoclonal antibody against F) for newborns and maternal vaccination with ABRYSVO in late pregnancy.

2
SARS-CoV-2 / 新冠病毒

SARS-CoV-2 is an enveloped positive-sense RNA virus of about 30 kb with helical nucleocapsid N and structural proteins S, E, M and N.

Spike S is a trimer divided into S1 and S2; S1 contains the receptor-binding domain and binds ACE2, whereas S2 mediates fusion.

Its proteins are produced in two phases worth remembering: early translation of polyproteins pp1a/pp1ab, then subgenomic mRNAs for structural proteins.

This is a good example of a positive-strand RNA virus with polyprotein translation and later structural gene expression.

3
Yellow fever / Huangrebing（黄热病）

Yellow fever virus is an orthoflavivirus transmitted mainly by Aedes aegypti and associated with non-human primates in enzootic cycles.

Most infections are asymptomatic or mild, but severe forms appear after a short remission period and are characterized by hemorrhagic disease, liver and kidney injury, and high mortality.

Useful contrast with dengue: both are mosquito-borne flaviviruses, but yellow fever strongly evokes hepatic failure and jaundice.

4
Chikungunya / 基孔肯雅

CHIKV belongs to Togaviridae, genus Alphavirus; it is an enveloped positive-sense RNA virus.

Acute disease combines fever, rash and very marked arthralgia; 30–40% of patients may have chronic recurrent joint pain.

It is a useful example if you need another arbovirus besides dengue and Zika.

5
Ebola / 埃博拉

Ebola virus is a filamentous enveloped negative-sense RNA virus of Filoviridae.

Human infection follows contact with infected animal fluids or direct/indirect human contact with infected body fluids.

Pathophysiology words worth remembering: inoculation through mucosa or skin wounds, dendritic-cell infection, viremia, widespread dissemination, tissue and vascular damage, coagulation disorders.

6
HAV and HEV in one glance / HAV 与 HEV 速看

HAV and HEV are classic fecal–oral hepatitis viruses causing mainly acute hepatitis; HAV does not chronicize, HEV chronicity is rare and mainly a problem in immunocompromised hosts.

These are useful examples when you want to contrast acute self-limited infection with chronic HBV/HCV infection.

Virus structure and modes of transmission / 病毒结构与传播方式

老师常怎么问 / Typical exam angle

• 综合题最爱问：What structures make up viruses? What are their functions?

• 然后继续追问：How does virus structure affect transmission?

• 老师不满意只写“enveloped viruses are fragile”，必须写出传播学后果。

最简得分答案 / Minimal scoring answer

EN
A virus is an obligate intracellular parasite containing either DNA or RNA, surrounded by a capsid and sometimes by an envelope. The capsid protects the genome and may participate in attachment. The envelope carries glycoproteins involved in attachment and fusion, but because it is fragile in the environment, enveloped viruses are usually transmitted by close contact, droplets, blood, sexual or vertical routes, whereas naked viruses are more resistant and are often transmitted indirectly or by the fecal–oral route.
中
病毒是专性细胞内寄生物，只含一种核酸（DNA 或 RNA），外有衣壳，有些还有包膜。衣壳保护基因组，并可参与附着；包膜上的糖蛋白参与结合和融合。但包膜在环境中较脆弱，因此有包膜病毒更常经近距离接触、飞沫、血液、性传播或母婴传播；无包膜病毒更耐环境，更常见于间接接触或粪口传播。

答题骨架 / Answer skeleton

1) Define virus briefly: either DNA or RNA + capsid ± envelope.

2) State capsid functions: protect genome; attachment for naked viruses; transport of genome.

3) State envelope functions: glycoproteins / spikes for attachment and fusion.

4) Make the epidemiological link: envelope fragile → close transmission routes; naked virus resistant → fecal–oral / indirect routes.

5) If asked about entry points, always pair body sites with examples.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

• Do not say viruses contain both DNA and RNA in one virion as their genome.

• Do not stop at “enveloped viruses are fragile”; write “therefore … transmission”.

• When writing entry points, give the body site and examples together.

Useful one-line contrast: Helical animal viruses are enveloped; icosahedral viruses may be naked or enveloped.

Key vocabulary to recognize: capsid, nucleocapsid, envelope, glycoproteins, spikes, entry points, fecal–oral, vector-borne, vertical route.

Persistent infection / 持续性感染

老师常怎么问 / Typical exam angle

• Define what a persistent infection is.

• Describe different types of persistent infection, with host factors and viral factors.

• Distinguish latent infection from chronic infection.

最简得分答案 / Minimal scoring answer

EN
Persistent infection is a long-term infection in which the viral genome is maintained in the host because the virus is not cleared by the immune system. The two main categories are latent infection and chronic infection.
中
持续性感染是指病毒基因组在宿主体内长期保留，因为免疫系统没有将其清除。主要分为潜伏感染（latent infection）和慢性感染（chronic infection）。

答题骨架 / Answer skeleton

1) Give the definition in one clean sentence.

2) Separate latent infection from chronic infection.

3) For latent infection: no or minimal production of infectious particles during latency; possible reactivation.

4) For chronic infection: continuous viral production and long-term transmission.

5) Add host factors and viral factors that favor persistence.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

• Do not mix up latency and chronicity.

• Do not only list viruses; explain why persistence is possible.

• If you mention immune escape, be specific (sanctuary sites, low antigen presentation, antigenic variation, etc.).

高频例子 / High-yield examples

Latent infection
HSV, VZV, EBV, CMV.
Chronic infection
HBV, HCV, HIV.

补充说明 / Clarifying notes

Typical viral factors: low cytopathic effect, latency, sanctuary sites, interference with antigen presentation, antigenic variation, replication in immune cells.

Typical host factors: immunodeficiency, pregnancy, extreme ages, comorbidities, genetic factors.

5
Influenza / 流感病毒

老师常怎么问 / Typical exam angle

• Detail the structure of the influenza virus. What is the role of hemagglutinin?

• What do H and N stand for? Which influenza A subtypes are common in humans?

• Why does a negative-strand virus need to carry its own polymerase?

最简得分答案 / Minimal scoring answer

EN
Influenza virus is an enveloped virus with a helical nucleocapsid and a segmented negative-sense single-stranded RNA genome. Its envelope contains hemagglutinin and neuraminidase. Hemagglutinin binds sialic acid, mediates fusion in endosomes, and is a major target of neutralizing antibodies.
中
流感病毒是有包膜、螺旋核衣壳、分节段负链单股 RNA 病毒。包膜上有血凝素（HA）和神经氨酸酶（NA）。HA 负责结合唾液酸受体、介导内体融合，并且是主要中和抗体靶点。

答题骨架 / Answer skeleton

1) Structure: enveloped, helical capsid / RNP, segmented ssRNA(-), polymerase complex PA/PB1/PB2, M1, M2, HA, NA.

2) HA roles: attachment (HA1 binds sialic acid), fusion (HA2 fusion peptide), major antigen.

3) H = hemagglutinin; N = neuraminidase.

4) Human influenza A subtypes usually discussed in class/exams: H1N1 and H3N2.

5) Drift = mutations; shift = reassortment; shift only in influenza A.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

• Do not forget to say “negative-strand RNA”, not just “RNA virus”.

• Do not confuse HA function (attachment + fusion) with NA function.

• If asked about polymerase, explicitly say negative-strand RNA is not directly translatable.

补充说明 / Clarifying notes

One-line answer for polymerase question: Negative-strand RNA must first be transcribed into mRNA, so the virion must carry a viral RNA-dependent RNA polymerase.

One-line answer for drift vs shift: Drift = point mutations causing seasonal epidemics; shift = reassortment causing potential pandemics.

6
HIV / 人类免疫缺陷病毒

老师常怎么问 / Typical exam angle

• What are the stages of HIV infection?

• Why is HIV highly variable?

• How do CD4 T cells and viral load evolve during the natural history of infection?

最简得分答案 / Minimal scoring answer

EN
HIV is an enveloped retrovirus with a diploid RNA genome and envelope spikes made of gp120 and gp41. The main stages of infection are primary infection, clinical latency and AIDS. HIV is highly variable because reverse transcriptase has no proofreading activity, viral replication is intense, and recombination occurs during reverse transcription.
中
HIV 是有包膜的逆转录病毒，具有二倍体 RNA 基因组，包膜刺突为 gp120/gp41。感染主要经历初感染、临床潜伏期和 AIDS。HIV 高度变异的原因是逆转录酶无校对功能、病毒复制量极大，并且逆转录过程中可发生重组。

答题骨架 / Answer skeleton

1) Structure: enveloped, cone-shaped capsid, diploid RNA, RT, integrase, protease, gp120/gp41.

2) Transmission: sexual, blood, mother-to-child (pregnancy, delivery, breastfeeding).

3) Target cells / tropism: CD4 receptor; CCR5 and CXCR4 coreceptors.

4) Natural history: primary infection → clinical latency → AIDS.

5) Marker evolution: viral load high → lower set point → high again; CD4 count drops → partial recovery → progressive fall → very low in AIDS.

6) Variability: no proofreading RT + huge replication + recombination.

Common traps / 容易失分的点

• Do not answer only with the names of the three stages; add CD4 and viral load trends if asked.

• Do not say HIV is variable only because it is an RNA virus; the better answer is no proofreading + replication dynamics + recombination.

• Do not forget that HIV persistence is linked to viral reservoirs.

补充说明 / Clarifying notes

Short sentence for tropism: HIV uses CD4 as receptor and CCR5/CXCR4 as coreceptors.

Short sentence for reservoirs: HIV persists in reservoirs such as lymph nodes, blood, CNS, GALT and other tissues.

Hepatitis viruses most worth scoring with / 最值得拿分的肝炎病毒

这一部分在卷面上最值得背的是 HBV replication 和 HCV structure/protein expression。

7.1 HBV / 乙肝病毒

HBV is an enveloped virus with an icosahedral capsid and a partially double-stranded circular DNA genome (~3.2 kb). It enters hepatocytes via NTCP.

最简答案 / Minimal answer

EN: After entry, the relaxed circular DNA reaches the nucleus and is converted into cccDNA. cccDNA serves as the template for transcription of viral RNAs, including pregenomic RNA. Inside nucleocapsids, the viral polymerase reverse-transcribes pgRNA into the negative DNA strand, RNase H degrades pgRNA, and the positive strand remains incomplete, giving rise to a partially double-stranded DNA genome.

中: HBV 入核后将松弛环状 DNA 修复为 cccDNA；cccDNA 转录产生病毒 RNA，包括 pgRNA；在核衣壳内，病毒聚合酶将 pgRNA 逆转录为负链 DNA，RNase H 降解 pgRNA，再以残余 RNA 片段为引物启动不完整正链 DNA 合成，因此形成部分双链 DNA 基因组。

答题骨架
NTCP → rcDNA to cccDNA → transcription → pgRNA packaging → reverse transcription → incomplete (+) strand → partially dsDNA
易错点
不要忘记 cccDNA；不要把 HBV 当成普通 DNA virus 只写 DNA replication；一定写 reverse transcription。

7.2 HCV / 丙肝病毒

HCV belongs to Flaviviridae (genus Hepacivirus). It is an enveloped positive-strand RNA virus (~9.6 kb) with many genotypes and quasispecies.

最简答案 / Minimal answer

EN: HCV is an enveloped positive-strand RNA virus. Its genome is translated as a single polyprotein, which is then cleaved by host and viral proteases into structural and non-structural proteins.

中: HCV 是有包膜的正链 RNA 病毒。其基因组先翻译成一个长的 polyprotein，然后再由宿主和病毒蛋白酶切割成结构蛋白和非结构蛋白。

答题骨架
family + genome + envelope + one polyprotein + cleavage + chronic blood-borne infection
易错点
不要忘记“positive-strand”；不要把 HCV 写成分节段病毒；不要把 polyprotein strategy 写丢。

8.1 Dengue / 登革热

最简答案 / Minimal answer

EN: Dengue virus is transmitted by Aedes aegypti and Aedes albopictus mosquitoes and has four serotypes. Severe dengue is characterized by plasma leakage, bleeding and organ impairment. The risk is higher during secondary infection because of antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE).

中: 登革热病毒由伊蚊传播，具有 4 个血清型。重症登革热的特征是血浆渗漏、出血和器官损伤。再次感染异型血清型时，由于抗体依赖性增强（ADE），重症风险升高。

8.2 Zika / 寨卡

最简答案 / Minimal answer

EN: Zika virus is transmitted by mosquito bites, sexual contact, blood transfusion and vertical transmission. Most infections are mild or asymptomatic, but complications include Guillain–Barré syndrome and congenital Zika syndrome.

必须会写

易错点
不要漏掉 sexual transmission 和 vertical transmission。

★★★ 2. 核心定义｜Core definitions you should be able to write

Parasite 寄生虫
A living organism dependent on another organism called the host.｜依赖宿主生存的生物。
Parasitism 寄生现象
Unilateral physiological dependence: the parasite benefits while the host is harmed.｜寄生虫获益，宿主受损。

Definitive host 终宿主：Host carrying the sexual form or adult form.｜宿主内存在有性阶段或成虫阶段。

Intermediate host 中间宿主：Host carrying the asexual form or larval form.｜宿主内存在无性阶段或幼虫阶段。

Vector 媒介
A host able to transmit the pathogen actively.｜能主动把病原传给下一宿主。

Biological vector 生物媒介：Parasite multiplies or matures in it; essential to the life cycle.｜寄生虫在其中发育/增殖，是生活史必需。

Mechanical vector 机械媒介：Only transports the parasite; not essential.｜只运输寄生虫，不是生活史必需。

Reservoir 储存宿主/储存库
Animal, plant, soil, or water ensuring parasite survival and availability for transmission.｜维持寄生虫生存与传播来源。

Monoxenous life cycle 单宿主生活史：Direct cycle: one host, sometimes host + environment.｜直接生活史。

Heteroxenous life cycle 多宿主生活史：Indirect cycle: several successive hosts are necessary.｜间接生活史。

Paratenic host 转续宿主：Optional host with no parasite transformation.｜寄生虫在其中不进一步发育，只是等待。

Dead-end host 死胡同宿主：Parasite enters but useful onward transmission stops.｜进入人体后传播链终止。

Exam traps｜常见陷阱

Plasmodium：human is the intermediate host; mosquito is the definitive host.

Taenia：human is the final host when the adult worm lives in the intestine.

Snails in schistosomiasis are intermediate hosts, but not vectors in the strict sense used by the slides.

Dog is an important reservoir for Leishmania infantum in zoonotic leishmaniasis.

★★★ 3. 生活史与感染形式｜Life-cycle logic and infective forms

Direct / monoxenous cycle：same host or host + environment. 典型例子：Giardia intestinalis, Entamoeba histolytica, Ascaris lumbricoides, Enterobius vermicularis.

Indirect / heteroxenous cycle：successive hosts are necessary. 典型例子：Toxoplasma gondii, Plasmodium spp., Leishmania spp., Echinococcus granulosus.

Main infective forms to memorize：embryonated eggs, mature cysts, oocysts, metacercariae, larvae. Leishmania 要单独记 promastigote / amastigote。

Main resistant forms in the environment：eggs, cysts, oocysts, spores, encysted larvae. 这些形式耐环境，利于传播。

★★★ 4. 传播方式总复习｜Transmission modes with examples

★★★ 4.1 Oral transmission｜经口传播

By contaminated water and food → faecal peril. 这是课件中最完整、最容易出题的传播方式。

Monoxenous protists：Entamoeba histolytica, Giardia intestinalis, Cryptosporidium parvum, Cyclospora cayetanensis, Isospora belli, microsporidia.

Monoxenous helminths：Ascaris lumbricoides, Enterobius vermicularis, Trichuris trichiura.

Heteroxenous oral parasites：Toxoplasma gondii, Hymenolepis nana, Echinococcus granulosus, Angiostrongylus cantonensis, Dracunculus medinensis.

★★★ 4.2 Muco-cutaneous transmission｜黏膜/皮肤传播

Through water and moist soil：Strongyloides stercoralis, hookworm, schistosomiasis, cutaneous larva migrans.

Through dry environment：myiases, tungiasis (Tunga penetrans).

Through human contact：scabies caused by Sarcoptes scabiei.

★★★ 4.3 Vector-borne transmission｜媒介传播

Night/evening vectors：malaria (Anopheles), leishmaniases (sandflies), lymphatic filariases (Culex/Anopheles/Aedes/Mansonia).

Diurnal vectors
human African trypanosomiasis (Glossina), loiasis (Chrysops), onchocerciasis (blackflies).

Terrestrial vector-borne disease：Chagas disease by triatomine bug.

★★ 4.4 Other routes｜其他传播途径

Sexual transmission：uro-genital trichomoniasis → Trichomonas vaginalis.

Congenital transmission：toxoplasmosis; malaria may also be congenital.

Blood transfusion
malaria, trypanosomiases, filariases, toxoplasmosis.
Organ transplant

★★★ 4.5 Quick logic｜快速判断逻辑

If infection comes from water/food and only one host is needed → think monoxenous oral parasite.

If there is a vector bite and the parasite changes form inside the vector → think heteroxenous vector-borne parasite.

If infective forms penetrate through skin or mucosa, especially from moist soil or freshwater → think muco-cutaneous transmission.

If the adult or sexual form is in humans → humans are the definitive host.

If humans are infected accidentally and transmission usually stops in humans → think dead-end host.

★★★ 6.1 Antileishmanial drugs｜抗利什曼药物

核心定义必须会写｜Core definitions you should be able to write
Parasite 寄生虫
A living organism dependent on another organism called the host. 寄生虫是依赖宿主生存的生物。
Parasitism 寄生现象
Unilateral physiological dependence: the parasite benefits while the host is harmed. 单方面获益、宿主受损。

Definitive host 终宿主：Host carrying the sexual form or adult form of the parasite. 宿主内存在寄生虫有性阶段或成虫阶段。

Intermediate host 中间宿主：Host carrying the asexual form or larval form. 宿主内存在无性阶段或幼虫阶段。

Vector 媒介
A host able to transmit the pathogen actively to another host. 能主动把病原传给下一宿主。

Biological vector 生物媒介：The parasite multiplies or matures in it; it is essential to the life cycle. 寄生虫会在其中发育或增殖，是生活史必需。

Mechanical vector 机械媒介：Only transports the parasite; not essential to the life cycle. 只起运输作用，不是生活史必需。

Reservoir 储存宿主/储存库
Animal, plant, soil, or water ensuring parasite survival and availability for transmission. 维持寄生虫长期生存并促进传播。

Monoxenous life cycle 单宿主生活史：Direct cycle: the parasite develops in one host, sometimes with part of development in the environment. 直接生活史。

Heteroxenous life cycle 多宿主生活史：Indirect cycle: mandatory transformations occur in successive hosts. 间接生活史。

Paratenic host 转续宿主/待机宿主：Optional host in which no parasite transformation occurs. 寄生虫在其中不进一步发育，只是等待。

Dead-end host 死胡同宿主：The parasite enters but does not continue a useful transmission cycle. 进入人体后传播链基本终止。

Exam trap｜常见陷阱

Plasmodium：human is the intermediate host; mosquito is the definitive host.

Taenia：human is the final host when adult worms live in the intestine.

Snails in schistosomiasis are intermediate hosts, but not vectors in the strict sense used by the slides.

Dog is an important reservoir for Leishmania infantum in zoonotic leishmaniasis.

3
生活史与感染逻辑｜Life-cycle logic and infective forms

这部分不是为了让你画全图，而是为了让你在题目里快速判断：感染阶段是什么、从哪里进入人体、人体在生活史里扮演什么角色。

Direct / monoxenous cycle：same host or host + environment. 例如 Giardia intestinalis、Entamoeba histolytica、Ascaris lumbricoides、Enterobius vermicularis。

Indirect / heteroxenous cycle：successive hosts are necessary. 例如 Toxoplasma gondii、Plasmodium spp.、Leishmania spp.、Echinococcus granulosus。

Infective forms to memorize：embryonated eggs、mature cysts、oocysts、metacercariae、larvae、promastigotes / amastigotes.

Forms of resistance in the environment：eggs, cysts, oocysts, spores, encysted larvae. 这些形式往往耐受环境、利于传播。

传播方式总复习｜Transmission modes with examples

按老师课件，主要传播方式包括 oral, muco-cutaneous, respiratory, vector inoculation, sexual, congenital, transfusion, transplantation。卷面最爱考的是 oral / contact / vector / sexual 这几类。

4.1 Oral transmission｜经口传播

By contaminated water and food → faecal peril. 课件中最完整。

Monoxenous protists：Entamoeba histolytica, Giardia intestinalis, Cryptosporidium parvum, Cyclospora cayetanensis, Isospora belli。

Monoxenous helminths：Ascaris lumbricoides, Enterobius vermicularis, Trichuris trichiura。

Heteroxenous oral parasites：Toxoplasma gondii, Hymenolepis nana, Echinococcus granulosus, Angiostrongylus cantonensis, Dracunculus medinensis。

4.2 Muco-cutaneous transmission｜黏膜/皮肤传播

Through water and moist soil：Strongyloides stercoralis, hookworm, schistosomiasis, cutaneous larva migrans。

Through dry environment：myiases, tungiasis (Tunga penetrans)。

Through human contact：scabies caused by Sarcoptes scabiei.

4.3 Vector-borne transmission｜媒介传播

Night/evening vectors：malaria (Anopheles), leishmaniases (sandflies), lymphatic filariases (Culex/Anopheles/Aedes/Mansonia)。

Diurnal vectors
human African trypanosomiasis (Glossina), loiasis (Chrysops), onchocerciasis (blackflies)。

Terrestrial vector-borne disease：Chagas disease by triatomine bug.

4.4 Other routes｜其他传播途径

Sexual transmission：uro-genital trichomoniasis → Trichomonas vaginalis.

Congenital transmission：toxoplasmosis; malaria may also be congenital.

Blood transfusion
malaria, trypanosomiases, filariases, toxoplasmosis.
Organ transplant
toxoplasmosis.
Airborne
pneumocystosis is listed in the slide set, but it is fungal, not a classical parasitology example.

4.5 一眼判断题模板｜How to classify a parasite in one glance

Quick logic｜快速判断逻辑

If infection comes from water/food and only one host is needed → think monoxenous oral parasite.

If there is a vector bite and the parasite changes form inside the vector → think heteroxenous vector-borne parasite.

If larvae penetrate skin from moist soil or freshwater → think muco-cutaneous transmission.

If adults live in the human intestine and reproduction is in humans → humans may be the final host.

If humans are only accidentally infected and transmission usually stops → dead-end host.

5.4 Antileishmanial drugs｜抗利什曼药物答题版

说明
上传课件明确给出了 amphotericin B 可用于 visceral / muco-cutaneous leishmaniasis，但旧卷也直接考过 antimonials 的机制与耐药。因此下面把 slide-based 内容和常规药理答题模板合在一起，方便你考试直接写。

Drug

Mechanism of action / 机制

High-yield adverse/resistance note

How to use in an answer

Pentavalent antimonials
(SbV)

Reduced to more active SbIII; disrupt parasite redox metabolism (trypanothione), energy metabolism, and macromolecule synthesis.

Resistance: decreased uptake, reduced activation, increased efflux/sequestration, increased thiol/trypanothione defenses.

Classic old-exam choice.

Amphotericin B / liposomal AmB

Binds ergosterol-like membrane sterols → pore formation → membrane leakage → parasite death.

Nephrotoxicity is the key toxicity; liposomal forms are less toxic. Slide set explicitly notes activity in visceral and muco-cutaneous leishmaniasis.

Safest modern choice.

Miltefosine

Alters membrane lipid metabolism and signaling; promotes apoptosis-like death in Leishmania.

Oral drug; GI toxicity and teratogenicity are classic points.

Good optional third example.

Fast answer template｜抗利什曼药机制速答模板

Pentavalent antimonials: after activation, they impair parasite redox and energy metabolism; resistance is due to decreased uptake / activation and increased efflux or thiol-based detoxification.

Amphotericin B: binds membrane sterols, forms pores, increases permeability, and kills the parasite; liposomal amphotericin B is less toxic.

If you only remember two drugs, choose antimonials + amphotericin B.

6.1 Sanitization / monitoring methods｜监测与卫生学控制

Risk identification and vector monitoring.

6.6 Mosquito-control question: safest 4-line answer

You can write this directly｜几乎可直接上卷

Two examples to control mosquitoes are: (1) elimination of stagnant water and breeding sites, and (2) insecticide-treated bed nets.

Other valid methods include larval surveillance, CO₂ or light traps, pyrethroid spraying, methoprene, and Bti biological larvicides.

The logic is either to reduce vector density or to reduce human-vector contact.

Q1. Give the name of a parasite having a monoxenous life cycle and transmitted orally.

Giardia intestinalis. It is transmitted orally by ingestion of mature cysts and has a monoxenous life cycle.

Q2. Give the name of a parasite having a heteroxenous life cycle and transmitted orally.

Toxoplasma gondii. It has a heteroxenous cycle and humans can be infected orally by oocysts or bradyzoites in undercooked meat.

Q3. Give the name of a parasite transmitted by contact (muco-cutaneous transmission).

Strongyloides stercoralis. Infective larvae penetrate the skin from moist soil.

Q4. Give the name of a parasite transmitted by a vector.

Plasmodium falciparum. It is transmitted by the bite of an Anopheles mosquito.

Q5. Give the name of a sexually transmitted parasite.

Trichomonas vaginalis.

Q6. Give the name of a parasite for which humans are the final host.

Taenia saginata.

问Q7. What is the difference between definitive host and intermediate host?

The definitive host carries the sexual or adult form, whereas the intermediate host carries the asexual or larval form.

问Q8. What are the two forms of Leishmania in its life cycle?

Promastigote in the sandfly and amastigote in macrophages.

Elimination of stagnant water and insecticide-treated bed nets.

Artemisinin derivatives and chloroquine. (Other valid examples may also be accepted depending on the context.)

10.1 Forms of parasitism and host specificity｜寄生类型与宿主特异性

Accidental parasitism 偶然寄生：parasite enters an unusual situation by chance. Example: fly larva in a wound.

Optional parasitism 兼性寄生：normally free-living or saprophytic organisms can become parasitic under some conditions. Fungi are the classic example.

Obligatory parasitism 专性寄生：the parasite must spend part or all of its life as a parasite. Temporary examples include Fasciola and Strongyloides; permanent examples include filariae and Plasmodium.

Erratic parasitism 异位寄生：the parasite is found in an abnormal host or abnormal site. Toxocara canis in humans is the classic dead-end / larva migrans example.

Stenoxenic parasites 狭宿主寄生虫：high host fidelity. Euryxenic parasites 广宿主寄生虫：low host fidelity, therefore usually harder to control in the field.

10.2 Resistant / infective forms worth recognizing｜需要眼熟的抗性/感染形式

Quiescent eggs 静止卵：not infectious when excreted; they need maturation outside the host. Example: Ascaris lumbricoides.

Embryonated eggs 胚胎化虫卵：infectious as soon as they are eliminated or after a very short delay. Example: Enterobius vermicularis.

Cysts 包囊：typical resistant form of protists. High-yield examples: Entamoeba histolytica and Giardia intestinalis.

Oocysts 卵囊：important for Toxoplasma gondii and Cryptosporidium parvum. Do not mix them up with simple cysts or eggs.

Metacercariae 囊蚴：encysted larval trematode stage, usually acquired by ingestion of contaminated aquatic plants or intermediate hosts.

Tachyzoites / bradyzoites 速殖子 / 缓殖子：very typical for Toxoplasma gondii. Tachyzoites spread rapidly; bradyzoites persist in tissues.

10.3 Lower-yield oral transmission examples｜经口传播里相对次重点的例子

Cyclospora cayetanensis：sporulated oocysts from contaminated water or food; monoxenous life cycle; diarrhea.

Isospora belli：mature oocysts containing sporozoites; monoxenous cycle; diarrhea, especially worth recognizing in immunocompromised settings.

Angiostrongylus cantonensis / A. costaricensis：oral acquisition from contaminated food or intermediate hosts such as snails/slugs; meningitis or digestive disease.

Dracunculus medinensis：infection by drinking water containing Cyclops; classic old-school example of control by water filtration.

Echinococcus granulosus：heteroxenous oral transmission through embryonated eggs; hydatid cyst mainly in the liver, sometimes lung or brain.

10.4 Muco-cutaneous / contact transmission beyond the safest answers｜接触传播的补充识别

Strongyloides stercoralis：still the safest exam answer for moist-soil skin penetration.

Hookworms 钩虫：another classic moist-soil skin penetration example; useful if the examiner asks for another valid parasite.

Schistosoma spp.：cercariae can penetrate the skin in contaminated fresh water. Important because the snail is an intermediate host, not a vector.

Cutaneous larva migrans 皮肤幼虫移行症：classically from animal hookworm larvae penetrating human skin.

Sarcoptes scabiei：contagious dermatitis by close human contact; useful if the question shifts from life cycle to cutaneous parasitosis.

Tunga penetrans：sand flea / tungiasis; a less central but recognizable muco-cutaneous ectoparasite example.

10.5 Vector-borne names worth recognizing even if not top priority｜最好眼熟的虫媒名称

Human African trypanosomiasis 非洲人类锥虫病：Trypanosoma brucei gambiense and T. b. rhodesiense, transmitted by tsetse flies; CNS involvement, coma, death.

Chagas disease 查加斯病：Trypanosoma cruzi, transmitted by triatomine bugs; cardiomyopathy and digestive megasyndromes.

Lymphatic filariasis 淋巴丝虫病：Wuchereria bancrofti, Brugia malayi, Brugia timori; mosquito-borne; lymphedema.

Loiasis 罗阿丝虫病：transmitted by Chrysops; Calabar swellings and eye worm.

Onchocerciasis onchocerciasis / river blindness 盘尾丝虫病：transmitted by blackflies; itchy skin lesions, nodules, visual impairment, blindness.

10.6 Leishmania and environmental change｜利什曼原虫与环境变化

Climate change 气候变化：changes in temperature and rainfall can modify vector distribution and seasonality.

Globalization 全球化：trade, transport, and migration facilitate the spread of vectors and parasites.

Human pressure 人类活动：deforestation, urbanization, and water management increase contact between humans, vectors, and reservoirs.

Leishmania infantum and dogs：dog is an important reservoir in the Mediterranean basin; this is worth remembering because it links zoonosis, reservoir, and vector transmission in one example.

Aedes albopictus / tiger mosquito and other invasive vectors：these examples are more eco-epidemiology than classical parasitology, but they help explain why emerging vector-borne diseases matter.
