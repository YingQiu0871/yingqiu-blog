Part 2 图题万能答题框架（必背）

1) 先把图“说清楚”

图类型识别
通路图 / Western blot / FACS / IHC / 存活曲线 / 迁移实验 / 代谢读数 / 剂量-反应等。Identify the figure type: pathway / WB / FACS / IHC / survival curve / migration assay / metabolic readout / dose-response, etc.
先写
实验对象（细胞/小鼠/患者样本）、处理（药物/基因敲除/缺氧等）、读出指标（蛋白/荧光/细胞数）。First state: model (cells/mice/patient sample), perturbation (drug/KO/hypoxia), and readout (protein/fluorescence/cell counts).

2) 再写“对照组-变量-结论”三件套

对照组是什么？（vehicle / WT / untreated / isotype / normoxia）。What is the control? (vehicle / WT / untreated / isotype / normoxia).

变量改变了什么？（↑/↓ 某通路、某标志物、细胞周期分布、凋亡比例、迁移速度）。What changed? (↑/↓ pathway, marker, cell-cycle distribution, apoptosis fraction, migration speed).

一句话结论
处理 → 机制方向（激活/抑制）→ 表型（增殖/死亡/侵袭/免疫逃逸）。One-line conclusion: perturbation → mechanism direction (activate/inhibit) → phenotype (proliferation/death/invasion/immune escape).

3) 最后补“机制 + 临床/治疗意义”

机制
尽量用课件词（例：PD-1/PD-L1、RIPK1/3-MLKL、MCT1/4、APC/C、E-cad/N-cad switch）。Mechanism: use slide keywords (e.g., PD-1/PD-L1, RIPK1/3-MLKL, MCT1/4, APC/C, E-cad/N-cad switch).
治疗意义
对应靶点/耐药/生物标志物/联合治疗思路（例：诱导非凋亡性死亡作为“backup”）。Clinical meaning: link to target/resistance/biomarker/combination (e.g., trigger non-apoptotic death as a backup).

模块1：Introduction — how a cell becomes cancerous

癌症=多步骤演化（clonal expansion）+ 获得多个Hallmarks/Enabling characteristics。Cancer = multistep evolution (clonal expansion) + acquisition of hallmarks/enabling characteristics.

典型驱动通路
RAS-RAF-MEK-ERK（RAS突变常通过GTPase活性受损→持续GTP结合态）。Key driver: RAS-RAF-MEK-ERK (oncogenic RAS often impairs GTPase activity → persistent GTP-bound state).
抑癌核心
pRb 与 p53（DNA损伤/生长信号整合→停滞/修复/凋亡/衰老）。Core suppressors: pRb and p53 (integrate DNA damage and growth signals → arrest/repair/apoptosis/senescence).
复制永生
端粒酶（telomerase）过表达→端粒维持。Replicative immortality: telomerase overexpression maintains telomeres.
侵袭转移
E-cadherin下降是EMT关键特征；HGF/c-Met可促进侵袭转移。Invasion/metastasis: E-cadherin loss is a key EMT feature; HGF/c-Met promotes invasion/metastasis.
血管生成
VEGF家族/VEGFR为主要轴，缺氧可诱导其表达。Angiogenesis: VEGF family/VEGFR is the main axis; hypoxia can induce expression.
代谢重编程
Warburg效应=偏好糖酵解（更快供能+提供生物合成底物）。Metabolic rewiring: Warburg effect = preference for glycolysis (fast ATP + biosynthetic intermediates).
基因组稳定
PARP参与单链断裂修复；DNA修复缺陷→突变累积。Genome stability: PARP participates in single-strand break repair; repair defects accelerate mutation accumulation.

模块2：Epigenetics

三大表观层面
DNA甲基化 + 组蛋白乙酰化 + 组蛋白甲基化（共同决定染色质开放/沉默）。Three epigenetic layers: DNA methylation + histone acetylation + histone methylation (control chromatin openness/silencing).
Writers
DNMTs（DNMT1/3a/3b等）加5mC；HATs；HMTs（如SETD2/DOT1L）。Writers: DNMTs (DNMT1/3a/3b) add 5mC; HATs; HMTs (e.g., SETD2/DOT1L).
Erasers
DNA去甲基化相关酶（如TET1）；HDACs；KDMs/LSDs。Erasers: DNA demethylation enzymes (e.g., TET1); HDACs; KDMs/LSDs.
Readers
MBD蛋白（含UHRF1）识别methyl-CpG；溴结构域蛋白如BRD4识别乙酰化。Readers: MBD proteins (incl. UHRF1) read methyl-CpG; bromodomain proteins like BRD4 read acetylation.
癌症甲基化特征
全局5mC降低 + 在启动子/增强子出现异常“局灶高甲基化”→抑癌基因沉默/致癌基因上调。Cancer methylation pattern: global loss of 5mC + focal hypermethylation at promoters/enhancers → suppress TSGs / enable oncogenes.
检测/研究
ChIP-chip/ChIP-seq用于定位组蛋白修饰与结合蛋白。Methods: ChIP-chip/ChIP-seq map histone marks and binding proteins.

表观药物（例）：DNMTi（azacitidine/decitabine）；HDACi；BETi（iBET762/OTX-015）；EZH2i（GSK-126/EPZ-6438）；DOT1Li（EPZ-5676）。Epigenetic drugs (examples): DNMTi (azacitidine/decitabine); HDACi; BETi (iBET762/OTX-015); EZH2i (GSK-126/EPZ-6438); DOT1Li (EPZ-5676).

模块3：Metabolic reprogramming & Angiogenesis

肿瘤核心压力
血流下降→氧/营养不足；需求上升→持续促血管生成信号。Core stress: reduced blood flow → low O2/nutrients; demand increases → sustained pro-angiogenic signaling.
VEGF轴
癌细胞高表达VEGF-A；内皮细胞高表达VEGFR-1/2；结合→二聚/自磷酸化→多通路效应。VEGF axis: tumor cells express VEGF-A; endothelial cells express VEGFR-1/2; binding → dimerization/autophosphorylation → pleiotropic effects.
Warburg效应关键后果
乳酸生成与外排（LDH-A上调；MCT1/4上调）→pH调控与微环境重塑。Warburg consequences: lactate production/export (↑LDH-A; ↑MCT1/4) → pH control and TME remodeling.
LDH
A/B亚基组成不同同工酶；肿瘤倾向LDH-A表达与“偏乳酸生成”的谱型。LDH: isoenzymes from A/B subunits; tumors favor LDH-A expression and lactate-producing profiles.
乳酸是信号分子
可通过GPCR（GPR81）↓cAMP，并影响免疫/炎症与血管生成。Lactate as a messenger: signals via GPCR (GPR81) to ↓cAMP and modulate immunity/inflammation/angiogenesis.
“Reverse Warburg”
癌细胞诱导CAF变‘分解代谢’→产生乳酸/丙酮酸供肿瘤利用（与MCT/LDH相关）。Reverse Warburg: cancer cells instruct CAFs to be catabolic → produce lactate/pyruvate to feed tumors (linked to MCT/LDH).
治疗思路
抗血管生成治疗 + 代谢适应控制（两条都可限制肿瘤生长）。Therapeutic concept: anti-angiogenic therapy + targeting metabolic adaptations can limit tumor growth.

模块4：Cytoskeleton

三大骨架
Actin（~8nm）/ Microtubule（~25nm）/ Intermediate filaments（~10nm）+ Septins（~5nm）。Cytoskeleton: Actin (~8nm) / Microtubules (~25nm) / Intermediate filaments (~10nm) + Septins (~5nm).
Actin动力学：成核→延长→稳态；存在临界浓度　；可treadmilling（+端聚合、-端去聚合）。Actin dynamics: nucleation → elongation → steady state; critical concentration (CC); treadmilling (+ end polymerization, - end depolymerization).
答案 C

Intermediate filaments：应对机械/多种应激；核内lamin与胞质IF都影响细胞形态与DNA完整性。Intermediate filaments: stress-response; nuclear lamins and cytoplasmic IFs affect cell shape and DNA integrity.

Septins
GTPase蛋白，形成异源寡聚体（六聚体/八聚体）并组装成丝；与膜曲率、PIPs、连接蛋白（anillin/BORG）相关。Septins: GTPase proteins form hetero-oligomers (hexamers/octamers) into filaments; linked to curvature, PIPs, and linkers (anillin/BORG).
Septin功能
胞质分裂（与actomyosin、ESCRT）、迁移、线粒体分裂、感染防御等。Septin functions: cytokinesis (actomyosin/ESCRT), migration, mitochondrial fission, infection defense, etc.
与癌症/耐药
BORG/Cdc42调控septin在actin/MT上的分布；BORG过表达促转移；septin-微管互作可影响紫杉烷（paclitaxel）敏感性/耐药。Cancer/resistance: BORG/Cdc42 controls septin localization on actin/MT; BORG overexpression is pro-metastatic; septin-MT interactions can affect taxane (paclitaxel) sensitivity/resistance.
Septin作为标志物
Septin 9甲基化DNA可用于结直肠癌血液检测（例子）。Septin as biomarker: Septin 9 methylated DNA used in blood testing for colorectal cancer (example).

模块5：Cell cycle, quiescence, senescence

细胞周期系统特点
顺序事件 + 开关式（不可逆）+ 鲁棒可调；可在G0静息可逆退出，或进入分化/衰老/死亡的永久退出。Cell-cycle system: ordered events + switch-like irreversibility + robust/adaptable; reversible exit to G0, or permanent exit to differentiation/senescence/death.
核心调控
Cyclin-CDK；CDK受CAK磷酸化激活，也受磷酸酶/激酶与CKI抑制。Core control: Cyclin-CDKs; CDKs activated by CAK phosphorylation and regulated by kinases/phosphatases and CKIs.
CKI家族
INK（抑制CDK4/6）；CIP/KIP（如p21/p27/p57）广泛抑制多种Cyclin-CDK。CKIs: INK family inhibits CDK4/6; CIP/KIP (e.g., p21/p27/p57) broadly inhibit cyclin-CDKs.

G1限制点（restriction point）：通过Rb-E2F开关决定是否承诺进入S期；受营养/生长因子/细胞大小/DNA完整性控制。G1 restriction point: Rb-E2F switch commits to S phase; controlled by nutrients/growth factors/cell size/DNA integrity.

泛素-蛋白酶体调控
APC/C（Cdc20/Cdh1）和SCF（如Skp2）通过降解关键蛋白推动周期。Ubiquitin-proteasome control: APC/C (Cdc20/Cdh1) and SCF (e.g., Skp2) degrade key regulators to drive the cycle.

纺锤体组装检查点（SAC）：MCC抑制Cdc20，直到所有动粒都正确连接；否则不进入后期。Spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC): MCC inhibits Cdc20 until all kinetochores attach; blocks anaphase otherwise.

后期与分裂
APC/C-Cdc20激活separase切割cohesin→姐妹染色单体分离；随后细胞质分裂由actomyosin环完成。Anaphase/cytokinesis: APC/C-Cdc20 activates separase to cleave cohesin → chromatid separation; actomyosin ring drives cytokinesis.
DNA损伤检查点
ATM/ATR→p53等；DDR与修复途径（HR/NHEJ/BER/NER/MMR，PARP1等）连接到周期停滞。DNA damage checkpoints: ATM/ATR → p53; DDR and repair pathways (HR/NHEJ/BER/NER/MMR, PARP1) couple to arrest.

模块6：Migration, polarity, EMT, metastasis

EMT/MET
发育与修复中正常；肿瘤中异常再激活→侵袭、入血（intravasion）、出血（extravasation）与转移定植。EMT/MET: normal in development/repair; aberrantly reactivated in cancer → invasion, intravasation, extravasation, colonization.
上皮连接类型
tight junction、adherens junction（E-cad）、desmosome、gap junction；细胞-基质连接：focal adhesion、hemidesmosome。Epithelial junctions: tight, adherens (E-cad), desmosome, gap; cell-matrix: focal adhesions and hemidesmosomes.

整合素（integrins）：focal adhesion中与talin/vinculin等连接actin；黏附周转（turnover）对迁移很关键。Integrins: in focal adhesions connect to actin via talin/vinculin; adhesion turnover is key for migration.

EMT经典开关
E-cad→N-cad；角蛋白→波形蛋白（vimentin）；半桥粒→黏着斑（hemidesmosome→focal adhesion）。EMT switches: E-cad → N-cad; keratin → vimentin; hemidesmosome → focal adhesion.
两种迁移模式
Mesenchymal（强黏附+ECM蛋白水解+lamellipodia，前端Rac1）；Amoeboid（低黏附+blebbing+后端RhoA/肌球蛋白收缩）。Two migration modes: mesenchymal (strong adhesion + ECM proteolysis + lamellipodia, front Rac1); amoeboid (low adhesion + blebbing + rear RhoA/myosin contraction).
方向决定因素
趋化梯度、基质硬度、黏附位点、电场，以及接触抑制/接触刺激迁移。Direction cues: chemoattractant gradients, stiffness, adhesion sites, electric fields, plus contact inhibition/stimulation of migration.
转移链条关键词
CTCs在血流中通常圆且硬；DTCs可进入休眠并耐药，醒来后导致复发。Metastasis keywords: CTCs in blood are often round/stiff; DTCs can be dormant and resistant, causing relapse upon reactivation.
治疗概念
Migrastatics（抗侵袭/抗转移药）是潜在方向，但时间窗与靶点选择困难。Therapy concept: migrastatics (anti-invasion/anti-metastasis drugs) are a potential direction, but timing/targets are challenging.

模块7：Cancer cell death

外源性凋亡
死亡受体（FAS/TNFR等）→DISC→caspase-8→caspase-3。Extrinsic apoptosis: death receptors (FAS/TNFR) → DISC → caspase-8 → caspase-3.
内源性凋亡
线粒体外膜通透化（Bax/Bak）→细胞色素c→Apaf1-凋亡小体（apoptosome）→caspase-9→caspase-3/7。Intrinsic apoptosis: MOMP (Bax/Bak) → cytochrome c → Apaf1 apoptosome → caspase-9 → caspase-3/7.
两条通路连接
caspase-8切割Bid→tBid，放大线粒体通路信号。Crosstalk: caspase-8 cleaves Bid → tBid to amplify mitochondrial signaling.
癌细胞抗凋亡机制
↑Bcl2/Bcl-XL，↑IAP/BIRC（如Survivin），↑cFLIP（抑制caspase-8），↓Bax/死亡受体，p53通路受损。Anti-apoptosis in cancer: ↑Bcl2/Bcl-XL, ↑IAP/BIRCs (e.g., Survivin), ↑cFLIP (blocks caspase-8), ↓Bax/death receptors, impaired p53.
促凋亡治疗例子
BH3 mimetics（如Venetoclax）；MDM2抑制剂稳定p53；IAP抑制剂/SMAC mimetics；死亡受体激动剂。Pro-apoptotic therapy examples: BH3 mimetics (e.g., Venetoclax); MDM2 inhibitors stabilize p53; IAP inhibitors/SMAC mimetics; death receptor agonists.

坏死性凋亡（Necroptosis）：TNFα/TNFR1→TRADD/RIPK1（去泛素化后分岔）→caspase-8被抑制时→RIPK1-RIPK3（necrosome）→MLKL成孔→炎症/DAMPs。Necroptosis: TNFα/TNFR1 → TRADD/RIPK1 (deubiquitination branch point) → when caspase-8 is inhibited → RIPK1-RIPK3 necrosome → MLKL pores → inflammation/DAMPs.

经典抑制剂
Necrostatin（RIPK1）；GSK-872（RIPK3）；NSA（MLKL）；zVAD（抑制caspases使细胞偏向坏死性凋亡）。Classic inhibitors: Necrostatin (RIPK1); GSK-872 (RIPK3); NSA (MLKL); zVAD inhibits caspases and can bias toward necroptosis.
生理/肿瘤意义
可作为凋亡失效时的“第二道清除”；但炎症也可能促肿瘤/促转移。Meaning: can act as a backup when apoptosis fails; but inflammation can also be pro-tumor/pro-metastatic.
MPT驱动坏死
ROS/Ca2+等→MPTP开放→ATP快速耗竭+氧化损伤。MPT-driven necrosis: ROS/Ca2+ triggers MPTP opening → rapid ATP depletion + oxidative damage.

模块8：Tumor heterogeneity, microenvironment & immunoediting

肿瘤异质性来源
克隆选择（线性/分支演化）+ CSCs + TME（CAFs、血管、免疫浸润）。Sources of heterogeneity: clonal selection (linear/branched evolution) + CSCs + TME (CAFs, vessels, immune infiltrates).
免疫编辑三阶段
Elimination → Equilibrium → Escape。Immunoediting phases: Elimination → Equilibrium → Escape.
Elimination
肿瘤新抗原经MHC-I呈递→CD8+ T细胞识别→穿孔素/颗粒酶B或FasL/TRAIL诱导死亡。Elimination: neoantigens via MHC-I → CD8+ T recognition → perforin/granzyme B or FasL/TRAIL-mediated killing.
TMB
肿瘤突变负荷高→更可能产生免疫原性新抗原→更易被T细胞识别。TMB: higher mutational burden → more immunogenic neoantigens → more likely T-cell recognition.
Equilibrium
免疫压力下选择“更不免疫原”的克隆（抗原丢失、MHC-I下调、PD-L1上调）。Equilibrium: immune pressure selects less immunogenic clones (antigen loss, MHC-I down, PD-L1 up).
PD-1/PD-L1轴
PD-L1（肿瘤）结合PD-1（T细胞）→抑制T细胞活化与抗肿瘤功能；IFN-γ可诱导肿瘤PD-L1表达。PD-1/PD-L1 axis: PD-L1 on tumor binds PD-1 on T cells → inhibits activation/effector function; IFN-γ can induce tumor PD-L1.

Escape（肿瘤内在）：抗原丢失、MHC-I下降、calreticulin下降（ICD下降）、↑STAT3/↑Bcl-2、↓FAS/TRAILR、↑CD39/CD73/IDO、↑TGF-β/IL-10。Escape (tumor-intrinsic): antigen loss, ↓MHC-I, ↓calreticulin (↓ICD), ↑STAT3/↑Bcl-2, ↓FAS/TRAILR, ↑CD39/CD73/IDO, ↑TGF-β/IL-10.

Escape（免疫细胞介导）：Tregs、MDSCs、TAM（偏M2）募集与抑制效应T细胞。Escape (immune-mediated): recruitment of Tregs, MDSCs, and TAMs (M2-like) to suppress effector T cells.

Tregs五种抑制
消耗IL-2；颗粒酶/穿孔素杀伤；CD39/CD73把ATP→腺苷；分泌TGF-β/IL-10/IL-35；表达CTLA-4抑制APC激活。Five Treg suppressive mechanisms: consume IL-2; granzyme/perforin killing; CD39/CD73 convert ATP→adenosine; secrete TGF-β/IL-10/IL-35; CTLA-4 inhibits APC activation.
MDSCs
PMN-MDSC（中性粒样）与M-MDSC（单核样）；通过耗竭氨基酸（L-Arg/Trp等）、氧化应激、诱导Tregs等抑制免疫。MDSCs: PMN-MDSC (neutrophil-like) and M-MDSC (monocyte-like); suppress via amino-acid depletion (L-Arg/Trp), oxidative stress, Treg induction, etc.
炎症双刃剑
ROS/TNF-α/IL-6/IL-1β等可提升突变率并通过NFκB/STAT3/AP-1促进存活、增殖、血管生成、侵袭与干性。Inflammation is double-edged: ROS/TNF-α/IL-6/IL-1β can increase mutation and via NFκB/STAT3/AP-1 promote survival, proliferation, angiogenesis, invasion, stemness.

模块9：Therapy, targets, biomarkers, resistance

靶向治疗例子
BCR-ABL（费城染色体）驱动CML；Imatinib设计用于抑制其ATP结合位点。Targeted therapy example: BCR-ABL (Philadelphia chromosome) drives CML; imatinib designed to inhibit its ATP-binding site.
四类生物标志物
易感性（predisposition）/ 诊断（diagnostic）/ 预测（predictive）/ 预后（prognostic）。Four biomarker types: predisposition / diagnostic / predictive / prognostic.
血清肿瘤标志物
通常不用于诊断（健康人与疾病人群重叠大）；更适合随访与复发监测（需要动态曲线）。Serum tumor markers: usually not for diagnosis (high overlap); better for follow-up/recurrence monitoring (needs longitudinal curve).
Liquid biopsy
CTCs、ctDNA、游离miRNA/ncRNA、外泌体/微囊泡、肿瘤教育血小板等。Liquid biopsy: CTCs, ctDNA, cell-free miRNA/ncRNA, extracellular vesicles (exosomes/microvesicles), tumor-educated platelets, etc.
细胞/组织标志物评估
IHC、RT-qPCR、免疫分析；DNA含量/倍体可用流式细胞术（直方图/散点图）分析。Tissue/cellular markers: IHC, RT-qPCR, immunoassays; DNA content/ploidy assessed by flow cytometry (histograms/dot plots).
耐药总框架

C. 4 个必考“设计实验”模板（直接背）

1）补救实验：共转染 siX 与 Y 过表达（或 miRNA 抑制剂），观察表型是否被救回。

Rescue experiment
co-transfect siX with Y overexpression (or a miRNA inhibitor) to test whether the phenotype is rescued.

2）机制验证：用含 WT 与突变结合位点的 luciferase reporter 证明直接调控。

Mechanism validation: use a luciferase reporter with WT and mutant binding sites to validate direct regulation.

3）通路依赖性：用通路抑制剂/激动剂检验 X 的效应是否依赖通路 Y。

Pathway dependency
treat with a pathway inhibitor/activator to test whether X-dependent effects require pathway Y.

4）体内验证：在移植瘤/转移模型中验证关键结论，并检测 Ki67、凋亡标志物和通路 IHC。

In vivo confirmation: validate the key finding in xenograft/metastasis models and assess Ki67, apoptosis markers, and IHC for the pathway.
