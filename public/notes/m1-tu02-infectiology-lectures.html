历史与病毒学发展History and Progress in Virology

1
19世纪末，巴斯德 End of 19th century, Pasteur

那时“病毒”泛指所有感染性病原体。 At that time, "virus" referred to all types of infectious agents.

“virus”一词拉丁语意为毒药或毒素。 The word "virus" comes from Latin, meaning poison or venom.

如炭疽病毒、疟疾病毒。 For example, anthrax virus, malaria virus. （补充：录音提到巴斯德通过证实病毒存在，推动了病毒学的起步，且在未鉴定病毒时就研发了狂犬病疫苗2。）

2
1892年，伊万诺夫斯基 1892, Ivanowski

发现烟草花叶病原体具有滤过性，可以通过细菌滤器。 Discovered the tobacco mosaic agent was filterable and could pass through bacterial filters.

这是病毒与细菌的重要区别。 This is an important distinction between viruses and bacteria. （补充：老师指出“滤过性”意味着病毒颗粒足够小，可通过滤器，且可通过滤过物传染其他生物体。）

3
1898年，贝耶林克 1898, Beijerinck

发现病毒不仅能滤过，而且具有连续传染性，首次明确提出病毒概念。 Discovered that viruses are not only filterable but also serially transmissible, and first introduced the concept of virus.

提出“活的传染性液体”（contagium vivum fluidum）或“感染性活流体”观点。 Proposed the idea of "contagium vivum fluidum" or "infectious living fluid". （补充：贝耶林克强调，病毒不像细菌那样在培养基中生长，只能在生物体内繁殖。）

4
光学显微镜下不可见的因子 Agents invisible under the optical microscope

“超病毒”或“可滤过病毒”概念由此产生。 Hence the terms "ultravirus" or "filterable virus" were created.

病毒体积过小，普通光学显微镜下不可见。 Viruses are too small to be seen under a regular optical microscope. （补充：老师再次强调常规光镜看不到病毒，实验操作时要注意这一点。）

5
20世纪初人兽疾病其他病毒发现 Other agents of human and animal diseases

1900年发现黄热病毒，1903年狂犬病毒，1909年脊髓灰质炎病毒，1911年鲁氏肉瘤病毒。 The yellow fever virus (1900), rabies virus (1903), poliovirus (1909), and Rous sarcoma virus (1911) were discovered. （录音补充：这些发现推动了病毒与癌症关系及病毒学研究发展，并帮助明确了病毒可致肿瘤2。）

6
1915-1917年，Twort与D’Herelle 1915-1917, Twort and D’Hérelle

发现噬菌体，即感染细菌的病毒。 Discovered bacteriophages, viruses that infect bacteria.

典型噬菌体如T4型，头部和尾部结构分明。 A typical bacteriophage is the T4-like virus, with distinct head and tail structures. （录音补充：噬菌体结构独特，能注入基因进入细菌，被形容为“像蜘蛛一样的形态”。）

7
1939年，冯·考舍 1939, Von Kausche

首次利用电子显微镜观察到烟草花叶病毒。 First observation of a virus (tobacco mosaic virus) under an electron microscope.

电子显微镜突破了病毒不可见的局限。 Electron microscopy overcame the limitation of virus invisibility by light microscopy.

发现人类免疫缺陷病毒（HIV）。 Discovery of the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV).

病毒的定义与基本特征Definitions and Essential Criteria of Viruses

一、1953年André Lwoff对病毒的定义, Definition of viruses by André Lwoff

病毒是具有感染性且可能致病的颗粒。 Viruses are infectious and potentially pathogenic particles.

病毒是由单一类型核酸（RNA或DNA）和蛋白质组成的核蛋白实体。 They are nucleoproteic entities with a single type of nucleic acid (RNA or DNA).

病毒通过宿主细胞利用其遗传物质进行复制。 They are reproduced by the cell from their genetic material.

病毒不能像细胞或细菌那样生长和分裂。 They are unable to grow and divide like cells or bacteria.

病毒没有Lipman系统（即缺乏自身代谢/能量系统）。 They have no Lipman system (i.e., no intrinsic metabolism or energy system)。

二、病毒颗粒Virion

病毒颗粒指的是成熟的、具有感染性并且处于细胞外的病毒粒子。 A virion is a viral particle which is mature, infectious, and extracellular1。

三、病毒的四个基本判据Four Essential Criteria of Viruses

1
单一类型核酸（DNA或RNA） One single type of nucleic acid (DNA or RNA)

病毒基因组只能是DNA或RNA中的一种。 The viral genome is either DNA or RNA, never both。

2
遗传物质驱动的复制 Replication from the genetic material

病毒必须依靠自身基因组完成复制过程。 Viruses replicate by using their own genetic material。

3
绝对细胞寄生性 Mandatory cellular parasitism

病毒缺乏独立的酶系统和能量系统，无法自行代谢。 Viruses lack their own enzymatic and energetic system, so they cannot metabolize independently。

只能在活细胞中培养分离。 They can only be isolated using live cell culture。

这也是抗病毒治疗难度高的原因之一。 This is why antiviral therapy is difficult。

4
明确的结构特征 Defined structure

病毒结构与细菌不同，有其独特的形态。 The structure of viruses is different from that of bacteria; viruses have unique morphology。

常见类型为二十面体或螺旋体衣壳。 Typical capsid types are icosahedral or helical。

补充（录音要点）Additional Teacher’s Notes (from Recording)

病毒需要宿主细胞才能进行生命周期的各个步骤，表现为绝对寄生性。 Viruses require a host cell for all steps of their life cycle, demonstrating absolute parasitism。

病毒无法独立生长、繁殖，亦无自有能量体系，且只含一种核酸。 Viruses cannot independently grow or reproduce, possess no intrinsic energy system, and have only one type of nucleic acid。

“成熟的病毒颗粒＝virion”，只能在细胞外存活并具有感染性。 The mature and infectious form, which is extracellular, is called a "virion"。

小结Summary

病毒是具有感染性、绝对细胞依赖性、结构独特、且只含一种核酸的非细胞生物。 Viruses are infectious, absolutely cell-dependent, structurally unique, and possess a genome of only one nucleic acid type, thus classed as acellular organisms。

病毒的结构Structure of a virus

一、基本结构组成 General structural elements

病毒的结构由核心核酸、衣壳（蛋白外壳），部分病毒还有包膜和核心组成。 The structure of a virus consists of nucleic acid at its core and a capsid (protein shell). Some viruses also possess an envelope and a core.

所有病毒都有核酸（DNA或RNA）和衣壳，这两部分合称“核衣壳”。 All viruses contain nucleic acid (DNA or RNA) and a capsid. Together, these are called the nucleocapsid.

有些病毒还会包含包膜，此外某些病毒带有“核心”，即蛋白与核酸的复合物（如逆转录病毒）。 Some viruses have an envelope. Additionally, some viruses have a “core”, which is a complex of proteins and nucleic acid (e.g., retroviruses).

二、病毒结构示意与重点解析 Schematic and key points

1
病毒的核心是遗传物质，可能是DNA或RNA，只能有其一。 The core of the virus is its genetic material, which can be either DNA or RNA, never both.
2
核酸外包裹蛋白衣壳，衣壳由蛋白质亚单位装配而成，负责保护核酸并参与感染。 Surrounding the nucleic acid is the capsid, formed by protein subunits, which protects the genome and is involved in infection.
3
有些病毒的核酸和蛋白质还会形成一个核心结构，特别是在逆转录病毒中。 In some viruses, the nucleic acid and proteins further assemble into a core structure, especially in retroviruses.
4
部分病毒颗粒外还有包膜，包膜由宿主细胞膜来源，其中嵌有病毒自身糖蛋白。 Some viral particles have an outer envelope, derived from the host membrane and containing viral glycoproteins.

四、病毒的常见形态 Typical shapes of viruses

1
球形（20面体）：如腺病毒、诺如病毒、甲型肝炎病毒。 Spherical (icosahedral): e.g., adenovirus, norovirus, hepatitis A virus.
2
棒状（螺旋体）：代表是烟草花叶病毒。 Stick-shaped (helical): the tobacco mosaic virus is the classic example.
复杂结构
如单纯疱疹病毒（有二十面体衣壳及包膜）、埃博拉病毒（长丝状）、狂犬病毒（弹头状）。 Complex shape: e.g., herpes simplex virus (with icosahedral capsid and envelope), Ebola virus (filamentous), rabies virus (bullet-shaped).
更复杂的病毒形态
如痘病毒（橄榄形、较大）、噬菌体（头部为二十面体，带有尾管和尾丝，形如“蜘蛛”结构）。 Even more complex shapes: e.g., poxvirus (oval and large), bacteriophages (with an icosahedral head, tail tube, and long tail fibers; resembling a “spider” structure).

五、老师补充解读 Teacher’s Additional Explanation

课上强调，不同病毒结构影响其感染机制及应用，如腺病毒和HSV都可作为生物技术的病毒载体。 It was emphasized in class that different viral structures influence their infection mechanisms and applications; for instance, both adenovirus and HSV can be used as viral vectors in biotechnology.

棒状病毒（如烟草花叶病毒）为螺旋对称，显示出与球形病毒完全不同的外观。 Rod-shaped viruses (like tobacco mosaic virus) show helical symmetry and have an appearance totally distinct from spherical viruses.

病毒形态非常多样，尤其复杂病毒如噬菌体、痘病毒体积巨大、结构各异。 Viral morphology is highly diverse. Complex viruses like bacteriophages and poxviruses are large and structurally unique.

不同病毒类型的形态可在电子显微镜下观察，是病毒判别及研究的重要基础。 Different morphologies of viruses can be observed under the electron microscope, providing an important basis for virus identification and research.

六、小结 Summary

病毒结构包括核心核酸、衣壳，部分含有包膜或核心。球形、棒状、复杂结构等多种形态决定了它们的功能和应用。 The structure of a virus includes a core nucleic acid and a capsid, with some having an envelope or core. Spherical, rod-like, and complex forms determine their functions and applications.

病毒大小与基因组结构Size and Genomic Structure of Viruses

一、病毒的大小范围与典型实例Size Range of Viruses and Typical Examples

1
病毒大小差异极大，从最大的巨病毒（Mimivirus，约650纳米）到最小的细小病毒（Parvovirus，约20纳米）。 The size of viruses varies greatly, from the largest mimivirus (about 650 nm) to the smallest parvovirus (about 20 nm).
2
巨病毒最初因其体积可与细菌媲美，曾一度被误认为是细菌。 The mimivirus was initially mistaken for a bacterium due to its large size comparable to bacteria.
3
巨病毒感染变形虫，这代表其宿主范围可以涵盖原生生物。 Mimivirus infects amoebae, showing that their host range can include protozoa.
4
“Mimicking Microbe”意指其具有类似细菌的外观，因此得名Mimivirus。 The term “Mimicking Microbe” highlights its resemblance to bacteria, thus named mimivirus.
5
细小病毒则仅有20纳米左右，是目前已知最小的病毒之一。 Parvoviruses are about 20 nm, making them one of the smallest known viruses.
6
课上强调，病毒肉眼不可见，电子显微镜下才可分辨病毒与细菌的形态与大小差异。 In class it was stressed that viruses cannot be seen under a standard light microscope and can only be distinguished from bacteria by electron microscopy.

二、常见病毒的具体尺寸与类别Examples and Sizes of Common Viruses

1
小型病毒包括细小病毒（18到26纳米）和脊髓灰质炎病毒（27纳米）。 Small viruses include parvovirus (18 to 26 nm) and poliovirus (27 nm).
2
大型病毒如流感病毒（100纳米）、单纯疱疹病毒（150到200纳米）、痘苗病毒（250到300纳米）。 Big viruses include flu virus (100 nm), herpes simplex virus (150 to 200 nm), and vaccinia virus (250 to 300 nm).
3
课上指出，病毒尺寸的了解有助于其研究与分类。 It was noted in class that knowing the size of viruses aids in their research and classification.

三、病毒基因组的主要研究参数Main Parameters for Studying Viral Genomes

本质
每种病毒只含有DNA或RNA之一，二者不会共存。 Nature: Each viral family contains either DNA or RNA, never both.
尺寸
不同病毒其基因组长度相差巨大。 Size: The genome length varies greatly across viruses.
结构
可为单链（ss）、双链（ds）或亚基因组片段。 Structure: The genome may be single-stranded, double-stranded, or contain subgenomic fragments.
拓扑
可为线性或环状。 Topology: The genome can be linear or circular.
碱基组成
关注G/C含量，影响基因组稳定性。 Base composition: G/C content is important for genome stability.
6
可能出现重复序列，包括 3'多聚腺苷段、异常碱基/糖、回文末端等，有助于环化。 Presence of repetitive sequences may include 3' polyadenylated segments, abnormal bases or sugars, and palindromic ends for circularization.
7
基因组类型是病毒分类的重要标准。 The nature of the genome is a key criterion for virus classification.

四、DNA病毒基因组的具体特征Features of the DNA Viral Genomes

1
基因组大小变化范围很大，如乙型肝炎病毒为3200碱基，细小病毒为5700碱基，人巨细胞病毒达235,000碱基，巨病毒则有1,200,000碱基。 The DNA genome size varies a lot: hepatitis B virus has about 3,200 bases, parvovirus 5,700, human cytomegalovirus 235,000, and mimivirus up to 1,200,000 bases.
2
G/C含量可变，范围为32%到75%。 The G/C content ranges from 32% to 75%.
3
某些DNA与碱性蛋白结合，如部分细胞组蛋白（多瘤病毒科、乳头瘤病毒科）或病毒自身蛋白（正疱疹病毒科）。 Some DNA is associated with basic proteins, such as cellular histones (Polyomaviridae, Papillomaviridae) or viral proteins (Orthoherepsviridae).
4
课上强调，DNA与组蛋白结合可影响其在宿主细胞中的生存和表达，并与病毒的潜伏特性、基因治疗应用相关。 In class it was stressed that DNA association with histones affects persistence and expression in host cells, and relates to latency and gene therapy.

五、DNA病毒的结构特点Structural Features of DNA Viruses

1
大多数DNA病毒基因组为双链线性DNA，呈经典的双螺旋三维结构。 Most DNA viruses have double-stranded, linear genomes with the classic three-dimensional double helix structure.
2
部分DNA病毒为环状或部分双链结构，具体依病毒家族而定。 Some DNA viruses are circular or partially double-stranded, depending on the virus family.
3
课上补充，不同DNA结构有助于病毒生命周期的不同阶段、基因表达以及宿主免疫逃逸。 It was added in class that different DNA structures aid different stages of the viral life cycle, gene expression, and immune evasion.

六、小结Summary

1
病毒在大小和基因组结构上多样，既有微小也有巨大类型。 Viruses are diverse in size and genome structure, with both very small and very large types.
2
病毒基因组的种类、尺寸、结构和与蛋白的结合方式，是病毒分类和功能的重要依据。 The genome type, size, structure, and association with proteins are essential for virus classification and function.
3
掌握病毒的这些基本特征，对于理解其生物学行为、致病机制及后续科研应用至关重要。 Understanding these features is crucial for studying viral biology, pathogenesis, and subsequent research applications.

RNA基因组的特点 Features of RNA Genomes

一、DNA病毒基因组的类型及代表家族 Types of DNA Virus Genomes and Representative Families

DNA病毒的基因组类型主要包括三种
单链、双链和部分双链。 The main types of DNA virus genomes include single-stranded, double-stranded, and partially double-stranded.

单链DNA病毒（如Parvoviridae科）仅有一股DNA链。 Single-stranded DNA viruses (such as Parvoviridae) contain only one DNA strand.

大多数DNA病毒为双链，可分为线性和环状结构。 Most DNA viruses are double-stranded and can be linear or circular in structure.

双链线性DNA代表有Adenoviridae科和Orthoherpesviridae科。 Linear double-stranded DNA examples include Adenoviridae and Orthoherpesviridae.

双链环状DNA常见于Papillomaviridae科和Polyomaviridae科。 Circular double-stranded DNA is found in Papillomaviridae and Polyomaviridae.

部分双链DNA病毒以Hepadnaviridae为代表，其基因组仅部分区域为双链。 Partially double-stranded DNA viruses are represented by the Hepadnaviridae family, where only parts of the genome are double-stranded.

二、DNA病毒家族的结构特点及示意 Structural Features and Schematics of DNA Virus Families

Parvoviridae科的病毒基因组结构为单股DNA，外层为规则五边形对称的衣壳。 Parvoviridae have a single-stranded DNA genome surrounded by a symmetric pentagonal capsid.

Adenoviridae科具有双股线性DNA，衣壳复杂，包括纤突、顶点、六联体蛋白和核心蛋白。 Adenoviridae contain linear double-stranded DNA with a complex capsid including fibers, penton bases, hexon proteins, and core proteins.

Papillomaviridae科的基因组为双链环状DNA，DNA与组蛋白结合，共同被衣壳蛋白包裹。 Papillomaviridae feature circular double-stranded DNA associated with histones, all surrounded by a capsid protein shell.

Hepadnaviridae科包括部分双链DNA，并包裹于富含多种糖蛋白的包膜下，同时存在聚合酶等酶。 Hepadnaviridae have a partially double-stranded DNA genome within an envelope rich in glycoproteins, also containing polymerase.

课上强调，不同基因组结构影响病毒生命周期和感染模式，如组蛋白结合帮助病毒基因组稳定且便于潜伏或持久存在。 In class, it was stressed that different genome structures influence the virus life cycle and infection patterns, for example, histone association helps stabilize the genome and facilitates latency or persistence.

三、RNA病毒基因组的类型与代表 Features and Types of RNA Virus Genomes

大部分RNA病毒的基因组是单股且线性。 Most RNA viruses have a single-stranded, linear genome.

少部分RNA病毒为双链结构，典型代表为Sedoreoviridae科。 A small portion of RNA viruses are double-stranded, with Sedoreoviridae as a typical example.

部分RNA病毒基因组以片段存在，如Sedoreoviridae有10或11个片段，流感病毒有7到8个片段。 Some RNA viruses have segmented genomes: Sedoreoviridae have 10 to 11 fragments, and flu viruses have 7 to 8 fragments.

片段化的基因组有利于基因的快速多样化和易于重组。 Segmented genomes contribute to rapid genetic diversity and easier recombination.

四、总结 Summary

DNA病毒基因组的主要类型包括单链、双链（分为线性和环状）、以及部分双链，代表性家族分别有Parvoviridae、Adenoviridae、Papillomaviridae和Hepadnaviridae。 The main types of DNA virus genomes are single-stranded, double-stranded (linear or circular), and partially double-stranded. Representative families are Parvoviridae, Adenoviridae, Papillomaviridae, and Hepadnaviridae.

RNA病毒基因组以单股线性为主，少数为双股或多片段。 RNA virus genomes are mainly single-stranded and linear, with some being double-stranded or segmented.

病毒基因组结构的多样性是其生物学行为和致病机制多样化的重要基础。 The diversity of viral genome structures forms the basis for the wide range of biological behaviors and pathogenic mechanisms of viruses.

RNA基因组的结构与极性 Schematic Representation and Polarity of RNA Genomes

一、RNA基因组的基本特点 Basic Features of RNA Genomes

大多数RNA病毒的基因组为单链且线性。 Most RNA virus genomes are single-stranded and linear.

少数RNA病毒为双链结构，代表为Sedoreoviridae科。 A few RNA viruses have double-stranded genomes, with Sedoreoviridae as an example.

部分RNA病毒的基因组呈亚基因组片段化，如Sedoreoviridae有10-11个片段，流感病毒有7-8个片段。 Some RNA viruses have subgenomic fragments; Sedoreoviridae have 10–11 fragments, and flu viruses have 7–8 fragments.

片段化的基因组有利于遗传多样性和基因重组。 Segmented genomes facilitate genetic diversity and recombination.

二、RNA基因组的分类与代表性家族 Types and Representative Families of RNA Genomes

单链线性RNA基因组典型家族
Picornaviridae、Togaviridae、Paramyxoviridae。 Typical families with single-stranded linear RNA genomes include Picornaviridae, Togaviridae, and Paramyxoviridae.

单链分段RNA基因组代表有Orthomyxoviridae和Bunyaviridae。 Segmented single-stranded RNA genomes are found in Orthomyxoviridae and Bunyaviridae.

单链环状RNA为Hepatitis D病毒的特征。 Circular single-stranded RNA is characteristic of hepatitis D virus.

双链分段RNA是Sedoreoviridae（如轮状病毒）所特有的。 Segmented double-stranded RNA is unique to Sedoreoviridae, e.g., rotavirus.

三、多片段结构多样化实例 Polygenomic Organization Examples

Orthomyxoviridae（如流感病毒）基因组由7到8个片段组成。 Orthomyxoviridae (such as flu virus) genomes consist of 7–8 fragments.

Sedoreoviridae（如轮状病毒）一般有10个片段，轮状病毒为11个片段。 Sedoreoviridae (such as rotavirus) typically have 10 fragments, and rotavirus has 11 fragments.

这些片段各自编码不同蛋白质，实现多基因表达。 Each fragment encodes a different protein, allowing for polygenic expression.

四、特殊RNA病毒家族结构说明 Unique RNA Families Organization

Retroviridae家族包含2条线性单链RNA，这两条RNA在5'端通过共价键连接。 Retroviridae family viruses contain two linear single-stranded RNAs, covalently linked at their 5' ends.

Hepatitis D病毒的基因组呈环状单链，且为负链伪双链结构。 The hepatitis D virus has a circular, single-stranded RNA genome with negative polarity, forming a pseudo-double-stranded structure.

五、单链RNA的极性 Polarity of Single-stranded RNAs

正链RNA（正极性）：可直接被翻译为病毒蛋白，具有直接感染性。 Positive-sense (positive polarity) RNA: can be directly translated into viral proteins and is directly infectious.

正链RNA的代表家族有Coronaviridae、Picornaviridae、Togaviridae。 Representative families for positive-sense RNA include Coronaviridae, Picornaviridae, and Togaviridae.

一些正链RNA病毒基因组带有帽子结构或者polyA尾，有助于mRNA功能和保护。 Some positive-sense RNA genomes have a cap and/or polyadenylated sequence, aiding mRNA function and protection.

负链RNA（负极性）：需先转录为mRNA后才能翻译。 Negative-sense (negative polarity) RNA: must be transcribed into mRNA before translation.

负链RNA的代表家族为Orthomyxoviridae和Rhabdoviridae。 Representative families for negative-sense RNA include Orthomyxoviridae and Rhabdoviridae.

负链RNA病毒需自带转录酶（RNA依赖的RNA聚合酶），因宿主细胞不具备此功能。 Negative-sense RNA viruses must carry their own RNA-dependent RNA polymerase, since the host cell lacks this enzyme.

六、总结 Summary

RNA病毒基因组存在单链线性、分段、环状和双链分段等多种形式。 RNA virus genomes exist in various forms: single-stranded linear, segmented, circular, and double-stranded segmented.

单链RNA的极性决定其能否直接作为mRNA翻译，以及感染机制。 The polarity of single-stranded RNA determines if it functions as mRNA directly and influences infection mechanisms.

多片段结构和不同极性增加了病毒的生物学多样性与进化能力。 Segmented genome structure and different polarity increase viral biological diversity and evolutionary capacity.

病毒衣壳与螺旋对称性管状衣壳 The Capsid and Tubular Capsid with Helical Symmetry

一、衣壳的基本特性 Key Characteristics of the Capsid

病毒衣壳是由蛋白质构成的高抗性、极其稳定的结构。 The viral capsid is a highly resistant and extremely stable protein structure.

衣壳可由单一类型蛋白或少量不同蛋白聚合而成。 Capsids can polymerize from a single type or a small number of different proteins.

衣壳有两大类
一类为具有螺旋对称性的管状衣壳，另一类为具有立方对称性的二十面体衣壳。 There are two main categories of capsid: tubular capsid with helical symmetry and icosahedral capsid with cubic symmetry.

衣壳的自组装遵循自组装原理，即蛋白质间能自动识别并聚合形成稳定结构。 Capsid assembly follows the principle of self-assembly; proteins can spontaneously recognize and aggregate into a stable structure.

衣壳的三个主要作用：

1
保护基因组免受外界环境损伤。
2
协助无包膜病毒吸附到宿主细胞膜上。
3
将病毒基因组运输至细胞内部。 The capsid has three main roles:
4
Protecting the genome from the external environment;
5
Facilitating attachment of naked viruses to the host cell membrane;
6
Transporting the viral genome inside the cell.

衣壳的对称性是病毒分类的重要标准。 The symmetry of the capsid is a criteria for virus classification.

二、螺旋对称性管状衣壳的模型与实例 Study Model and Examples of Tubular Capsid with Helical Symmetry

经典研究模型为烟草花叶病毒。 The classic study model is tobacco mosaic virus.

烟草花叶病毒感染后的植物叶片可见花叶斑驳。 Leaves of tobacco plants show mosaic mottling after infection by tobacco mosaic virus.

病毒颗粒呈刚性空心棒状，长约300nm，直径约15nm。 The virus particles are rigid hollow rods, about 300nm in length and 15nm in diameter.

三、烟草花叶病毒结构与特性 Structure and Features of Tobacco Mosaic Virus

烟草花叶病毒是第一个被结晶并通过X射线衍射分析的病毒（20世纪30年代）。 Tobacco mosaic virus was the first virus crystallized and analyzed by X-ray diffraction (1930s).

其为无包膜病毒，基因组为正极性单股RNA，全长约6.4kb。 It is a non-enveloped virus with a positive-sense, single-stranded RNA genome, about 6.4kb long.

RNA以螺旋方式被包装在管状衣壳中。 The RNA is helically arranged inside a tubular capsid.

整体由2130个完全相同的衣壳蛋白亚单位组成。 The whole structure comprises 2,130 identical capsid protein subunits.

四、烟草花叶病毒衣壳的装配与结构细节 Assembly and Details of Tobacco Mosaic Virus Capsid

衣壳由158个氨基酸组成的单一蛋白质构成，蛋白为马蹄形（sabot）结构。 The capsid is composed of a single protein of 158 amino acids, in a sabot shape.

基因组核酸长度决定病毒棒状颗粒的长度。 The length of the nucleic acid determines the length of the rod.

螺旋对称性的管状衣壳装配可以以穿鞋带作类比——衣壳蛋白如鞋带，核酸贯穿其中。 The assembly of the helical tubular capsid can be likened to threading a shoelace—the capsid proteins form the tube and nucleic acid is inserted like a lace.

五、动物病毒中螺旋对称性管状衣壳 Animal Viruses with Helical Symmetry Tubular Capsid

动物病毒中具螺旋对称性的病毒总带有包膜。 Animal viruses with helical symmetry are always enveloped.

病毒的核衣壳是柔性的，并在包膜内呈折叠状态。 Their nucleocapsids are flexible and folded inside the envelope.

代表病毒包括狂犬病毒和流感病毒。 Representative viruses include rabies virus and flu virus.

六、狂犬病毒和流感病毒实例 Examples: Rabies Virus and Flu Virus

狂犬病毒核衣壳为螺旋结构，直径15nm，长度120-180nm，宽约50nm。基因组为负极性、线性、单股RNA。 Rabies virus nucleocapsid has a helical structure, 15nm diameter, 120-180nm length, about 50nm width; genome is negative-sense, linear, single-stranded RNA.

流感病毒核衣壳同为螺旋结构，直径8nm，完整病毒颗粒约100nm，基因组为负极性、分节（7或8段）、单股线性RNA。 Flu virus nucleocapsid also has helical structure, 8nm diameter, whole particle about 100nm, genome is negative-sense, segmented (7 or 8 fragments), single-stranded and linear RNA.

补充讲解老师强调要点 Additional Key Teaching Points

老师指出螺旋对称性衣壳的装配可用“穿鞋带”模型直观理解，帮助学生在家用鞋和管辅助理解病毒结构。 The teacher highlighted using the shoe and tube model to help visualize the assembly of helical capsids, allowing students to use household items (shoe and tubing) to better understand virus structure.

动物病毒的螺旋对称性核衣壳一定带包膜，区别于植物病毒。 Animal helical nucleocapsids are always enveloped, unlike plant viruses.

这些结构特征不仅有助于分类，也是病毒感染机制和稳定性的基础。 Such structural features are critical for classification and are also fundamental for understanding infection mechanisms and viral stability.

二十面体衣壳的立方对称性 | Icosahedral Capsid with Cubic Symmetry

一、二十面体基本结构 Icosahedron Basic Structure

二十面体是正二十面体，有20个面、12个顶点、30条边。 An icosahedron is a regular polyhedron with 20 faces, 12 summits (vertices), and 30 edges.

每个面都是等边三角形，构成了高度对称和稳定的结构。 Each face is an equilateral triangle, resulting in a highly symmetrical and stable structure.

二、二十面体衣壳的组成 Components of Icosahedral Capsid

二十面体的面为等边三角形。 The faces of the icosahedron are equilateral triangles.

衣壳由结构亚单位（壳粒，capsomere）组成，包括五聚体（penton）与六聚体（hexon）。 Capsid consists of structural subunits (capsomeres), including pentamers (pentons) and hexamers (hexons).

所有具立方对称性的病毒二十面体衣壳均有12个五聚体，分别位于12个顶点； All viruses with cubic symmetry icosahedral capsids have 12 pentons, each at a summit of the icosahedron.

六聚体数量在不同病毒中变化，分布于面与边上，以调整衣壳大小。 The number of hexons is variable among viruses and is located on edges and faces to adjust capsid size.

三、典型组装方式 Examples of Assembly

最简形式T=1二十面体，仅有12个五聚体，含60个蛋白亚单位。 The simplest T=1 icosahedron has 12 pentons and 60 protein subunits.

复杂型如T=3，含12个五聚体和20个六聚体，共180个蛋白亚单位。 A more complex T=3 form contains 12 pentons and 20 hexons, totaling 180 protein subunits.

随着T数增加，二十面体可包含12个五聚体和更多六聚体，例如T=7有60个六聚体，T=25有240个六聚体。 With higher T numbers, the icosahedron can include 12 pentons and increasing hexons: T=7 has 60 hexons, T=25 has 240 hexons.

相应地，这些大型二十面体分别包含420（T=7）和1500（T=25）个蛋白亚单位。 Accordingly, these large icosahedrons have 420 (T=7) and 1,500 (T=25) protein subunits respectively.

四、病毒实例及二十面体衣壳结构 Virus Examples and Icosahedral Capsid Structure

HPV（乳头瘤病毒，Papillomaviridae）：

二十面体衣壳，由72个壳粒组成，直径52-55nm，基因组为环状双链DNA。

HPV (Papillomaviridae): Icosahedral capsid with 72 capsomeres, viral particle diameter 52–55nm, genome is circular double-stranded DNA.

脊髓灰质炎病毒（小RNA病毒科，Picornaviridae）：

二十面体衣壳，162个壳粒，病毒直径约30nm，基因组为单链正义极性线状RNA。

Poliovirus (Picornaviridae): Icosahedral capsid with 162 capsomeres, particle diameter about 30nm, genome is positive-sense, single-stranded, linear RNA.

腺病毒（腺病毒科，Adenoviridae）：

二十面体衣壳，252个壳粒，直径90-120nm，基因组为双链线状DNA。

Adenovirus (Adenoviridae): Icosahedral capsid with 252 capsomeres, diameter 90–120nm, genome is double-stranded, linear DNA.

五、通用结构原理 Universal Principle of Construction

二十面体衣壳的对称性和几何结构可见于许多自然和工程结构中，如蒙特利尔的地球穹顶、足球和C₆₀巴克球分子。 The symmetry and geometry of the icosahedral capsid are also seen in many natural and man-made structures, such as the geodesic dome in Montreal, football, and buckminsterfullerene (C₆₀) molecules.

教师举例说明，这类高度对称结构具备良好的稳定性和自组装能力，是许多病毒选择二十面体对称性的原因。 The teacher gave examples to illustrate that this highly symmetrical structure provides stability and self-assembly ability, which is why many viruses adopt icosahedral symmetry.

六、总结 Summary

二十面体衣壳由等边三角形面、十二个顶点和三十条边构成，通过五聚体和六聚体实现不同大小的装配，广泛存在于动物及植物病毒。 Icosahedral capsids are formed from equilateral triangular faces, 12 vertices, and 30 edges; with pentons and hexons allowing assembly in various sizes; they are common in animal and plant viruses.

二十面体结构不仅赋予病毒高度机械稳定性，还简化了基因对结构蛋白的编码要求。 The icosahedral structure not only grants viruses high mechanical stability but also minimizes genetic coding requirements for structural proteins.

病毒包膜的结构与来源The Structure and Origin of the Viral Envelope

一、包膜的基本特性 Basic Features of the Viral Envelope

包膜在病毒中属于可有可无的结构。 The envelope is an optional structure for viruses.

包膜具有双重来源，既来自于宿主细胞也来自于病毒本身。 The envelope has a double origin, from both the host cell and the virus.

包膜主要由脂质、糖类和蛋白质组成。 The envelope is mainly composed of lipids, carbohydrates, and proteins.

包膜赋予病毒一定的脆弱性，使病毒对理化因素高度敏感，易受热失活。 The envelope makes viruses fragile and highly sensitive to physico-chemical factors, and heat-sensitive.

这一特性对流行病学和病毒诊断具有重要影响。 This has important implications for epidemiology and diagnosis.

老师补充说明
带包膜病毒在环境中生存能力较差，需要密切接触才能传播（如HIV、流感病毒），而无包膜病毒更耐环境，可经水、食物传播。 The teacher added: Enveloped viruses have poor environmental survival and require close contact to transmit (e.g. HIV, flu), while non-enveloped viruses are more resistant and can transmit via water or food.

二、包膜的来源 Origin of the Viral Envelope

病毒包膜来源于宿主细胞的多种细胞膜系统。 The viral envelope originates from various membrane systems of the host cell.

包膜可来自质膜，如流感病毒、狂犬病毒、HIV。 The envelope can stem from the plasma membrane, as in flu virus, rabies virus, and HIV.

包膜可来自细胞内膜结构，如高尔基体、内质网、胞内囊泡，如丙型肝炎病毒和冠状病毒。 The envelope may also originate from intracellular membranes, such as the Golgi apparatus, endoplasmic reticulum, and intracellular vesicles, as in hepatitis C virus and coronavirus.

有些病毒的包膜还可暂时包裹于核膜，如疱疹病毒。 For some viruses, the envelope transiently wraps from the nuclear membrane, as in herpesvirus.

老师强调，不同病毒通过不同膜系统获得包膜，这影响病毒装配、出芽及感染特性。 The teacher emphasized that the acquisition of the envelope from different membrane systems affects viral assembly, budding, and infection features.

三、病毒获得包膜的方式 Acquisition of the Viral Envelope

病毒通过出芽(budding)的方式从细胞膜或胞内膜系统获得包膜。 Viruses acquire their envelope by budding from cellular or internal membranes.

出芽过程中，衣壳逐渐包裹，由宿主膜折叠形成新的包膜结构。 During budding, the capsid becomes gradually surrounded as the host membrane folds to form the new envelope.

图像展示了包膜的获得过程，两种图示分别显示衣壳与质膜接触并“包裹”形成完整病毒颗粒。 The images show the acquiring process, with the capsid contacting and “wrapping” with the plasma membrane, forming a complete viral particle.

四、流感病毒的结构示例 Schematic Representation: Flu Virus

流感病毒带有包膜，该包膜为脂质双层。 The flu virus is enveloped, with a lipid bilayer membrane.

包膜内侧有M蛋白，包膜上有血凝素（hemagglutinin）和神经氨酸酶（neuraminidase）等糖蛋白。 The inner side of the membrane contains matrix (M) protein, and the envelope carries glycoproteins such as hemagglutinin and neuraminidase.

包裹内部为核衣壳，包含病毒RNA基因组。 The envelope encases the nucleocapsid containing the viral RNA genome.

五、艾滋病毒（HIV）结构示例 Schematic Representation: HIV

HIV的包膜外部有gp120与gp41等糖蛋白，参与病毒附着和入侵。 On the surface of the HIV envelope, glycoproteins gp120 and gp41 facilitate viral attachment and entry.

包膜包裹基质和截锥形对称衣壳，内部为病毒基因组。 The envelope wraps around the matrix and a truncated conical symmetrical capsid; inside is the viral genome.

六、老师讲课补充内容 Teacher's Additional Points

教师特别强调，包膜的存在导致病毒对外界环境敏感，因此包装、运输与诊断需注意保存温度，否则病毒很快失活。 The teacher highlighted that the envelope makes viruses sensitive to the environment, so correct storage temperature is essential for transport and diagnosis; otherwise, viruses lose infectivity quickly.

包膜病毒易受去污剂、乙醇等影响，常需通过血液、密切接触传播；无包膜病毒则更为稳定，可经消化道传播。 Enveloped viruses are easily inactivated by detergents and alcohol, thus usually transmitted via blood or close contact; non-enveloped viruses are more stable and can transmit via gastrointestinal routes.

病毒增殖的阶段 The stages of viral multiplication

总览 Overview

病毒增殖分为启动期（Initiation phase）、蚀变期（Eclipse phase）和晚期（Late phase）。 Viral multiplication is divided into the initiation phase, eclipse phase, and late phase.
  • A启动期 Initiation phase

包括病毒吸附（Attachment）和侵入（Entry）。 Includes viral attachment and entry.

病毒首先通过包膜或胶体蛋白与宿主细胞表面受体结合。 The virus first binds to host cell surface receptors via envelope or capsid proteins.

吸附后，病毒通过细胞膜融合或胞吞作用进入细胞。 After attachment, the virus enters the cell via membrane fusion or endocytosis.

对于无包膜病毒，多通过胞吞进入；有包膜病毒可通过膜融合直接释放基因组。 Non-enveloped viruses usually enter via endocytosis, while enveloped viruses may fuse membranes to release their genome directly.

B. 蚀变期 Eclipse phase

病毒去衣壳（Decapsidation/Uncoating），释放遗传物质到细胞内。 The virus undergoes uncoating, releasing its genetic material into the cell.

此后进行复制（Replication）、转录（Transcription）、翻译（Translation）。 Then, replication, transcription, and translation take place.

病毒基因组复制依赖于病毒及宿主细胞的酶。 Viral genome replication depends on both viral and host enzymes.

病毒mRNA由病毒基因组转录产生，并由细胞核糖体翻译成蛋白质。 Viral mRNA is transcribed from the genome and translated into proteins by host ribosomes.

C. 晚期 Late phase

主要包括病毒的组装（Assembly/Maturation）和释放（Release）。 This phase mainly includes viral assembly/maturation and release.

病毒结构蛋白和基因组在细胞内自组装成新的病毒颗粒。 Viral structural proteins and genomes self-assemble into new virus particles.

病毒可通过细胞溶解（lysis）或出芽（budding）方式释放。 Viruses are released via cell lysis or budding.

有包膜病毒多通过出芽从细胞膜获得包膜，无包膜病毒常通过细胞裂解释放。 Enveloped viruses usually gain their envelope by budding from the cell membrane, while non-enveloped viruses are often released by cell lysis.

3
阶段关键点 Key Points of Each Stage

启动期——病毒进入细胞，但未开始复制。 Initiation phase—virus enters the cell but has not yet started replication.

蚀变期——病毒基因组活化，合成mRNA和蛋白，但细胞内尚未检测到新病毒。 Eclipse phase—the genome is active, mRNA and proteins are synthesized, but no new virus particles can be detected yet.

晚期——新病毒颗粒组装、成熟、释放。 Late phase—new virus particles assemble, mature, and are released.

问1. 病毒增殖需完成的步骤 What does the virus have to do to multiply?
答

病毒必须复制自身的基因组，以便产生新的病毒颗粒。 The virus must replicate its genome to produce new viral particles.

翻译策略 Translation strategies

真核细胞只识别单顺反子mRNA，即一个mRNA只编码一条多肽链。 Eukaryotic cells only recognize monocistronic mRNA, i.e., one mRNA encodes one polypeptide.

多种病毒发展出不同策略解决此问题： Several strategies have been developed by viruses to address this:

单顺反子mRNA：一个mRNA生成一个蛋白。 Monocistronic mRNA: one mRNA makes one protein.

多顺反子mRNA：翻译得到一个大前体蛋白，然后由病毒或细胞蛋白酶切割成多个蛋白。 Polycistronic mRNA: translation produces a large precursor protein that is cleaved by viral or cellular proteases into multiple proteins.

剪接：通过RNA剪接机制产生多个不同的mRNA。 Splicing: generates several different mRNAs through splicing.

DNA病毒的复制与腺病毒和AAV的增殖周期Replication of DNA viruses & Multiplication cycle of Adenovirus and AAV

1
DNA病毒的复制过程Replication of DNA viruses

复制分为早期阶段、DNA复制阶段和晚期阶段。 Replication is divided into early phase, DNA replication phase, and late phase.

早期阶段 Early phase

病毒DNA利用宿主细胞的RNA聚合酶，转录生成早期mRNA。 Viral DNA uses host cell RNA polymerase to transcribe early mRNA.

早期mRNA在细胞质中被翻译成早期蛋白，这些多为酶类（非结构蛋白）。 Early mRNA is translated in the cytoplasm into early proteins, mainly enzymes (non-structural proteins).

这些酶类蛋白介导随后病毒DNA的复制。 These enzymatic proteins mediate subsequent viral DNA replication.

DNA复制阶段 DNA replication phase

早期蛋白（如病毒DNA聚合酶）促进病毒DNA的复制，生成多个病毒基因组副本。 Early proteins (such as viral DNA polymerase) promote replication of viral DNA, generating multiple copies of the viral genome.

晚期阶段 Late phase

病毒DNA再次利用宿主RNA聚合酶转录生成晚期mRNA。 Viral DNA again uses host RNA polymerase to transcribe late mRNA.

晚期mRNA在胞质中翻译成晚期蛋白，这些为结构蛋白。 Late mRNA is translated in the cytoplasm into late proteins, which are structural proteins.

结构蛋白+新复制的DNA基因组共同组装成新病毒颗粒，随后释放。 Structural proteins and newly replicated viral DNA assemble into new virions, which are then released.

2
腺病毒的增殖周期Multiplication cycle of Adenovirus

腺病毒的复制同样分为早期和晚期阶段。 The adenovirus replication cycle likewise has early and late phases.

早期阶段 Early phase

入胞后病毒基因组到达细胞核，先转录E1A、E1B和E2等早期基因。 After entry, the viral genome enters the nucleus, and early genes like E1A, E1B, and E2 are transcribed first.

这些早期蛋白调控细胞周期、促进病毒基因表达和基因组复制。 Early proteins regulate the cell cycle, promote viral gene expression, and viral genome replication.

随后转录E3和E4基因，这些主要参与病毒免疫逃逸。 Next, E3 and E4 genes are transcribed, mainly involved in immune evasion.

DNA复制 DNA replication

在早期蛋白帮助下，病毒DNA进行大量复制。 With the help of early proteins, the viral DNA undergoes massive replication.

晚期阶段 Late phase

复制后转录晚期基因，生成结构蛋白。 After replication, late genes are transcribed to produce structural proteins.

结构蛋白与新基因组组装成病毒颗粒。 Structural proteins assemble with new genomes to form virions.

新病毒颗粒经包装，最后释放出细胞。 New viral particles are packaged and finally released from the cell.

课上强调：病毒复制流程高度时序化，早期基因主要调控、晚期基因主要编码结构蛋白。 In the lecture, emphasis was placed on the strict timing of viral replication: early genes mainly regulate, late genes mainly encode structural proteins.

3
AAV的增殖模式Multiplication of AAV (Adeno-Associated Viruses)

AAV本身不具备独立增殖能力，需辅助病毒如腺病毒协助。 AAV cannot replicate independently and needs a helper virus such as adenovirus for assistance.

溶原周期 Lysogenic cycle

在无辅助病毒时，AAV基因组可整合到宿主染色体（多为19号染色体的AAVS1位点）。 Without a helper virus, the AAV genome can integrate into the host chromosome, often at the AAVS1 site on chromosome 19.

集成状态下病毒处于潜伏状态，不表达不增殖。 In the integrated state, the virus is latent, with no expression or multiplication.

溶菌周期 Lytic cycle

当有辅助病毒感染时，AAV可被激活，转入溶菌周期。 When a helper virus infects the cell, AAV can be activated and enter the lytic cycle.

病毒基因组复制并包装成新病毒颗粒，最后释放。 The viral genome replicates, is packaged into new virions, and finally released.

如果AAV已整合，还可以“救援”出来并复制。 If AAV is already integrated, it can be rescued from the host genome and replicate.

小结 Summary

DNA病毒复制有严格的时序分工，早期主控调节，晚期主控装配。 DNA virus replication has strict temporal division: early for regulation, late for assembly.

腺病毒和AAV是研究真核病毒增殖机制的重要模型。 Adenovirus and AAV are important models for studying eukaryotic viral multiplication mechanisms.

AAV有独特的溶原与溶菌双周期，需辅助病毒激活复制。 AAV uniquely alternates between lysogenic and lytic cycles, requiring a helper virus for productive replication.

病毒转录（RdRp复合体和NP介导，负链RNA转录为mRNA） Viral transcription (RdRp complex and NP direct the transcription of vRNA to mRNA in the nucleus)
6
基因组复制（负链RNA经抗基因组正链中间体复制更多负链RNA） Genome replication (vRNA is replicated via a positive-strand antigenome intermediate)
7
mRNA转运到胞质翻译，合成病毒蛋白（包括包膜和结构蛋白） mRNA is transported to cytoplasm for translation, generating viral proteins including the envelope and structural proteins
8
病毒核衣壳组装，并由M1、NS2等蛋白导出至胞质 Ribonucleoprotein complexes are assembled and exported into the cytoplasm via M1, NS2 proteins
9
病毒组装、出芽并离开宿主细胞 Assembly, packaging, and budding to exit the host cell
10
部分病毒（如有包膜）在细胞膜出芽获得包膜 Enveloped virions acquire their envelope by budding at the plasma membrane
11
细胞因感染可发生凋亡，病毒NS1蛋白可拮抗干扰素反应 Infected cells may undergo apoptosis; viral NS1 protein antagonizes the interferon response.

关键总结Key summary

负链RNA病毒需自身携带转录酶完成mRNA转录。 Negative-strand RNA viruses must bring their own RNA polymerase for mRNA synthesis.

新合成的+链既为蛋白翻译所用，也参与基因组复制。 The newly synthesized (+)RNA serves for both protein translation and genome replication.

流感病毒具有分段基因组，每段一蛋白，复制与转录均在细胞核内进行且高度复杂。 The flu virus has a segmented genome, each segment codes for one protein, and its replication and transcription take place in the nucleus with high complexity.

病毒增殖后期阶段Late phase of viral multiplication

1
后期阶段定义及主要内容 Definition and key events of the late phase

后期阶段对应病毒的装配、成熟以及病毒颗粒的释放。 The late phase refers to the assembly, maturation, and release of virus particles.

此阶段是病毒生命周期的最后关键步骤。 This stage represents the final critical steps in the virus life cycle.

2
发生的细胞部位 Location of late phase events

DNA病毒的晚期步骤多数发生在细胞核（痘病毒除外）。 For DNA viruses, late-phase events mostly occur in the nucleus (except for poxvirus).

RNA病毒的晚期步骤一般在细胞质完成。 For RNA viruses, these steps usually take place in the cytoplasm.

3
产量和装配效率 Yield and assembly efficiency

病毒后期的产量仍然较低，并非所有核酸和蛋白都能组装成完整病毒。 Viral yield in the late phase is still low, as not all nucleic acids and proteins successfully assemble into complete virions.

以疱疹病毒为例，装配率有限。 Herpesvirus is an example where assembly efficiency is restricted.

4
病毒颗粒的装配 Assembly of viral particles

新合成的蛋白和核酸自发组装成病毒前体结构，称为前衣壳（procapsid）。 Newly synthesized proteins and nucleic acids spontaneously assemble into a precursor structure called the procapsid.

病毒蛋白在装配后需进行翻译后修饰，如裂解、二硫键形成和糖基化等。 After assembly, viral proteins undergo post-translational modifications such as cleavage, disulfide bridge formation, and glycosylation.

包膜病毒的包膜糖蛋白会发生寡聚化，利于形成病毒外壳。 Envelope glycoproteins of enveloped viruses oligomerize to facilitate viral envelope formation.

5
无包膜病毒的组装实例 Example of naked virus assembly

以脊髓灰质炎病毒为例，其装配为多步级联过程，并涉及前体蛋白剪切。 Poliovirus assembly involves a complex cascade with precursor protein cleavage.

具体步骤为：P1多肽切割→单体原体（protomer，VP0, VP1, VP3）→五聚体单位（capsomere）→六十聚体前衣壳（procapsid）→核酸进入形成前病毒体（provirion）→最终成熟为病毒体（virion，VP4, VP2, VP3, VP1）。 The process: P1 polypeptide cleavage → protomer (VP0, VP1, VP3) → capsomere (five protomers) → procapsid (sixty capsomeres) → RNA enters to form provirion → final mature virion (VP4, VP2, VP3, VP1).

6
病毒颗粒的释放 Release of viral particles

无包膜病毒通过细胞溶解（裂解）释放。 Naked (non-enveloped) viruses are released by cell lysis.

包膜病毒通过出芽（如质膜）或胞吐（内膜）方式释放，病毒成熟阶段是在离开细胞过程中完成，获得感染力。 Enveloped viruses are released by budding (at the plasma membrane) or exocytosis (via internal membranes); a maturation stage during or after release is necessary to acquire infectious power.

以HIV和狂犬病毒为例，包膜病毒在细胞膜出芽，最终成熟为具有感染性的病毒颗粒。 For HIV and rabies virus, viral particles bud from the cell membrane and mature to become infectious virions.

总结 Summary

后期阶段包括病毒的组装、成熟及释放，是病毒生命周期的最后步骤。 The late phase includes assembly, maturation, and release, as the final steps in the viral life cycle.

DNA病毒后期多在核内，RNA病毒多在胞质。 DNA viruses usually complete these steps in the nucleus, RNA viruses in the cytoplasm.

病毒装配自发完成，但效率有限；释放方式依有无包膜而异。 Viral assembly is spontaneous but not fully efficient; release methods differ for naked and enveloped viruses.

包膜病毒需经历出芽和成熟步骤才能具有感染性。 Enveloped viruses must undergo budding and maturation to become infectious.
