Q1 – Mechanisms of contraction and relaxation

Question

问What mechanisms make the vascular smooth muscle cell contract and relax?
答

🔹考试作答推荐（英文箭头版）

1
Contraction of vascular smooth muscle

Contraction of vascular smooth muscle occurs through the following Ca²⁺-dependent pathway:

Intracellular Ca²⁺ ↑ (via L-type voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels and/or SR release)

→ Ca²⁺–calmodulin complex

→ activation of MLCK (myosin light-chain kinase)

→ phosphorylation of myosin light chain (MLC, Ser19)

→ actin–myosin cross-bridge cycling ↑

→ vascular smooth muscle contraction (vasoconstriction)

1
Relaxation of vascular smooth muscle

Relaxation occurs when:

Intracellular Ca²⁺ ↓→Ca²⁺–calmodulin↓ →MLCK activity ↓ + MLCP (myosin light-chain phosphatase) dephosphorylates MLC

→ actin–myosin cross-bridge cycling ↓

→ vascular smooth muscle relaxation (vasodilation)

🔹背诵用中文逻辑 + 关键词

收缩：细胞内 Ca²⁺↑（L 型电压依赖 Ca 通道 / SR 释放）→ Ca²⁺–钙调蛋白复合物（Ca²⁺–calmodulin）→ 激活 MLCK（myosin light-chain kinase）→ 磷酸化肌球蛋白轻链 MLC（Ser19）→ 肌动-肌球蛋白交联增加（cross-bridge cycling↑）→ 血管平滑肌收缩（vasoconstriction）

舒张：细胞内 Ca²⁺↓ → Ca²⁺–calmodulin 减少 → MLCK 活性下降＋ MLCP（myosin light-chain phosphatase）去磷酸化 MLC→ 交联减少（cross-bridge cycling↓）→ 平滑肌舒张 → 血管扩张（vasodilation）

一句话核心：Ca²⁺–MLCK 促收缩，MLCP 促舒张，两者平衡决定 vascular tone。

Q2 – Arterial diameter, blood flow and blood pressure

Question

问Why is arterial diameter important for determining blood flow and blood pressure?
答

🔹考试作答推荐（英文版）

Arterial diameter is a major determinant of vascular resistance.

According to Poiseuille’s law, resistance is inversely proportional to the fourth power of the radius, so small changes in arterial diameter produce very large changes in resistance.

Vasoconstriction (decrease in diameter) increases peripheral resistance, reduces blood flow and tends to raise arterial blood pressure.

Conversely, vasodilation (increase in diameter) decreases resistance, facilitates blood flow and tends to lower arterial blood pressure.

你也可以简化成箭头写法：

Arterial diameter ↓ (vasoconstriction)

→ resistance ↑ (1 / r⁴)

→ blood flow ↓

→ arterial blood pressure ↑

Arterial diameter ↑ (vasodilation)

→ resistance ↓

→ blood flow ↑

→ arterial blood pressure ↓

🔹背诵用中文逻辑 + 关键词

阻力 ~ 1 / r⁴（Poiseuille 定律，radius⁴ 非常敏感）

动脉直径减小（vasoconstriction）→ 外周阻力↑（peripheral resistance）→ 器官血流量↓（blood flow）→ 动脉血压↑（arterial blood pressure）

动脉直径增大（vasodilation）→ 阻力↓ → 血流↑ → 血压↓

一句话核心：动脉直径通过 1/r⁴ 关系改变外周阻力，从而决定血流和血压。

Q3 – Ex vivo measurement of arterial tone (myography) 动脉张力的离体测量（肌电图）

Question

问Which method can be used to monitor arterial tone ex vivo? What is the principle?
答

🔹考试作答推荐（英文版）

Arterial tone ex vivo can be assessed by myography.

Small arteries are isolated from the animal, cut into short rings and mounted on wires or pins in a myograph chamber containing physiological solution.

The rings are stretched to a defined resting tension, and changes in isometric force are recorded when vasoconstrictor or vasodilator agents are applied.

This allows precise measurement of vascular smooth muscle contraction and relaxation without interference from circulating hormones or the sympathetic nervous system.

也可以用箭头结构写得更像流程：

Take small artery → cut into arterial rings

→ mounted on wires/pins in a myograph chamber (physiological solution)

→ set to a defined resting tension

→ add vasoconstrictor / vasodilator

→ record changes in isometric force

→ evaluation of arterial tone (contraction / relaxation)

🔹背诵用中文逻辑 + 关键词

方法：myography（肌张测定）

操作思路：取小动脉 → 切成短“血管环”（arterial rings）→ 用钢丝/针挂在 myograph 装置里（wires / pins in myograph chamber）→ 浸在生理溶液中，设定固定的初始张力（resting tension）

原理：加入缩血管 / 扩血管药物（vasoconstrictor / vasodilator）→ 记录等长收缩张力的变化（isometric force）→ 定量评估血管平滑肌收缩/舒张 → 反映 arterial tone

优点：体外系统，条件可控，没有循环激素和交感神经干扰。

Excitation–contraction coupling in an adult ventricular cardiomyocyte成人心室心肌细胞的兴奋-收缩耦合

→ 动作电位沿肌膜和 T 管传播

答

A3

Mitochondria are the main ATP producers (oxidative phosphorylation).

Q4

问How is energy transferred within cardiomyocytes and why are specialized energy-transfer systems required? 心肌细胞内的能量是如何传递的？为什么需要专门的能量传递系统？
答

A4

1️⃣ Creatine kinase shuttle (CKS)

Mitochondrial ATP

→ mitochondrial CK: ATP + creatine → ADP + phosphocreatine (PCr)

→ PCr diffuses through cytosol

→ cytosolic CK near myofibrils: PCr + ADP → ATP + creatine

→ local ATP regeneration at ATPases.

2️⃣ Direct adenine nucleotide channeling (DANC)

Ordered ultrastructure → mitochondria packed near myofibrillar ATPases

→ microdomains / channels allow direct ATP/ADP exchange.

Reason: simple ATP diffusion would be too slow for the very high, localized energy demand of the beating heart.

中文提纲

两套系统：CK 接力棒 + DANC 直通车。

确保高需能位点（收缩装置、离子泵）能快速、高效拿到 ATP。

Q5

Describe the main Ca²⁺-dependent pathways involved in excitation–contraction coupling in cardiomyocytes and how they are altered in heart failure.

A5

1️⃣ Calcineurin–NFAT pathway

Ca²⁺ ↑ → Ca²⁺–calmodulin → activates calcineurin (phosphatase).

Ca²⁺ 升高 → 形成 Ca²⁺–钙调蛋白复合物 → 激活去磷酸酶 calcineurin。

Calcineurin dephosphorylates NFAT → NFAT enters nucleus → hypertrophic gene transcription.

Calcineurin去磷酸化 NFAT → NFAT 入核 → 启动心肌肥厚相关基因转录。

2️⃣ CaMKII–HDAC–MEF2 pathway

Gq-coupled GPCR agonists (e.g. endothelin) → PLC → IP₃ → nuclear Ca²⁺ ↑.

Gq 偶联受体激动剂（如内皮素）→ 激活 PLC → 生成 IP₃ → 核周 Ca²⁺ 升高。

↑ nuclear Ca²⁺ → activates CaMKII → phosphorylates class II HDAC bound to MEF2.

核内 Ca²⁺ 升高 → 激活 CaMKII → 磷酸化与 MEF2 结合的 II 类 HDAC。

HDAC is exported from nucleus → free MEF2 activates hypertrophic genes.

HDAC 被排出细胞核 → 游离 MEF2 激活肥厚基因表达。

3️⃣ Changes in heart failure 心衰时的改变

In heart failure
CaMKII and calcineurin activities are increased.
心力衰竭时
CaMKII 和 calcineurin 活性升高。

SERCA ↓, NCX ↑ → impaired SR Ca²⁺ reuptake and abnormal Ca²⁺ transients.

SERCA 表达/功能下降，NCX 增加 → SR 回收 Ca²⁺ 受损，Ca²⁺ 瞬变异常。

Result
systolic and diastolic dysfunction, higher risk of arrhythmias.

结果：收缩和舒张功能障碍，心律失常风险升高。

中文提纲

两条 Ca²⁺-依赖肥厚通路
Calcineurin–NFAT、CaMKII–HDAC–MEF2。
心衰
CaMKII/Calcineurin 过度激活，SERCA↓、NCX↑ → Ca²⁺ 处理失衡 → 收缩舒张受损 + 心律失常。

Q8

Describe the clinical and morphological characteristics, risk factors, proposed causes and suggested therapies of anthracycline-induced cardiomyopathy.

A8

Characteristics

Dilated LV, systolic dysfunction, ↓ LVEF, fibrosis, HF symptoms, arrhythmias; usually no hypertrophy.

Risk factors

High cumulative dose / previous exposure

old age or very young

pre-existing heart disease, diabetes, obesity

other cardiotoxic drugs or chest irradiation

genetic susceptibility.

Causes/mechanisms

Anthracyclines inhibit topoisomerase IIβ in cardiomyocytes

redox cycling → ROS overproduction

→ mitochondrial and DNA damage, energetic failure

→ cardiomyocyte death → cardiomyopathy.

Suggested therapies

Early ACE inhibitors + β-blockers

± dose limitation, liposomal forms, dexrazoxane.

典型为扩张型心肌病样改变
左室扩大、LVEF↓、纤维化、心衰/心律失常。
危险因素
累积剂量大、基础心病/代谢病、合并放疗/其他心毒药、年龄、遗传。
机制
Topo IIβ 抑制 + ROS 过多 → 线粒体/DNA 损伤 → 肌细胞死亡。
治疗
早期 ACEI + β 阻滞剂，控制剂量等。

VASCULAR TONE

问Why is arterial diameter important for determining blood flow & blood pressure?
答

Because resistance is extremely sensitive to radius, according to Poiseuille’s law:

A small decrease in radius → HUGE increase in resistance

Resistance controls arterial pressure and organ perfusion

Thus vascular tone, by adjusting VSMC contraction and radius, is a major determinant of blood pressure.

问What mechanisms make the vascular smooth muscle cell contract?
答

Ca2+ enters the cell

Ca2+ binds to RyR → Releases more Ca2+

Ca2+ binds to Calmodulin (CaM)

Ca2+/CaM complex activates Myosin Light Chain Kinase (MLCK)

MLCK phosphorylates Regulatory Light Chain 20 (RLC20) on myosin heads → Allow for the myosin heads to bind to actin filaments

→ Start actin-myosin cross-bridge cycling

→ Muscle contraction (vasoconstriction)

问What mechanisms make the vascular smooth muscle cell relax?
答

Ca2+ leaves the cell / comes back to the SR

No more Ca2+/CaM complex → Deactivation of Myosin Light Chain Kinase (MLCK)

Myosin Light Chain Phosphatase (MLCP) is activated → Dephosphorylates RLC20

Myosin heads detach from actin filaments

→ Stop actin-myosin cross-bridge cycling

→ Muscle relaxation (vasodilation)

问What method can be used to monitor arterial tone ex vivo? What is the principle?
答

Isolate artery → mount between wires or pins

Stretched to resting tension in physiological buffer

Drugs are added: vasoconstrictors, vasodilators, …

The mechanical signals are recorded

→ Record tension vs. time
